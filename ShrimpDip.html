Shrimp Dip

Ingredients

  • 2 8-oz packages cream cheese, softened
  • 1 lb shrimp
  • 1 bunch green onions, chopped
  • 1 tbs mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Creole seasoning
  • Pinch cayenne, paprika, garlic powder

Directions

  1. Boil shrimp in shrimp boil. Drain, chop them finely, and save the liquid used to boil them.
  2. Soften cream cheese and add saved shrimp boil. Add chopped green onions, mayonnaise and shrimp. Mix until smooth.
  3. Add all seasonings and chill for at least one hour before serving.
