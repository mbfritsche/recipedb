Béchamel Sauce

Ingredients

  • 2 tbs butter
  • 1½ tbs flour
  • ¾ C milk
  • 4 drops Tabasco
  • ½ tsp salt
  • dash nutmeg

Directions

  1. Melt Butter: In a heavy saucepan over low heat, melt the butter without browning.
  2. Add Flour: Gradually stir in flour, whisking constantly to keep smooth. Do not let the flour cook.
  3. Add Milk: Slowly whisk in milk, stirring constantly and reaching the bottom and sides of the pan to prevent lumps.
  4. Thicken: Cook gently over low heat until the sauce thickens.
  5. Finish: Remove from heat and stir in Tabasco, salt, and nutmeg until fully blended.
