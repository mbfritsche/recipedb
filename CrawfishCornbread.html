Spinach

Ingredients

  • 1 box Jiffy Corn Muffin mix
  • 1 lb crawfish tails, chopped
  • 1 small yellow onion, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3-4 green onions, chopped
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 can Rotel tomatoes, drained
  • 8 oz pepper jack cheese, grated
  • Cajun seasoning, onion powder and garlic powder, to taste
  • 1 stick butter

Directions

  2. Preheat: Heat oven to 350°F and place a cast iron skillet with 1 stick of butter inside while the oven preheats.
  3. Mix: Combine all remaining ingredients and mix until well blended.
  4. Bake: Pour the mixture into the hot, buttered skillet and bake until set and golden, about 40–50 minutes.

Chef's Notes

  • A black iron skillet is the traditional vessel for this recipe, but 9x13" baking pan can be used successfully if a cast iron skillet is not available.
