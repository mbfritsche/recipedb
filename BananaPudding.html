Banana Pudding

Ingredients

  • ¾ C sugar
  • ¼ C all-purpose flour
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 3 C milk
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1½ tsp vanilla extract
  • 8 oz vanilla wafers (about 60 cookies), divided
  • 4 large ripe bananas, cut into ¼" slices

Directions

  1. Make Pudding: In a saucepan, combine sugar, flour, and salt. Whisk in milk and cook over medium heat until thickened and bubbly. Reduce heat and cook briefly, then remove from heat.
  2. Temper Eggs: Whisk eggs in a small bowl. Gradually whisk in a small amount of the hot pudding mixture, then return everything to the saucepan. Cook gently until thickened. Remove from heat, stir in vanilla, and let cool slightly.
  3. Assemble: In an 8" square dish, layer vanilla wafers, banana slices, and pudding. Repeat layers.
  4. Chill: Press plastic wrap directly onto the surface and refrigerate until fully chilled.
  5. Serve: Remove plastic wrap, crush remaining wafers over the top, and serve.
