Vegan Pâté

Ingredients

  • 1 C roasted sunflower seeds
  • 2 C boiling water
  • 2 tsp vegetable oil
  • 85 g shallots (~5 shallots), minced
  • 8 g garlic, minced
  • 300 g cremini mushrooms, washed and finely sliced
  • 10 g tomato paste
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • 15 ml soy sauce
  • ¼ tsp white pepper
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • 2 tbs vegan butter

Directions

  1. Soak Seeds: Soak sunflower seeds in boiling water 15 minutes, then drain well.
  2. Cook Aromatics: Heat vegetable oil in a pan and cook minced shallots until translucent. Add garlic and cook until fragrant.
  3. Cook Mushrooms: Add mushrooms and cook until browned. Stir in tomato paste and paprika; cook ~1 minute, then remove from heat.
  4. Blend: In a food processor or high-speed blender, combine drained sunflower seeds, mushroom-onion mixture, white pepper, soy sauce, salt, and vegan butter. Blend until smooth, scraping down sides as needed.
  5. Refine (Optional): For a smoother texture, pass the mixture through a fine metal sieve.
  6. Serve & Store: Serve on bánh mì, baguette, or crackers. Store refrigerated 3-4 days.
