Sweet Potato Latkes (v)

Ingredients

  • 2 large sweet potatoes, peeled & grated
  • 2 large eggs
  • 4 green onions, white and light green parts only, chopped
  • 45 g all-purpose flour (or gluten-free flour)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 C vegetable oil, for frying
  • Sour cream and green onions for service

Directions

  1. Wrap the sweet potato in a muslin cloth or some kitchen paper and squeeze out as much moisture as possible.
  2. Add the sweet potato, eggs, green onion, flour, and salt to a large mixing bowl and stir together to combine.
  3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan on a medium-high setting.
  4. Scoop out ¼ C of the mixture, compacting it into the cup.
  5. Tip the cup upside down into the hot oil to make a mound, then use a spatula to flatten it into a patty as it fries.
  6. Cook for about 2 minutes or until browned on the bottom, then flip and cook for another 2 minutes on the other side.
  7. Remove the latkes from the oil with a slotted spatula and cool on a wire rack. Dab excess oil with a paper towel.
  8. Serve with a dollop of sour cream and sliced green onion tops

Chef's Notes

  • This is a vegetarian dish - replace eggs with a plant-based alternative to make vegan
