Sweet Potato Latkes (v)

Ingredients

  • 2 large sweet potatoes, peeled & grated
  • 2 large eggs
  • 4 green onions, white and light green parts only, chopped
  • 45 g all-purpose flour (or gluten-free flour)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 C vegetable oil, for frying
  • Sour cream and green onions for service

Directions

  1. Prep: Peel sweet potato and squeeze out as much moisture as possible using a cloth or paper towel.
  2. Mix: In a large bowl, combine sweet potato, eggs, green onion, flour, and salt. Mix until evenly combined.
  3. Fry: Heat oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Scoop about ¼ C of the mixture, shape into a mound, and flatten into a patty in the pan. Cook until browned, about 2 minutes per side.
  4. Drain: Remove with a slotted spatula and place on a wire rack. Blot excess oil if needed.
  5. Serve: Serve with sour cream and sliced green onion tops.

Chef's Notes

  • This is a vegetarian dish - replace eggs with a plant-based alternative to make vegan
