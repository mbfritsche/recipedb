Fresh Fruit Smoothie

Ingredients

  • 350 ml soy milk
  • 120 ml orange juice
  • 40 ml vanilla yogurt
  • 60 g baby spinach
  • 120 g strawberries
  • 85 g pineapple
  • 60 g banana

Directions

  1. Combine: Mix soy milk, juice, and yogurt until well blended.
  2. Layer: Add fruits first, then vegetables so vegetables sit closest to the blade.
  3. Blend: Add remaining liquid, secure lid, and blend on high until smooth and fully combined.
  4. Adjust Texture: If too thick, add 2 tbs water and blend again to desired consistency.

Chef's Notes

  • Use about 510 ml liquid to 325 g fruit and vegetables. More liquid makes a thinner smoothie; more fruit creates a gelato-like texture.
  • Any frozen berries work well. Blackberries and raspberries have more seeds; blueberry skins take slightly longer to blend.
  • Frozen fruits work best, but fresh fruits are fine.
  • Frozen mango is an excellent substitute for frozen pineapple; both are commonly available in Thailand.
  • Kale can replace spinach, though spinach may be easier to find in Thailand.
  • My grandson called this Foofah and the name stuck
