Cream of Broccoli Soup (v)

Ingredients

  • 700-800G broccoli, cut into florets
  • 2 potatoes , peeled and diced into 1½cm cubes
  • 4 C vegetable stock
  • 1 onion , diced
  • 2 garlic cloves , minced
  • 1 tbs butter (or olive oil)
  • 1½ C water
  • ¾ tsp kosher salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Directions

  1. Sauté: Melt butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion and garlic and cook until softened and fragrant, about 2 minutes.
  2. Simmer: Add broth, broccoli, potato, salt, and pepper. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook until the broccoli is tender, about 20 minutes.
  3. Blend: Remove from heat and purée until smooth with an immersion blender.
  4. Season: Taste and adjust salt as needed.
  5. Serve: Ladle into bowls and garnish with crushed pistachios and olive oil, if desired.

Chef's Notes

  • Despite the name, this version contains no cream. Substitute the butter with a plant‑based alternative to make it vegan.
  • Parmesan or cheddar make excellent toppings, but the soup is intended to be light and healthy on its own.
