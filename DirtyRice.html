Dirty Rice

Ingredients

  • ½ lb chicken gizzards
  • ½ lb chicken liver
  • ½ lb ground pork
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 stalks celery, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 C uncooked parboiled rice
  • 1 tbs parsley
  • 2 green onions, chopped
  • 3 C chicken stock
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp cayenne
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp dry mustard
  • 3 tbs butter

Directions

  1. Cook Giblets: Boil giblets in 4 cups water with celery leaves and 1 whole green onion for ~20 minutes, until tender. Separate and finely chop the liver and gizzards.
  2. Brown Meats: Heat 1 tbs oil in a black iron pot. Sauté pork and gizzards ~6 minutes, until browned.
  3. Add Aromatics: Stir in seasonings, onion, celery, bell pepper, garlic, and butter. Mix well and simmer 10 minutes.
  4. Add Stock & Rice: Add stock and simmer 5 minutes, then stir in rice, chopped liver, green onions, and parsley. Simmer 5 minutes more to incorporate.
  5. Steam Rice: Cover, reduce heat to lowest setting, and cook 10-20 minutes, until rice is fluffy. Do not stir once heat is reduced to avoid pasty rice.
  6. Serve: Fluff gently and serve hot.
