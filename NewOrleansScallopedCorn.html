New Orleans Scalloped Corn

Ingredients

  • 4 tsp butter
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 1 large sweet red pepper, finely chopped
  • 4 C frozen corn
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 C fat-free milk
  • 1 tbs sugar
  • 1 to 2 tsp hot pepper sauce
  • ½ tsp dried thyme
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp pepper
  • 1¼ C crushed reduced-fat butter-flavored crackers (about 30 crackers)
  • 5 green onions, sliced

Directions

  1. Preheat: Heat oven to 350°F.
  2. Sauté: In a large skillet, melt butter over medium‑high heat. Add onion and pepper and cook until tender.
  3. Cook Corn: Add corn and heat through, stirring occasionally. Remove from heat.
  4. Mix: In a small bowl, whisk eggs, milk, sugar, pepper sauce, thyme, salt, and pepper. Add to corn mixture and stir in crushed crackers and green onions.
  5. Assemble: Transfer mixture to a greased 2-qt baking dish.
  6. Bake: Bake uncovered until set and a knife inserted in the center comes out clean, about 35-40 minutes.
