Sausage Balls

Ingredients

  • 1 lb bulk hot sausage
  • 3 C Bisquick
  • 1 lb sharp cheddar cheese, grated

Directions

  1. Combine ingredients and knead until well mixed.
  2. Shape into 1" balls and place on a parchment-lined cookie sheet.
  3. Bake at 375° for 25 minutes. You can substitute other cheeses for cheddar, like marble jack or pepper jack, both of which have a very nice flavor.

Chef's Notes

  • Total prep time is 30 minutes. Incorporating ingredients takes approximately the time needed to pre-heat the oven. Using a stand mixer will significantly decreases prep time
  • Use 2 oz cookie scoop to form the balls
  • ½ C milk
  • ½ C grated parmesan cheese (4 oz)
