Cajun Cabbage

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 medium green pepper, chopped
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 can (10 ounces) Rotel tomatoes
  • 1 can (8 ounces) tomato sauce
  • ½ C uncooked long grain rice
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp dried basil
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • ¼ - ½ tsp each white, black and cayenne pepper
  • 4 to 6 drops hot pepper sauce
  • 1 small head cabbage, chopped
  • 1 cup shredded Colby cheese
  • Fresh basil, optional

Directions

  1. Cook Filling: In a skillet over medium heat, cook beef with green pepper, onion, and garlic until meat is no longer pink; drain.
  2. Season: Stir in tomatoes, tomato sauce, rice, seasonings, and hot sauce.
  3. Assemble: Spread mixture into an ungreased 13x9" baking dish. Top with cabbage, then cheese.
  4. Bake: Cover and bake at 350°F for 65-75 minutes, until rice is tender.
  5. Finish: Garnish with basil and serve.
