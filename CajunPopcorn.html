Cajun Popcorn

Ingredients

  • 1 lb crawfish tails
  • 2 eggs
  • 1¼ C milk (or Half-and-half)
  • 1 tbs creole mustard
  • 1 tsp Louisiana hot sauce
  • 1 C corn flour
  • ½ C flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • oil for deep frying

Directions

  1. Make Batter: Whisk eggs, milk, Creole mustard, and hot sauce. Add dry ingredients and stir until the batter has the consistency of heavy cream. Rest 1 hour.
  2. Coat Crawfish: Add crawfish tails to the batter and toss until fully coated.
  3. Fry: Heat oil to 350°F. Fry battered crawfish 2-3 minutes, until golden brown.
  4. Drain & Serve: Drain on a wire rack and serve with your choice of dressings.

Chef's Notes

  • Great in salads or po-boy sandwiches.
