Gnocchi Malfacti (v)

Ingredients

  • 1 C ricotta cheese
  • 1 C spinach, blanched and chopped
  • 1 egg yolk
  • ½ C flour
  • pinch salt
  • pinch nutmeg

Directions

  1. Mix ricotta, spinach, and egg yolk until combined. Add just enough flour to bind the mixture.
  2. Flour your hands, and roll about 1 tbs of the dough into a small ball. If the mixture sticks to your hands, add a small portion of additional flour.
  3. Add the gnocchi to a pot of salted water at a rolling boil, and boil them until they float. Remove and drain.
  4. Serve topped with melted butter and fresh grated Parmesan.

Chef's Notes

  • This is a vegetarian dish and vegan if ricotta cheese and eggs are substituted with a plant-based alternative
