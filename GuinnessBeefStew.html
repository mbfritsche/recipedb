Guinness Beef Stew

Ingredients

  • 3-4 lbs chuck eye steak or pork shoulder, cubed
  • 2 tbs bacon grease
  • 2 large onions, chopped
  • 1 stalk celery, chopped
  • 1-2 Jalapenos, minced
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 2 tbs tomato paste
  • ¼ C flour
  • 2 tsp Colman’s dry mustard
  • 3 C chicken or vegetable broth
  • 16 oz Guinness or Shiner
  • 1 tsp Marmite
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1½ tbs brown sugar
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1 lb carrots, peeled and chopped
  • 1½ lbs Yukon gold potatoes, peeled and chopped

Directions

  1. Prep Oven & Meat: Preheat oven to 325°F. Season cubed chuck steak with salt and pepper.
  2. Brown Meat: Melt bacon drippings over medium-high heat. Brown meat in batches to avoid crowding, adding more drippings if needed. Remove browned meat to a bowl.
  3. Cook Vegetables: Add onions, celery, garlic, and tomato paste to the pot. Cook until vegetables soften and begin to brown, adding more bacon drippings if the pot becomes dry.
  4. Build Stew: Stir in flour, then add chicken broth, thyme, brown sugar, Marmite, salt, pepper, and ½ C Guinness. Return meat to the pot, stir well, and cook uncovered in the oven 1½ hrs.
  5. Add Vegetables: After 1½ hrs, add carrots and potatoes. Return to the oven and cook 45 minutes more.
  6. Finish: During the final 15 minutes, add remaining Guinness. Total oven time is ~3 hours.
  7. Season & Serve: Finish with 1 tsp Cajun seasoning and an optional splash of apple cider vinegar. Serve hot.

Chef's Notes

  • Pork shoulder is a good substitute.
  • Peas, green beans, and mushrooms all work well as additions.
    • Green beans: Add with the potatoes.
    • Mushrooms: Sauté first, then add with the potatoes.
    • Peas: Add at the very end to preserve color and texture.
