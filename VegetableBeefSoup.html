Vegetable Beef Soup

Ingredients

  • 3 lb crosscut beef shanks
  • 4 tbs salt
  • 5 peppercorns
  • 2 bay leaves
  • ½ tsp oregano
  • ¼ tsp marjoram
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 1 stalk celery, chopped
  • ¼ lb fresh green beans, chopped
  • 2 potatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 4 ears corn (or 1 10-oz container of frozen corn)
  • 2 carrots, peeled and chopped
  • 1 yellow squash, cubed
  • 1 zuchini, cubed

Directions

  1. Simmer Base: Add beef, seasonings, onion, celery, and tomatoes to 8 cups water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer 2 hours.
  2. Finish: Add remaining ingredients and simmer 20–30 minutes, until tender.
  3. Thicken (Optional): For a thicker soup, continue simmering ~20 minutes more to reduce.
