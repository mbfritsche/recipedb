Rib Roast with Wine Sauce

Ingredients

Rib Roast
  • 7½ lb bone-in rib eye
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Garlic Salt
  • Pepper
  • Butter
  • Olive oil
Wine sauce
  • 1 large shallot
  • ½ lb button mushrooms
  • 6 tbs butter
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp garlic salt
  • ¼ tsp sugar
  • ½ C red wine

Directions

  1. Season: Coat beef evenly with salt, garlic salt, pepper, butter, olive oil, and a small amount of sugar, applying extra seasoning to the fatty side.
  2. Rest: Wrap and refrigerate the beef for several hours.
  3. Bring to Room Temp: Remove from refrigerator and let sit until it reaches room temperature.
  4. Roast: Cook at 325°F until desired doneness is reached, adjusting cook time based on size and preference.
  5. Serve: Rest briefly, then slice and serve.
Wine Sauce
  1. Sauté: Melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add shallots and cook until translucent.
  2. Cook Mushrooms: Add mushrooms and sauté until lightly softened, about 2-3 minutes.
  3. Finish: Add seasonings and wine. Cook until reduced by about one‑quarter.

Chef's Notes: Tips for Precision

  • Use a meat thermometer in the thickest part of the roast, away from bone or fat, for the most reliable reading.
  • Let roast rest, tented with foil, for 20–30 minutes. Expect the internal temperature to rise slightly during this time - carryover cooking typically raises the internal temperature of a rib roast by 5-10°F during the resting phase
  • Slice after resting and serve
