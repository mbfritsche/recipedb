Yorkshire Pudding

Ingredients

Eight-Cup
  • 166 g all-purpose flour
  • 150 g water
  • 150 g milk
  • 4 large eggs
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Beef dripping or bacon grease
Twelve-Cup
  • 250 g all-purpose flour
  • 225 g water
  • 225 g milk
  • 6 eggs
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • Beef dripping or bacon grease

Directions

  1. Heat Fat: Add 8 g beef dripping to each of 12 pudding cups and place in the oven as it preheats to 410°F. If using bacon grease, use just enough to coat about half the bottom of each cup.
  2. Make Batter: Whisk eggs and salt. Add flour and whisk into a thick paste. Gradually whisk in a little liquid until smooth, then add remaining liquid without worrying about lumps. Whisk vigorously 1-2 minutes to incorporate air.
  3. Chill Batter: Transfer batter to a jug or measuring cup and refrigerate until needed.
  4. Fill & Bake: Once the oven is fully preheated and fat is smoking hot, remove the pan and fill cups no more than halfway with batter.
  5. Bake: Return to oven and bake 25-30 minutes without opening the door, until well risen and deeply golden.

Chef's Notes

  • Do not use convection with Yorkshire pudding. The forced air prevents them from rising completely
  • You can add some Colman's dry mustard to the batter to add a little spice.
