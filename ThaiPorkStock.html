Thai Pork Stock

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork bones (neck bones or back bones)
  • 3½ L water
  • Top half of one lemongrass, crushed and chopped into 2" pieces
  • 4-5 cloves garlic, crushed until broken
  • 2 cilantro roots, crushed, or 5 cilantro stems
  • 4" section of a daikon or half an onion, peeled and diced
  • ½ tsp white peppercorns, crushed

Directions

  1. Make Stock: Rinse bones in cold water and place in a large stock pot. Cover with water, bring to a simmer, then reduce heat and cook gently for about 1 hour.
  2. Add Aromatics: Skim off any foam from the surface. Add aromatics and continue simmering for another hour.
  3. Adjust Volume: Add more water if desired, keeping in mind that additional water will dilute the stock.
  4. Strain: Strain the stock into a clean container.
  5. Save Meat: Pick any remaining meat from the bones and reserve for use in other dishes.
  6. Use or Store: Use immediately or cool and refrigerate for later use

Chef's Notes

  • This is a very versatile stock that can be used as the foundation for many Thai dishes.
  • The stock will last up to a week in the refrigerator. You may also freeze the stock in portions for later use.
