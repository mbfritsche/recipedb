Balsamic Chicken Thighs

Ingredients

  • 6 chicken thighs
  • ¼ C balsamic vinegar
  • 2 tbs olive oil
  • 3-5 cloves garlic
  • ¼ C chopped basil

Directions

  1. arinate: Wash chicken thighs, pat dry, and place in a deep container. Combine remaining ingredients, pour over chicken, cover, and refrigerate at least 2 hours.
  2. Bake: Remove chicken from marinade and place in a shallow baking dish. Bake at 375°F to an internal temperature of 165°F, about 1-1½ hours
  3. Finish: As the balsamic roasts, its natural sugars will caramelize and darken the skin. Serve hot.
