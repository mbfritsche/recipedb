Peanut Butter Chocolate Fudge

Ingredients

  • 1 C milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 3 C sugar
  • ¼ C peanut butter
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ C butter
  • 2 tbs light corn syrup
  • 3 1-oz. grated chocolate squares
  • 85 g coarsely chopped peanuts (unsalted)

Directions

  1. Cook Base: Bring milk to a boil, then remove from heat.
  2. Dissolve: Stir in sugar, salt, chocolate, and corn syrup until fully dissolved.
  3. Steam Sugar: Cover and cook over medium heat 2-3 minutes, until steam washes sugar crystals from the sides of the pan.
  4. Cook Candy: Uncover and continue cooking without stirring until the mixture reaches 234-240°F (soft‑ball stage).
  5. Finish: Remove from heat and add vanilla, peanut butter, and butter. Beat until the mixture thickens and loses its shine.
  6. Add Nuts: Stir in peanuts, then spoon mixture into a buttered 8" square pan.
  7. Set: Let stand until firm, then cut and serve.
