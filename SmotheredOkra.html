Smothered Okra

Ingredients

  • 2 pkg frozen okra
  • 1 can Rotel tomatoes
  • 2 small cans tomato sauce
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 large shallot, chopped
  • 2 stalks celery, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 jalapeño pepper, finely diced
  • ½ lb bacon, diced
  • 1½ lb chicken thighs, boned and sliced
  • 1 lb smoked sausage
  • ½ lb shrimp
  • 1 tbs vegetable oil
  • 2 tbs vinegar
  • Salt, pepper and hot sauce, to taste

Directions

  1. Cook Bacon: Heat a heavy pot over medium heat. Add bacon with a little water and cook until browned and fat renders. Remove and set aside.
  2. Brown Chicken: Add chicken thighs, season with salt, pepper, and hot sauce, and brown well. Remove and set aside.
  3. Brown Sausage: Add sausage and cook until browned. Remove and set aside.
  4. Sauté Vegetables: Add vegetables to the pot and scrape up browned bits. Cook briefly, then add okra and vinegar. Reduce heat slightly and cook until vegetables soften.
  5. Simmer: Add tomatoes and sauce, then return meats to the pot. Cover and cook for about 10-15 minutes.
  6. Add Shrimp: Stir in shrimp, cover, and cook until just done, about 2-3 minutes.
  7. Serve: Serve hot over rice with French bread.
