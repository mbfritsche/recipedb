Southern Fried Corn

Ingredients

  • 2 tbs butter
  • 2 C fresh corn kernels
  • 1 tbs finely minced white onion
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper
  • Cajun seasoning, to taste

Directions

  1. Sauté: Melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
  2. Cook: Add remaining ingredients and cook, stirring every 2-3 minutes, until the corn begins to caramelize and brown.
