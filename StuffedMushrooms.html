Gran Padano Stuffed Mushrooms

Ingredients

  • 1 lb crabmeat
  • ½ C gran padano parmesian
  • ½ lb chopped shrimp
  • Stems from 12 mushrooms
  • 30 g Italian bread crumbs
  • 1 stick butter
  • 2 tbs parsley
  • 1 onion
  • ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 stalks celery
  • Salt, red pepper, black pepper, white pepper, garlic powder, Louisiana hot sauce, and Tabasco to taste
Mushroom basting sauce
  • 60 g butter
  • 80 ml white wine

Directions

  1. Sauté onions, celery, parsley, and mushroom stems until vegetables are wilted.
  2. Add shrimp and crabmeat and sautee another 3-5 minutes.
  3. Remove from heat and add Parmesan and bread crumbs.
  4. Stuff mushroom caps with mixture and top with basting mixture and extra Parmesan.
  5. Bake in buttered pan @350° oven for 15 minutes.
