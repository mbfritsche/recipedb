Gran Padano Stuffed Mushrooms

Ingredients

  • 1 lb crabmeat
  • ½ C gran padano parmesian
  • ½ lb chopped shrimp
  • Caps and stems from 12 mushrooms
  • 30 g Italian bread crumbs
  • 1 stick butter
  • 2 tbs parsley
  • 1 onion
  • ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 stalks celery
  • Salt, red pepper, black pepper, white pepper, garlic powder, Louisiana hot sauce, and Tabasco to taste
Mushroom basting sauce
  • 60 g butter
  • 80 ml white wine

Directions

  1. Sauté Base: Cook onion, celery, parsley, and mushroom stems until softened.
  2. Add Seafood: Add shrimp and crabmeat and cook until just heated through, about 3-5 minutes.
  3. Mix: Remove from heat and stir in Parmesan and bread crumbs.
  4. Stuff: Fill mushroom caps with the mixture and top with basting mixture and additional Parmesan.
  5. Bake: Place in a buttered pan and bake at 350°F until heated through and lightly browned, about 15 minutes.
