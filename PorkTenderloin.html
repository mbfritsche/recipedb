Pork Tenderloin

Ingredients

  • 2-3 lbs pork tenderloin
  • ½ C soy sauce
  • ½ C sesame oil
  • 3 tbs vinegar
  • ¾ tsp dry mustard
  • 2 tsp garlic

Directions

  1. Trim Pork: Remove excess fat from the tenderloins.
  2. Marinate: Combine dry ingredients in a deep dish. Add tenderloins, cover, and refrigerate at least 1 hour, preferably overnight.
  3. Prepare Grill: Set up a charcoal grill for indirect heat.
  4. Grill: Remove pork from marinade, reserving liquid for basting. Grill over indirect heat, turning occasionally and basting frequently, ~45 minutes.
  5. Rest: Remove from grill and wrap tightly in foil. Rest 30 minutes.
  6. Serve: Slice thinly on the bias and serve with vegetables and rice.
