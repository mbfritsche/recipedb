Busy Day Stew

Ingredients

  • 2 lb beef, cubed (e.g., round steak)
  • 2 potatoes, cubed
  • 2 carrots, cubed
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 stalks celery, diced
  • 2 C V8 juice or Bloody Mary mix
  • 2 tbs quick tapioca
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbs sugar
  • 1 tbs Worcestershire

Directions

  1. Assemble: Combine all ingredients in a heavy casserole dish.
  2. Bake: Cover tightly with foil and bake at 250°F for 5 hours.
