Breakfast Sausage Casserole

Ingredients

  • 1 can biscuits or 15-20 tater tots (~170 g)
  • 1 lb hot sausage
  • 6 eggs
  • ¾ C milk
  • 2 C cheddar cheese (1 lb total, cheddar and pepper jack mix)

Directions

  1. Prep Pan: Generously grease an 8x8‑inch baking dish.
  2. Add Base: Cut biscuits into 6-8 pieces and place in the pan. (If using tater tots, add them instead.)
  3. Cook Sausage: Brown sausage in a skillet, drain thoroughly, and spread evenly over the biscuits or tots.
  4. Add Custard: Beat eggs with milk, season with salt and pepper, and pour evenly over the casserole.
  5. Top & Bake: Sprinkle cheese on top and bake at 400°F for 35-40 minutes, checking the center for doneness.
  6. Rest & Serve: Let rest 5-10 minutes before serving. Great with salsa.

Chef's Notes

  • Tater tots work well in place of canned biscuits, and cutting the cheese in half still yields great results.
  • Adding about 8 oz shredded Gruyère enhances flavor and creates a richer crust.
  • Season the eggs with a splash of fish sauce and Louisiana hot sauce. For extra zing, consider adding Coleman's dry mustard.
