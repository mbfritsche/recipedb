Shrimp Verdé

Ingredients

  • 1 lb cooked, cleaned shrimp, chopped in ¼" dice
  • ¼ C lime juice
  • ¼ lb tomatillos, husks removed and diced
  • 1 bunch green onions, sliced
  • 4 jalapeños, seeded and chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, peeled
  • 1 bunch cilantro leaves, chopped
  • 1 small avocado, peeled and pit removed
  • 1 tsp sea salt
  • ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper

Directions

  1. Marinate the shrimp in the lime juice for one hour. Drain and reserve the juice.
  2. In a large bowl combine the shrimp, tomatillos, green onions, jalapeños, garlic, cilantro, avocados, salt and pepper and toss to coat. Serve immediately. Yields 4 to 6 servings
