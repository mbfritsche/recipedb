Shrimp Verdé

Ingredients

  • 1 lb cooked, cleaned shrimp, chopped in ¼" dice
  • ¼ C lime juice
  • ¼ lb tomatillos, husks removed and diced
  • 1 bunch green onions, sliced
  • 4 jalapeños, seeded and chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, peeled
  • 1 bunch cilantro leaves, chopped
  • 1 small avocado, peeled and pit removed
  • 1 tsp sea salt
  • ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper

Directions

  1. Marinate Shrimp: Marinate shrimp in lime juice 1 hour. Drain, reserving the lime juice.
  2. Combine: In a large bowl, toss shrimp with tomatillos, green onions, jalapeños, garlic, cilantro, avocados, and reserved lime juice. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  3. Serve: Serve immediately.
  4. Yield: 4-6 servings
