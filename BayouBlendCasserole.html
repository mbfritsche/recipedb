Bayou Blend Casserole

Ingredients

  • 1 lb cooked medium shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 2 cans (6 ounces each) crabmeat, drained, flaked and cartilage removed
  • 1 jar (4½ oz) sliced mushrooms, drained
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 celery ribs, chopped
  • 1 large green pepper, chopped
  • 4 tbs butter, divided
  • 1 package (8 ounces) cream cheese, cubed
  • 1 can (10¾ ounces) condensed cream of mushroom soup, undiluted
  • ¾ C cooked rice
  • 1 tsp garlic salt
  • ¾ tsp hot pepper sauce
  • ½ tsp cayenne pepper
  • ¾ C shredded cheddar cheese
  • ½ C crushed butter-flavored crackers (about 12 crackers)

Directions

  1. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Melt Cheese Base: In a small saucepan over low heat, melt cream cheese with 2 tbs butter until smooth.
  3. Sauté Veggies: In a large cast-iron or ovenproof skillet, sauté onion, celery, and green pepper in remaining butter until tender.
  4. Combine: Stir in shrimp, crab, soup, rice, mushrooms, garlic salt, pepper sauce, cayenne, and the cream cheese mixture.
  5. Top: Mix cheddar cheese with cracker crumbs and sprinkle evenly over the top.
  6. Bake: Bake uncovered 25-30 minutes, until bubbly and lightly browned.

Chef's Notes

  • This is a classic Cajun recipe from the 70s - the Green Bean Casserole of South Louisiana, a regular fixture on every monthly menu
