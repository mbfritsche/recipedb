Shrimp Etouffee

Ingredients

  • 3 lb raw deveined shrimp
  • 2 C chopped onions
  • 1 C chopped celery
  • ½ C bell pepper
  • 2 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 1 large can tomatoes
  • 2 small cans tomato paste
  • 1 C flour
  • 1 C corn oil
  • 3 tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp red pepper
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbs chopped parsley
  • 2 tbs chopped green onion tops

Directions

  1. Make Roux: Cook flour and oil together until a smooth roux forms.
  2. Add Trinity: Stir in onion, celery, bell pepper, and garlic; cook until vegetables are softened.
  3. Build Base: Add tomatoes and tomato paste, mix well, and cook ~5 minutes.
  4. Simmer: Add 6 cups water and simmer ~1 hour.
  5. Add Shrimp: Stir in 3 lb raw, deveined shrimp and cook 15 minutes, until just done.
  6. Finish: Add parsley and green onion tops ~5 minutes before serving.
  7. Serve: Serve hot over rice with French bread.
