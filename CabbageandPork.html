Stir-Fried Cabbage with Crispy Pork

Ingredients

  • 500 g cabbage, chopped and separated
  • 300 g pork belly, sliced, with top skin removed
  • ½ tsp white peppercorns
  • 2 Thai chili
  • 2 tbs garlic
  • 1 tsp shrimp paste (gapi)
  • ½ tbs soy sauce
  • ½ tbs fish sauce
  • 1½ tbs oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp dark soy sauce

Directions

  1. Prep: Chop cabbage and separate leaves. Slice pork belly into 1" pieces after removing outer skin.
  2. Make Paste: Grind peppercorns, chili, garlic, and gapi into a paste.
  3. Render Pork: Cook pork belly with a small amount of water over medium-high heat until fat renders, then remove to a collander to drain
  4. Sauté: Add gapi paste and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  5. Cook Cabbage: Add cabbage and a little water, then stir-fry until tender.
  6. Finish: Return the pork and add the sauces and sugar. Stir-fry until reduced to desired consistency.
  7. Serve: Serve with jasmine rice.

Chef's Notes

  • Use white pepper sparingly, as too much can overpower the subtle salty and sweet notes of sauce.
  • This recipe works with other proteins such as chicken thigh or tofu
