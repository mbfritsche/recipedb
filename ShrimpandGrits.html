Shrimp & Grits

Ingredients

Grits
  • 475 ml vegetable broth
  • 475 ml milk
  • 80 g butter, cubed
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 130 g uncooked old-fashioned grits
  • 230 g shredded pepper jack (~8 oz)
Shrimp
  • 8 thick-sliced bacon strips, chopped
  • 1 lb uncooked medium shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 4 green onions, chopped

Directions

  1. Make Grits: In a large saucepan, bring broth, milk, butter, salt, and pepper to a boil. Slowly stir in grits, reduce heat, cover, and cook 12-14 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened.
  2. Add Cheese: Stir in cheese until melted. Keep warm.
  3. Cook Bacon: In a large skillet over medium heat, cook bacon until crisp. Remove to a wire rack with a slotted spoon, reserving 4 tsp drippings.
  4. Sauté Shrimp: Add shrimp, garlic, and seasoning to the reserved drippings. Sauté until shrimp turn pink.
  5. Serve: Spoon shrimp over grits and garnish with green onions.
