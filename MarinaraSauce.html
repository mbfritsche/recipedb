Marinara Sauce

Ingredients

  • 2 cans San Marzano canned tomatoes (28 oz)
  • 2 tbs tomato paste
  • 1 onion, julienned
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 6 basil leaves
  • 1 C red wine
  • dash olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Sauté Onions: Heat olive oil over medium‑low. Add onions, season with salt and pepper, and sweat ~10 minutes until soft and translucent.
  2. Add Garlic & Herbs: Stir in garlic and cook 3 minutes more. Add basil leaves and red chili flakes (if using) and wilt ~2 minutes.
  3. Cook Tomato Paste: Add tomato paste and cook 3–5 minutes to caramelize and bring out sweetness.
  4. Deglaze: Increase heat to medium-high, add wine, and reduce by half, 3–5 minutes.
  5. Simmer Sauce: Reduce heat to medium-low, add canned tomatoes, and cook ~90 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Finish: Remove from heat and purée with an immersion blender to desired texture. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
