Korean Spicy Pork (Jaeyuk Bokkeum)

Ingredients

  • 450 g pork shoulder, cut into big bite size pieces
  • 1 medium onion, sliced
  • 3 stalks scallion, chopped (white part for sauce, green part for garnish)
  • 1 teaspoon cooking oil
Sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ginger, minced
  • 1 tbs gochujang
  • 1 tbs soy sauce
  • 1 tbs fish sauce
  • 2 tbs rice wine
  • 1 tbs brown sugar
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 tbs sesame oil
  • 1 tbs gochugaru (Korean chili flakes)
  • 1 tbs white part green onion

Directions

  1. In a bowl, combine all sauce ingredients and mix well.
  2. Heat the pan over medium to medium-high and add 1 tsp of oil
  3. Add the meat and cook until golden brown. Remove the meat to a colander to drain.
  4. Add sliced onion to the pan and fry until translucent.
  5. Lower the heat, add the sauce and mix well, and cook for 3 minutes.
  6. Taste and adjust seasonings, garnish with reserved green onions, and serve with steamed rice
