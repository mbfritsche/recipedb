Chicken and Carrots

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken thighs
  • 1½ pounds carrots, peeled and trimmed
  • 1 large onion, peeled and cut into eighths
  • 1 head of garlic
  • 4 tbs olive oil
  • 1 tbs chopped fresh rosemary
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Prep Vegetables: Arrange carrots and onion in a single layer on a greased baking sheet. Slice the top off a head of garlic and place it on the tray.
  3. Season: Drizzle vegetables with 2 tbsp olive oil, season with rosemary, salt, and pepper.
  4. Add Chicken: Place chicken thighs on top. Rub each thigh with 1 tsp olive oil and season with salt and pepper.
  5. Roast: Roast 15–20 minutes, until chicken skin is golden and carrots are tender.
  6. Serve: Divide chicken and vegetables among four plates and serve hot.
