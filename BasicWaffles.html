Basic Waffles

Ingredients

  • 2 C flour
  • 4 tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ C sugar
  • 2 C milk
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ C butter, melted
  • 2 tbs oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Directions

  1. Mix Dry: Sift dry ingredients together.
  2. Add Wet: Whisk eggs into milk and stir into dry ingredients.
  3. Finish Batter: Add butter, oil, and vanilla; fold gently with a silicone spatula. Do not overmix, some lumps are fine.
  4. Cook Waffles: Preheat a Belgian waffle iron. Pour ½ cup batter onto the center and spread outward in a circular motion.

Chef's Notes

  • For a lighter waffle, separate the eggs: mix yolks into the batter, whip whites to stiff peaks, and gently fold them in.
  • You can swap some milk for buttermilk for extra tang, and add cinnamon if desired.
  • For savory waffles, omit the vanilla and reduce the sugar by 50%.
