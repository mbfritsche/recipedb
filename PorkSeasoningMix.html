Pork Seasoning Mix

Ingredients

  • ¼ C salt
  • 2 tbs red pepper
  • 1 tbs black pepper
  • 1 tbs white pepper
  • 2 tbs onion powder
  • 1½ tbs garlic powder
  • 1 tsp dry mustard
  • ½ tsp ground bay leaves
  • ½ tsp cilantro
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp chervil
  • ½ tsp celery seed
  • ¼ tsp sage
  • ¼ tsp ginger
  • pinch thyme

Directions

  1. Mix all ingredients together and store in a glass jar. Use as needed in pork recipes as a replacement for salt and pepper.
