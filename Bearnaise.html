Béarnaise Sauce (Classic Method)

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb clarified butter
  • 2 oz shallots, minced
  • 4 oz tarragon vinegar
  • 1 tbs dried tarragon
  • ½ tsp crushed peppercorns
  • 1½ tbs fresh tarragon, chopped
  • 6 egg yolks
  • Salt, cayenne, and lemon juice to taste

Directions

  1. Reduce Vinegar: In a non-aluminum pan, combine vinegar, shallots, dried tarragon, and peppercorns. Reduce by ¾. Cool slightly.
  2. Start Sauce: Transfer reduction to a stainless-steel bowl. Add egg yolks and mix well.
  3. Thicken Yolks: Set bowl over a barely simmering water bath and whisk until thick and creamy. Remove from heat if it gets too hot.
  4. Emulsify: Slowly whisk in clarified butter, starting drop by drop. Thin with lemon juice or warm water if needed.
  5. Finish: Strain through cheesecloth. Season with salt, cayenne, lemon juice, and remaining fresh tarragon.
  6. Hold & Serve: Hold warm no longer than 1½ hours.

Chef's Notes

  • To make Sauce Paloise, substitute fresh mint for the fresh tarragon
  • This is a good sauce for lamb
