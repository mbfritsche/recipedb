Shrimp and Corn Soup #2

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs medium shrimp, peeled
  • ½ C celery, chopped
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 C crushed tomatoes
  • 2 bell peppers, chopped
  • 1 can Rotel tomatoes
  • 2 lbs frozen white kernel corn
  • 3 qt water
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp parsley
  • ½ lb butter
  • ½ tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1 C flour
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Saut%eacute Vegetables: In a three‑gallon pot, sauté all vegetables in butter until softened.
  2. Make Roux: Stir in flour and add all tomatoes. Cook for 1-2 minutes to lightly brown the flour.
  3. Boil: Add water and bring to a boil. Stir in corn and seasonings, then return to a boil.
  4. Finish with Shrimp: Add shrimp and bring back to a boil while stirring constantly.
  5. Rest: Turn off the heat and let the mixture sit for 30 minutes before serving or freezing.
  6. Serve: Garnish with chopped parsley and serve over rice.
