Sriracha Sauce

Ingredients

  • ½ lb fresh red jalapeno peppers
  • ½ lb fresh mini sweet snacking peppers
  • 7 garlic cloves, smashed and peeled
  • 80 ml apple cider vinegar (or white or unsweetened rice vinegar)
  • 3 tbs tomato paste
  • 4 tbs honey
  • 2 tbs fish sauce
  • 2 tbs Bragg's Liquid Aminos or Coconut Aminos

Directions

  1. Blend: Add all ingredients to a food processor or high‑speed blender and blend until smooth.
  2. Cook: Transfer to a saucepan and cook over high heat until it just comes to a boil.
  3. Simmer: Reduce heat and simmer 10-15 minutes, tasting and adjusting with honey, vinegar, or Bragg's as needed for balance.
  4. Cool: Turn off heat and let the sauce cool completely.
  5. Store: Transfer to jars and refrigerate. Keeps up to 2 weeks.

Chef's Notes

  • Use any peppers you have on hand; for a red sauce, choose red varieties.
  • A combination of red jalapeño or red serrano with mini sweet peppers works especially well.
  • Mini sweet peppers are smaller and sweeter than bell peppers, helping balance heat without dulling color.
