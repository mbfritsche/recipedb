Chicken Ranch Mac & Cheese

Ingredients

Macaroni and sauce
  • 3 C uncooked elbow macaroni
  • 3 tbs butter
  • 2 tbs all-purpose flour
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp pepper
  • 1 C milk
  • 1½ C shredded cheddar cheese
  • ½ C grated Parmesan cheese
  • ½ C shredded Swiss cheese
  • ¾ C ranch salad dressing
  • 1 C coarsely chopped cooked chicken
Topping
  • ½ C seasoned bread crumbs
  • 2 tbs butter, melted
  • 10 bacon strips, cooked and crumbled
  • 1 tbs minced fresh parsley

Directions

  1. Prep Oven & Pasta: Preheat oven to 350°F. Cook macaroni al dente, drain, and return to the pot.
  2. Make Cheese Sauce: Melt butter over medium heat. Whisk in flour, salt, and pepper, then gradually whisk in milk. Bring to a boil, stirring, and cook 1-2 minutes until thickened.
  3. Finish Sauce: Stir in cheeses until melted, then mix in dressing.
  4. Assemble: Toss chicken and cheese sauce with macaroni. Transfer to a greased 13x9" baking dish.
  5. Bake: Bake 40 minutes, until brown and bubbly.
