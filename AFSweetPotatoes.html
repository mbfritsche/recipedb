Air Fryer Sweet Potatoes

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs sweet potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 1 tbs avocado oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 2 red bell peppers, diced
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp dried parsley
  • 1½ tsp pink sea salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Directions

  1. Prep: Rinse cubed sweet potatoes, pat dry, and place in a large mixing bowl.
  2. Season: Add onion, red bell pepper, and garlic. Drizzle with avocado oil, then add paprika, parsley, salt, and black pepper. Toss well to coat evenly.
  3. Air Fry: Arrange sweet potatoes in the air fryer basket in a single layer, working in batches if needed to avoid overcrowding.
  4. Cook: Air fry at 400°F for 20 minutes. Toss, then continue cooking until browned and tender, about 3-5 minutes more. Add a few extra minutes for a crispier texture if desired.
  5. Serve: Garnish with fresh parsley if desired and serve warm with ketchup or hot sauce.

Chef's Notes

  • Dice sweet potatoes into small, even cubes for the best texture and more even cooking.
  • Soak diced sweet potatoes in water for 20–30 minutes, then rinse and pat dry. This helps remove excess starch and promotes crispness.
  • Avoid overcrowding the air fryer basket. Arrange potatoes in a single layer with space between pieces for proper air circulation.
  • Regular potatoes, butternut squash, or pumpkin can be used in place of sweet potatoes.
  • Refined coconut oil or olive oil can be used instead of avocado oil.
