Bajed Creole Eggplant

Ingredients

  • 2 eggplant, diced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 C raw peeled shrimp
  • 1 stalk celery, chopped
  • ½ C butter or oil
  • Cajun seasoning to taste

Directions

  1. Cook Eggplant: Boil eggplants until soft; drain.
  2. Sauté Filling: Sauté onion, chopped shrimp, and celery in butter or oil until tender. Combine with cooked eggplant.
  3. Assemble: Transfer mixture to a buttered baking dish and sprinkle with breadcrumbs.
  4. Bake: Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes, until heated through and lightly browned.
