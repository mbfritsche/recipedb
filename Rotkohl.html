Rotkohl (Red Cabbage)

Ingredients

  • 1 medium red cabbage, shredded
  • 2–3 tart apples (e.g., Granny Smith), diced or grated
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2–3 tbs butter or bacon grease
  • ¼ C apple cider or red wine vinegar
  • 2 tbs brown or white sugar (more to taste)
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 3–4 whole cloves
  • ½ C water
  • 1–2 tbs lingonberry or cranberry jam (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tbs cornstarch + 2 tbs cold water (optional, for thickening)

Directions

  1. Sauté: Cook onions in fat until softened. Add apples and cook briefly.
  2. Braise: Stir in cabbage, vinegar, water, sugar, bay leaf, and cloves. Cover and cook gently until tender.
  3. Reduce: After about 45 minutes, stir in jam if using. Uncover and continue cooking until excess liquid has reduced.
  4. Finish: Adjust seasoning and thicken if desired.

Chef's Notes

  • Slice the cabbage as thinly as possible for even cooking and better flavor absorption. A mandoline works well if used carefully.
  • Braise low and slow to develop deeper flavor and mellow the acidity.
  • Taste during cooking and adjust sweetness or tartness with additional sugar or vinegar as needed.
  • For a different flavor profile, substitute balsamic vinegar for apple cider vinegar or add cranberry jam for extra tang and depth.
  • This dish can be prepared a day in advance. The flavors continue to develop and improve as it rests.
