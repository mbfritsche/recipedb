Schnitzel

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs pork tenderloin, sliced into 4 oz portions
  • 1 C breadcrumbs
  • ½ C flour
  • 2 eggs
  • ¼ C half-and-half
  • ½ C olive oil
  • 3 tbs butter

Directions

  1. Flatten Pork: Pound tenderloins flat using a mallet or the back of a frying pan.
  2. Season: Season with salt, pepper, and paprika; let rest at room temperature.
  3. Prep Dredge: Whisk eggs and half-and-half with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and hot sauce. Season flour with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  4. Heat Fat: Heat olive oil and butter in a large saucepan until hot and butter stops sizzling.
  5. Bread Cutlets: Coat cutlets in flour, dip into egg mixture, then coat with breadcrumbs.
  6. Sauté: Carefully add cutlets to the hot oil and cook 2-3 minutes per side until golden.
  7. Finish: Remove to a wire rack and season lightly with salt and pepper. Serve hot.
