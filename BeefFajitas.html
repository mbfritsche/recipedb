Beef Fajitas

Ingredients

  • 1¼ lbs flank steak
  • 240 ml pineapple juice
  • 120 ml soy sauce
  • 60 ml fresh lime juice (2 large)
  • ¼ C cilantro
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded and chopped
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 80 ml water
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • Flour Tortillas

Directions

  1. Make Marinade: Mix pineapple juice, soy sauce, lime juice, cilantro, jalapeños, onions, water, pepper, salt, and cumin.
  2. Marinate Steak: Place steak in a gallon zip-top bag, add marinade, seal, and refrigerate 4-6 hours.
  3. Grill: Grill steak 12-15 minutes, turning occasionally.
  4. Rest & Serve: Remove from grill and rest 10 minutes before slicing and serving.

Chef's Notes

  • Popular toppings for beef fajitas include guacamole, sour cream, salsa, pico de gallo, queso, spanish rice, lettuce, jalapeños
  • This is a Pappasito's copycat recipe
