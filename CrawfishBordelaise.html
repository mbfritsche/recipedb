Crawfish Bordelaise Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 tbs minced shallots
  • 1 tbs minced garlic
  • 1 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • ½ C dry red wine
  • ¼ lb crawfish tails
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1½ cups Veal or Beef Glaze
  • 1 tbs chopped green onions
  • 2 tbs unsalted butter (at room temp)

Directions

  1. Start Base: In a small nonreactive saucepan over high heat, combine shallots, garlic, and Cajun seasoning. Cook ~30 seconds, watching carefully to avoid burning.
  2. Deglaze: Add wine and bring to a boil.
  3. Add Crawfish: Stir in crawfish, salt, and pepper; return to a boil.
  4. Glaze: Add the glaze and bring back to a boil.
  5. Simmer: Reduce heat and simmer, skimming off fat and impurities as they rise.
  6. Clarify: Increase heat to high, skim remaining impurities, and cook 1-2 minutes.
  7. Finish: Add butter and whisk ~30 seconds, until fully incorporated.
  8. Final Touch: Stir in green onions and remove from heat.
