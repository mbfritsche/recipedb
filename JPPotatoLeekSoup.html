Easy Potato Leek Soup

Ingredients

  • 1 medium leek, coarsely chopped
  • 6-8 button mushrooms, coarsely chopped
  • 4 C chicken stock (vegetable stock will substitute)
  • Cooking oil
  • 1 C Instant mashed potato flakes
  • 1-1½ C milk or Half-and-half
  • Grated Swiss cheese

Directions

  1. Coarsely chop leeks and mushrooms and sweat them in the cooking oil.
  2. Add 4 C of chicken stock and bring to a boil. Add instant mashed potato flakes and return to a boil. Once the soup boils again, it is ready.
  3. Finish with 1-1½ C of milk and serve topped with grated Swiss cheese

Chef's Notes

  • This is a Jacques Pepin classic
