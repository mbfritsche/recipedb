BBQ Shrimp #2

Ingredients

  • 8-10 lbs shrimp
  • 1 lb butter
  • 1 lb margarine
  • 6 oz Worcestershire sauce
  • 8 tbs black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon rosemary
  • 4 lemons (sliced)
  • 1 teaspoon Tabasco
  • 4 teaspoons salt
  • 2-4 cloves garlic

Directions

  1. Marinate Shrimp: Rinse shrimp and spread in a shallow pan. Combine sauce ingredients in a saucepan over low heat, then pour over shrimp.
  2. Chill: Refrigerate for several hours, turning and basting shrimp every 30 minutes.
  3. Bake: Bake at 300°F for 30 minutes, turning shrimp every 10 minutes.
  4. Serve: Serve hot in soup bowls with French bread for dipping in the sauce.
