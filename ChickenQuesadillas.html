Grilled Chicken with Corn Relish and Avocado Salsa

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • ½ C Grilled Chicken Marinade
  • 3 tbs olive oil
  • Three 6" flour tortillas
  • ¼ C grated Monterey jack cheese
  • ¼ C grated white cheddar cheese
  • Salt and pepper
  • ¼ C corn relish
  • ¼ C avocado salsa

Directions

  1. Grill Chicken: Marinate chicken at least 1 hour. Grill until internal temperature reaches 165°F, about 5 minutes per side. Cut into large pieces and set aside.
  2. Assemble: Place 2 tortillas on an ungreased baking sheet. Spread ½ the cheese and chicken over each tortilla; season with salt and pepper.
  3. Stack & Grill: Stack the tortillas, top with the remaining tortilla, and brush with olive oil. Grill 3 minutes per side, until tortillas are crisp and cheese is melted.
  4. Serve: Cut into quarters and serve hot, garnished with corn relish and avocado salsa.
