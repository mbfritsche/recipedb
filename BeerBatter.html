Beer Batter

Ingredients

  • 1 C all-purpose flour
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper
  • 1½ C beer (12 oz)

Directions

  1. In a small mixing bowl add flour, egg, garlic powder, and black pepper.
  2. Stir in 1 C (8 oz) of beer ax mix until smooth. Add the remaining beer until the batter is the desired texture
