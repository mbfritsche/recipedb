Beer Batter

Ingredients

  • 1 C all-purpose flour
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper
  • 1½ C beer (12 oz)

Directions

  1. Mix Base: In a small bowl, combine flour, egg, garlic powder, and black pepper.
  2. Add Beer: Stir in 1 cup (8 oz) of beer and mix until smooth.
  3. Adjust Texture: Add remaining beer gradually until the batter reaches your desired consistency.
