Stir-Fried Bean Sprouts (v)

Ingredients

  • 1 lb bean sprouts
  • 2 green onions
  • 3 slices ginger
  • ¼ Knorr chicken bouillon cube
  • 2 tsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbs oyster sauce
  • 3 tbs peanut oil

Directions

  1. Prep Vegetables: Wash bean sprouts thoroughly three times. Slice green onions into ½" pieces and ginger into ¼" slices.
  2. Heat Wok: Heat wok until very hot and smoking. Add peanut oil and heat until smoking.
  3. Stir‑Fry: Add ginger and fry about 30 seconds, taking care not to burn. Add bean sprouts and green onions and stir-fry until sprouts turn translucent, about 5 minutes, stirring constantly.
  4. Season: Halfway through cooking, add the bouillon cube and soy sauce; continue stirring.
  5. Finish: Add oyster sauce, stir to combine, transfer to a serving dish, and serve immediately.

Chef's Notes

  • This dish can be made vegan by substituting ½ tsp Better Than Bouillon vegetable base for the chicken bouillon cube and using vegetarian mushroom oyster sauce in place of oyster sauce.
