Carrot Gnocchi (v)

Ingredients

  • 2 cups peeled and chopped carrots
  • 2 egg yolks
  • Pinch of salt
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese, plus more for garnishing
  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 8 tablespoons (1 stick) unsalted butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • ¼ cup sage leaves
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper

Directions

  1. Cook Carrots: Boil carrots until very tender (12-15 minutes); drain.
  2. Make Dough: Mash carrots until smooth. Stir in egg yolks, salt, and Parmesan, then add flour and mix just until combined.
  3. Shape Gnocchi: Roll dough into ¾" logs, cut into 1" pieces, and place on a floured baking sheet.
  4. Cook Gnocchi: Boil gnocchi in salted water until they float (3-4 minutes); drain.
  5. Brown Butter: Melt butter over medium heat and cook 8-10 minutes until lightly browned and nutty.
  6. Finish Sauce: Add garlic and sage; cook 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
  7. Combine & Serve: Toss gnocchi in the butter sauce, season with salt and pepper, garnish with Parmesan, and serve immediately.

Chef's Notes

  • Vegetarian as written, vegan with plant-based alternatives for eggs and Parmesan cheese
