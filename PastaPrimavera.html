Pasta Primavera (v)

Ingredients

  • 1 lb asparagus
  • 2 C broccoli flowerets
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 1 tbs olive oil
  • 1 large carrot, diagonally cut
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 yellow pepper
  • 1 C whipping cream
  • ½ C vegetable broth
  • 3 green onions, chopped
  • 2 tsp basil
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 8 oz cooked lnguine
  • ½ lb fresh mushrooms
  • 1 C parmesan cheese
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Directions

  1. Prep Vegetables: Snap off tough asparagus ends, peel the lower quarter, and cut diagonally into 1½" pieces.
  2. Steam: Place asparagus and broccoli florets in a steamer over boiling water. Cover and steam 6-8 minutes, until crisp-tender. Set aside.
  3. Sauté Aromatics: In a large skillet over medium heat, sauté onion and garlic in oil until tender.
  4. Add More Veg: Add carrot and chopped peppers; sauté until crisp-tender. Remove from heat and drain.
  5. Make Cream Base: In a medium skillet, combine whipping cream, broth, green onions, basil, and salt. Cook over medium-high 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Cook Pasta: Cook linguine per package directions. Drain well and place in a large serving bowl.
  7. Combine: Add reserved vegetables, cream mixture, and sliced mushrooms. Toss gently to combine.
  8. Finish: Sprinkle with Parmesan and pepper, toss lightly, and serve immediately.

Chef's Notes

  • This is vegetarian and can be made vegan with a plant-based substitute for Parmesan cheese
