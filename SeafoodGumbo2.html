Seafood Gumbo

Ingredients

  • 2 lb shrimp, peeled
  • 1 lb crawfish, peeled
  • 1 lb lump crabmeat
  • 1 qt oysters and liquid
  • 2 qt water
  • 1 C oil
  • 1 C flour
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 rib celery, chopped
  • 1 bag frozen okra (optional)
  • 5 green onions, chopped
  • 3-4 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1-2 tsp white pepper
  • ½ tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp thyme
  • 3 tsp parsley
  • 2 tsp Tabasco

Directions

  1. Make Roux: Cook oil and flour until a dark brown roux forms. Remove from heat.
  2. Add Trinity: Stir in onions, bell pepper, and celery, mixing constantly until the roux cools slightly.
  3. Simmer Base: In an 8-qt stock pot, heat water and add garlic powder, oyster juice, and the roux. Bring to a boil, then simmer 45 minutes. (Add sliced okra at this stage if using.)
  4. Add Seafood: Add seafood and seasonings; simmer 15 minutes.
  5. Finish: Stir in green onions, parsley, and Tabasco and simmer 10 minutes more.
  6. Serve: Serve over rice, topped with gumbo filé.
