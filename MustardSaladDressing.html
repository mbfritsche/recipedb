Mustard Salad Dressing

Ingredients

  • 80 ml olive oil
  • 2 tbs white wine
  • ½ clove garlic, chopped
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients. Pour over salad and toss. Season to taste.
