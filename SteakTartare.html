Steak Tartare

Ingredients

  • 300g high-quality beef fillet, freshly minced or finely chopped
  • 1 small shallot, finely diced
  • 2 tbs of capers, rinsed and chopped
  • 2 tbs of fresh parsley, finely chopped
  • 1 tbs of Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbs of olive oil or avocado oil
  • 1 tsp of Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 raw egg yolk (optional)
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • Toast or crackers, for serving
  • Cornichons for serving

Directions

  1. Start Fresh: Use very fresh, well-chilled beef. It's crucial for steak tartare
  2. Mix: In a bowl, combine minced beef, shallots, capers, parsley, mustard, oil, and Worcestershire sauce until evenly mixed.
  3. Season: Add salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste.
  4. Plate: Shape into patties or mounds. Make a small well in the center and add an egg yolk, if using.
  5. Serve: Serve immediately with toast or crackers on the side. Cornichons or pickles make a great crunchy, tangy side.
