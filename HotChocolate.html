Hot Chocolate

Ingredients

  • ¼ C cocoa
  • ¼ C sugar
  • pinch salt
  • 4 C milk
  • ½ C water
  • ½ tsp vanilla

Directions

  1. Mix Base: In a medium saucepan, whisk dry ingredients. Gradually add water and mix until smooth.
  2. Cook: Bring to a boil over medium heat, then reduce heat and simmer 2 minutes.
  3. Add Milk: Stir in milk (use whole milk for a richer flavor if desired).
  4. Heat Gently: Raise heat to medium and warm just to scalding - do not boil.
  5. Finish: Add vanilla and whisk vigorously to create froth and prevent a skin from forming.
  6. Adjust & Serve: Taste and adjust cocoa and sugar incrementally. Serve immediately.
