Cajun Seasoning

Ingredients

  • 2½ tbs paprika
  • 2 tbs salt
  • 2 tbs garlic powder
  • 1 tbs onion powder
  • 1 tbs black pepper
  • 1 tbs cayenne pepper
  • 1 tbs dried leaf oregano
  • 1 tbs dried leaf thyme

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients thoroughly and store in an airtight container. Makes about ¾ cup.
