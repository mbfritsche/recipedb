Egg Salad

Ingredients

  • 6 hard-cooked eggs, diced
  • 3 tbs mayonnaise
  • 1 tbs Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbs sweet pickle relish
  • 1 tbs Louisiana hot sauce
  • 1 tsp horseradish
  • 1 stalk celery, finely diced
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp curry powder
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp sugar
Optional Ingredients
  • 1 small red onion or shallot, finely diced
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp lemon juice

Directions

  1. Dice the eggs using a knife or an egg slicer and set aside. This is one time in which a gadget is worth its weight
  2. Add the mayonnaise, Dijon, hot sauce, relish, and seasonings. If you are using any optional ingredients, add them now
  3. Gently fold with a silicon spatula to avoid breaking the eggs into smaller pieces
