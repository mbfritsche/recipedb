Macquechoux #2

Ingredients

Corn
  • 12 ears corn
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 large tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 1 tbs sugar
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp Louisiana Hot Sauce
  • 1 tbs butter
  • 2 tbs peanut oil
Sauteed Shrimp
  • 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp white pepper
  • ½ tsp Accent
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbs rice wine
  • 1 tbs soy sauce
  • 1 tbs Tony Chacherie's seasoning
  • 1 tbs olive oil

Directions

Corn
  1. Prep Corn: Remove kernels from cobs with a sharp knife. Scrape cobs to extract juices.
  2. Sauté Base: Heat peanut oil and butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat until butter melts. Add onions, garlic, and bell pepper and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
  3. Build: Add remaining ingredients and cook for about 5 minutes.
  4. Simmer: Reduce heat, cover, and cook until corn is tender, about 15-20 minutes.
Shrimp
  1. Marinate: Season shrimp with salt, white pepper, Accent, soy sauce, and rice wine. Let rest briefly.
  2. Sauté: Heat olive oil in a hot cast iron skillet. Add shrimp and cook quickly.
  3. Deglaze: Add a little rice wine halfway through cooking.
  4. Finish: Cook until shrimp are pink and opaque, then remove from heat.
  5. Combine: Add shrimp to the corn mixture during the final minutes of cooking.
