Vegan Pumpkin Risotto (V)

Ingredients

  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • 2 C canned pumpkin
  • 2 tbs coconut oil
  • 2 C rice
  • 6 C vegetable broth
  • ¼ C pumpkin seeds
  • ¼ C chopped pecans

Directions

  1. Start Risotto: Heat olive oil in a large saucepan. Add pumpkin and cook over medium heat ~7 minutes, stirring often. Stir in rice to coat.
  2. Add Broth: Add 3 cups vegetable broth, bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer, stirring constantly.
  3. Cook Rice: Continue adding vegetable broth gradually as it is absorbed, stirring frequently, until rice is tender and creamy, ~15-20 minutes total.
  4. Finish: Stir in coconut oil, pumpkin seeds, and pecans. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Serve: Serve warm.
