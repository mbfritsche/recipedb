Shrimp Creole #2

Ingredients

  • 3 lb medium shrimp
  • 2 qt shrimp stock
  • 1 lb butter
  • 4 onions, chopped
  • 2 bell pepper, chopped
  • 6 ribs celery, chopped
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 cans stewed tomatoes
  • 2 small cans tomato paste
  • 1 qt ketchup
  • ½ tsp white pepper
  • 2 tbs Cajun seasoning
  • 3 tbs Tiger Sauce
  • 1 tbs sugar
  • 2 tsp Tabasco sauce
  • 2 tbs Worcestershire sauce
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1½ tsp thyme
  • 4 tbs lemon juice

Directions

  1. Prep Shrimp: Peel and devein shrimp, reserving the heads and shells.
  2. Make Stock: In 3 quarts cold water, add quartered onion, celery with leaves, garlic, 1 tsp salt, and the shrimp heads and shells. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer 30 minutes. Strain the stock twice to remove sediment.
  3. Sauté Vegetables: In a pot, sauté onion, celery, bell pepper, and garlic in butter until tender.
  4. Build Sauce: Add all remaining ingredients except shrimp. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer 30 minutes.
  5. Cook Shrimp: Add shrimp and simmer 10 minutes, just until cooked through.
  6. Serve: Mound ~150 g cooked rice in the center of the plate and ladle sauce around it. Garnish with freshly chopped parsley and serve hot.
