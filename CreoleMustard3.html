Creole Mustard Sauce #3

Ingredients

  • 2 oz shallots, minced
  • 1 oz cider vinegar
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 small bay leaf
  • ½ C dry white wine
  • ½ C heavy cream
  • 8 oz unsalted butter, softened
  • ¼ C Creole mustard
  • 2 tbs Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbs Gulden's Spicy Brown Mustard

Directions

  1. Combine the shallots, vinegar, peppercorns, bay leaf, and wine and
  2. Reduce the mixture to about 6 ounces, then add the cream and reduce by half.
  3. Strain the sauce and return to the heat.
  4. Whisk in the butter gradually over low heat and add the mustards.
