Creole Mustard Sauce #3

Ingredients

  • 2 oz shallots, minced
  • 1 oz cider vinegar
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 small bay leaf
  • ½ C dry white wine
  • ½ C heavy cream
  • 8 oz unsalted butter, softened
  • ¼ C Creole mustard
  • 2 tbs Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbs Gulden's Spicy Brown Mustard

Directions

  1. Reduce Base: Combine shallots, vinegar, peppercorns, bay leaf, and wine in a saucepan. Reduce over medium heat to about 6 oz.
  2. Add Cream: Stir in cream and continue reducing until the mixture is reduced by half.
  3. Strain: Strain the sauce, discarding solids, and return the liquid to the pan over low heat.
  4. Finish: Gradually whisk in butter until emulsified, then whisk in the mustards.
  5. Serve: Serve warm.
