Beer Biscuits

Ingredients

  • 4 C Bisquick
  • 12 oz beer
  • 2 tbs sugar

Directions

  1. Mix & Shape: Combine all ingredients, drop spoonfuls onto a greased baking sheet, and brush tops with melted butter.
  2. Bake: Bake at 450°F for 10–12 minutes, until golden.
