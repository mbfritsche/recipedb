Oatmeal Butterscotch Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1¼ C flour
  • 3 C oatmeal (Quick or Old Fashioned uncooked)
  • 1 C butter
  • 12 oz butterscotch morsels
  • ¾ C sugar
  • ¾ C brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¾ tsp cinnamon

Directions

  1. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Mix Dry: Combine flour, salt, cinnamon, and baking soda; set aside.
  3. Cream Base: Cream butter with sugars, then beat in eggs and vanilla.
  4. Combine: Add dry ingredients and mix well. Fold in morsels and oatmeal until just combined.
  5. Bake: Spoon dough by tablespoonfuls onto an ungreased baking sheet. Bake 7-9 minutes.
  6. Cool: Transfer to a wire rack to cool. Cookies will firm up slightly as they cool.
