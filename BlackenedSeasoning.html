Blackened Seasoning

Ingredients

  • 2 tbs paprika
  • 2 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 2 tbs garlic powder
  • 2 tbs onion powder
  • 1 tbs kosher salt
  • 1 tbs black pepper
  • ½ tsp cayenne
  • 1 tbs sugar

Directions

  1. Combine all seasonings and store in an airtight container
