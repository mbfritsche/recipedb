Oysters en Brochette

Ingredients

  • 2 dz oysters and their liquor
  • 2 dz mushroom caps
  • ½ lb thick sliced bacon
  • 2 dz cherry tomatoes
  • 8 oz butter
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbs Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tsp Tabasco sauce
  • 5 tbs lemon juice
  • 2 tbs bacon drippings

Directions

  1. Cook Bacon: In a skillet, fry thick-sliced bacon strips until almost crisp. Remove bacon and reserve the drippings.
  2. Assemble: Place oysters, cherry tomatoes, and mushroom caps in a fireproof dish. Cover with sauce and bacon cut into 1½" pieces.
  3. Bake: Bake at 375°F for ~15 minutes, until oysters curl and are heated through.
  4. Serve: Remove from oven and spoon oysters and sauce over toasted French bread. Garnish with chopped spinach and serve immediately.
