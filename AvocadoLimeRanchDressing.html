Avocado Lime Ranch Dressing (v)

Ingredients

  • 240 g ripe Haas avocados (~2 medium)
  • 120 g light mayo
  • 60 g distilled white vinegar
  • 60 g fat free buttermilk
  • 60 g lime juice
  • 1 packet ranch seasoning mix
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp sugar
  • pinch each salt and pepper
  • 1 tbs water

Directions

  1. Blend Base: Add avocados, mayo, vinegar, buttermilk, lime juice, ranch mix, and seasonings to a food processor. Blend until smooth.
  2. Emulsify: With the processor running, slowly stream in olive oil until combined.
  3. Adjust: Add water to reach desired thickness and adjust seasoning to taste.
  4. Chill: Cover and refrigerate until ready to use.

Chef's Notes

  • This is a Chik-fil-A copycat recipe
  • You can substitute buttermilk by mixing ¼ C milk with 1 tbs white vinegar or lemon juice
  • Dairy-free buttermilk will make this recipe dairy-free
  • Dairy-free buttermilk and vegan mayo will make this recipe vegan
