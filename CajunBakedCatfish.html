Cajun Baked Catfish

Ingredients

  • 2 large raw catfish fillets, about 1 pound
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp Louisiana hot sauce
  • Salt, black pepper, and cayenne pepper to taste
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp dried parsley flakes
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 3 tbs cold butter, cut into pieces
  • ½ tsp lemon zest, optional

Directions

  1. Prep Oven & Pan: Preheat oven to 400°F. Liberally grease a 12" cast‑iron skillet with oil.
  2. Arrange Fish: Place catfish fillets in the skillet.
  3. Season with Citrus: Pour ½ of the lemon juice over one fillet and repeat with the second. Use a brush or spoon to coat the tops evenly.
  4. Add Heat: Repeat the process using the hot sauce to coat both fillets.
  5. Season: Sprinkle salt, black pepper, and cayenne pepper to taste over the fillets. Distribute ½ of the dried seasonings over each fillet.
  6. Add Butter & Zest: Arrange butter pieces around the fish. Sprinkle lemon zest over the top if using.
  7. Bake: Bake 15–20 minutes, until the fish flakes easily and a thermometer inserted into the fillets reads at least 145°F.
  8. Serve: Remove from oven and serve immediately.
