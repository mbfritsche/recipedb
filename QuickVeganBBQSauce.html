Quick Vegan BBQ Sauce

Ingredients

  • 2 C organic ketchup
  • 2 tbs molasses
  • ¼ C brown sugar or pure maple syrup
  • ¼ C apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tbs low sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tbs vegan Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tsp Sriracha, or other hot sauce

Directions

  1. Add all ingredients to a medium sized bowl and whisk to combine.

Chef's Notes

  • Yield: 18 oz, store refrigerated for up to a week
  • Look for a ketchup without high fructose corn syrup.
  • For Gluten Free, use gluten free tamari instead of the low sodium soy sauce.
