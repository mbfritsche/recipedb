Cream of Mushroom Soup (v)

Ingredients

  • 3 tbs butter
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 4 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 3 tbs olive oil
  • 2 lbs assorted cleaned and sliced fresh mushrooms (reserve ~½ C for garnish)
  • ½ C white wine (e.g., Chardonnay, Pinot Grigio or Sauvignon Blanc)
  • ½ C all-purpose flour
  • 3 qt vegetable stock
  • 1½ C heavy whipping cream
  • 3 tbs fresh parsley, minced
  • 1 tbs fresh thyme, minced
  • Sea salt and pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Caramelize Onions: In a stockpot over medium-low heat, melt butter and cook onions ~45 minutes, stirring occasionally, until deeply caramelized. This step is critical.
  2. Add Garlic: Stir in garlic and cook 1-2 minutes, until fragrant.
  3. Cook Mushrooms: Add olive oil and mushrooms, increase heat to medium-high, and sauté 15-20 minutes, stirring often, until mushrooms are fully cooked down and concentrated. (Take your time—this step matters.)
  4. Deglaze: Add white wine and cook ~5 minutes, stirring, until absorbed.
  5. Build Soup: Stir in flour, add stock, increase heat to high, and bring to a boil - the soup should thicken.
  6. Blend: Purée until smooth using an immersion blender or countertop blender.
  7. Finish Soup: Stir in cream, herbs, salt, and pepper to taste.
  8. Garnish: Heat 2 tbs olive oil in a hot sauté pan. Add reserved mushrooms and sauté ~5 minutes, until browned; add a pinch of herbs for color.
  9. Serve: Ladle soup into bowls and top with sautéed mushrooms, chopped parsley, and a few drops of good olive oil.
  10. Yield: About 10 servings

Chef's Notes

  • Omit the heavy cream and use a plant‑based butter to make this a vegan mushroom soup.
  • Soup can be made up to 2 days in advance.
  • Reheat gently in a small pot over low heat until hot.
  • Refrigerate up to 5 days. Freeze, well covered, up to 3 months. Thaw 1 day in the refrigerator before reheating.
  • If the soup is too thin, add a slurry of ¼ C cornstarch + ¼ C cold water, then bring to a boil to activate.
  • If cream separates during reheating, whisk in a small slurry (2 tbs cornstarch + 1 tbs water) and bring to a boil while whisking.
