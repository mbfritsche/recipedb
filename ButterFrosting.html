Basic Butter Frosting

Ingredients

  • 3 tbs butter
  • 3 tbs shortening
  • 1½ tsp vanilla
  • 3 C confectioners' sugar
  • 1½ tbs milk or cream

Directions

  1. Cream butter with vanilla.
  2. Add confectioners' sugar gradually, beating thoroughly after each addition.
  3. Stir in milk and beat until frosting is of spreading consistency.
