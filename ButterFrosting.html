Basic Butter Frosting

Ingredients

  • 3 tbs butter
  • 3 tbs shortening
  • 1½ tsp vanilla
  • 3 C confectioners' sugar
  • 1½ tbs milk or cream

Directions

  1. Cream Butter: Beat butter with vanilla until smooth.
  2. Add Sugar: Gradually add confectioners' sugar, beating well after each addition.
  3. Finish: Stir in milk and beat until the frosting reaches a smooth, spreadable consistency.
