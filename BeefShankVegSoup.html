Beef Shank Vegetable Soup

Ingredients

  • 3-4 lb beef shanks
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder and onion powder, to taste
  • 2 tbs oil or bacon dripping
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 stalks celery, chopped
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 jalapeno, chopped
  • 1 tbs garlic, minced
  • 1 tbs tomato paste
  • ½ C all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbs Colman's dry mustard
  • 1 tbs red wine vinegar
  • 1 tbs Worcestershire sauce
  • 4 C vegetable stock
  • 1 pint Guinness stout
  • 4-6 potatoes, cubed
  • 3-5 carrots, sliced
  • 1 lb frozen mixed vegetables
  • 1 lb frozen corn
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1 tsp rosemary, crushed
  • 1 tsp thyme, crushed
  • Louisiana Hot Sauce, to taste

Directions

  1. Prep Oven & Beef: Preheat oven to 300°F. Wash beef shanks, pat dry, and let sit at room temperature 1-2 hours.
  2. Brown Beef: Heat oil over medium heat and brown shanks in 2-3 batches, about 10 minutes, until well browned on all sides. Remove and set aside.
  3. Cook Veggies: Add onions, celery, carrots, bell pepper, and jalapeño; sauté 5-6 minutes until limp.
  4. Build Base: Stir in tomato paste and cook until darkened. Add garlic and cook 1 minute. Stir in flour and cook 1-2 minutes to remove raw taste.
  5. Deglaze & Season: Add vegetable stock and Guinness, then Worcestershire, red wine vinegar, salt, pepper, and herbs. Simmer briefly to reduce.
  6. Braise: Return beef to the pot, cover with vegetables and sauce, cover, and bake 2 hours, stirring occasionally.
  7. Add Vegetables: Add potatoes, carrots, frozen vegetables, and any other root vegetables you have. Cover and return to oven 90 minutes more.
  8. Finish: When vegetables are tender, remove meat, chop coarsely, return to the pot, and cover with liquid. Adjust seasoning with salt and pepper.

Chef's Notes

  • Use all the vegetable scraps plus 2 tbs anchovy paste to the stock
