Toll House Cookies

Ingredients

  • ½ lb butter (2 sticks)
  • ¾ C sugar
  • 2¼ C flour
  • ¾ C light brown sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 12 oz semi-sweet chocolate chips (1 package)
  • 12 oz nuts (OPTIONAL)

Directions

  1. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Cream Base: Cream butter and sugars until combined. For chewier cookies, melt the butter first; otherwise, use room-temperature butter.
  3. Add Eggs: Beat in eggs one at a time, then add vanilla.
  4. Add Dry: Sift dry ingredients together and gradually mix into the batter until fully incorporated.
  5. Mix‑Ins: Stir in chocolate chips and nuts, if using (pecans or walnuts work well).
  6. Bake: Drop dough by tablespoonfuls 1" apart on an ungreased baking sheet. Bake ~7 minutes, until set.
  7. Cool: Transfer to a wire rack to cool.
