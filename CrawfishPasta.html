Crawfish Pasta

Ingredients

  • 1 lb peeled crawfish
  • ¼ C parsley
  • 1 package noodles (any)
  • 1 medium bell pepper
  • 1 stick butter
  • ½ pint Half & Half or whipping cream
  • 1 bunch green onions
  • 1 medium onion
  • Salt, red pepper, white pepper, black pepper, and garlic powder, to taste

Directions

  1. Sauté Base: Sauté onions, bell pepper, parsley, and green onion tops, seasoning to taste, until softened.
  2. Add Crawfish: Add crawfish and sauté 10 minutes.
  3. Finish Sauce: Stir in cream and cook until the sauce thickens.
  4. Cook Pasta: Prepare noodles according to package directions.
  5. Combine & Serve: Add cooked noodles to the crawfish mixture, toss to coat, and serve immediately.
