Sheet Pan Sausage and Potatoes

Ingredients

  • 24 oz petite red potatoes
  • 12 oz kielbasa
  • 1 yellow onion
  • 12 oz frozen green beans
  • 2 tbs olive oil
  • ½ tbs Montreal steak seasoning

Directions

  1. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Prep Ingredients: Cut potatoes into 1" pieces, slice kielbasa into medallions, and dice onion into 1" pieces.
  3. Assemble: Add potatoes, kielbasa, onion, and frozen green beans (no need to thaw) to a large baking sheet.
  4. Season: Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with steak seasoning. Toss until everything is evenly coated.
  5. Roast: Roast 20 minutes, then stir the contents of the pan.
  6. Finish: Return to the oven and roast 15 minutes more, or until browned to your liking.
  7. Serve: Serve hot.
