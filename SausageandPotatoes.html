Sheet Pan Sausage and Potatoes

Ingredients

  • 24 oz petite red potatoes
  • 12 oz kielbasa
  • 1 yellow onion
  • 12 oz frozen green beans
  • 2 tbs olive oil
  • ½ tbs Montreal steak seasoning

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400° F.
  2. Wash and slice the potatoes into 1-inch pieces, slice the kielbasa into medallions, and dice the onion into 1-inch pieces
  3. Add the potatoes, kielbasa, onion, and frozen green beans (no need to thaw) to a large baking sheet.
  4. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle the steak seasoning over top. Toss the ingredients until everything is coated in oil and spices.
  5. Transfer the baking sheet to the oven and roast in the oven for 20 minutes, then give the ingredients on the sheet pan a good stir.
  6. Roast for an additional 15 minutes, or until the ingredients achieve the level of browning you desire. Serve hot.
