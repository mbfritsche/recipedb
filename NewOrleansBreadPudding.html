New Orleans Bourbon Bread Pudding

Ingredients

Bread Pudding
  • 1 18-20" French baguette, torn into 1-inch pieces (10 cups)
  • 1 C raisins
  • ¾ C bourbon (divided)
  • 8 egg yolks
  • 1½ C light brown sugar
  • 3 C heavy cream
  • 1 C whole milk
  • 1 tbsp vanilla extract
  • 1½ tsp ground cinnamon (divided)
  • ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 6 tbsp unsalted butter (cubed and chilled)
Bourbon Sauce
  • ¼ C bourbon (divided)
  • 1½ tsp cornstarch
  • ¾ C heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp unsalted butter (cut into small pieces)
  • Pinch salt

Directions

Bread Pudding
  1. Prep Oven & Dish: Preheat oven to 450°F. Butter a 9x13" baking dish.
  2. Toast Bread: Arrange bread in a single layer on a baking sheet. Bake ~12 minutes, turning halfway and rotating the pan, until crisp and browned. Let cool. Reduce oven temperature to 300°F.
  3. Plump Raisins: Heat raisins with ½ C bourbon in a small saucepan over medium-high until simmering (2-3 minutes). Strain, reserving raisins and bourbon separately.
  4. Make Custard: Whisk egg yolks, brown sugar, cream, milk, vanilla, 1 tsp cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt. Whisk in remaining ¼ C bourbon plus the bourbon used to plump the raisins.
  5. Soak Bread: Add toasted bread and toss to coat. Let sit ~30 minutes, tossing occasionally. If bread is still firm when squeezed, soak an additional 15-20 minutes.
  6. Assemble: Pour half the bread mixture into the prepared dish and sprinkle with half the raisins. Repeat with remaining bread and raisins. Cover with foil.
  7. Bake Covered: Bake 45 minutes.
  8. Make Topping: Mix granulated sugar and ½ tsp cinnamon. Pdevilinch in 6 tbs butter until pea‑sized crumbs form.
  9. Bake Uncovered: Remove foil, sprinkle topping over pudding, and bake 20-25 minutes until custard is just set.
  10. Crisp Top: Increase oven to 450°F and bake ~2 minutes until the top forms a golden crust.
  11. Cool & Serve: Cool on a wire rack 30 minutes (or up to 2 hours). Serve plain or with Bourbon Sauce.
Bourbon Sauce
  1. Make Slurry: In a small bowl, whisk cornstarch with 2 tbs bourbon until smooth.
  2. Heat Cream: In a small saucepan over medium heat, warm cream and sugar until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Thicken Sauce: Whisk in the cornstarch mixture and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to low and cook 3-5 minutes, until thickened.
  4. Finish: Remove from heat and stir in salt, butter, and remaining 2 tbs bourbon.
  5. Serve: Drizzle warm sauce over bread pudding.
  6. Store & Reheat: Leftover bread pudding keeps up to 3 days refrigerated. Reheat in the microwave or in a 350°F oven, covered with foil.

Chef's Notes

  • French baguette works best, but other breads may be used if preferred.
  • Raisins may be omitted or replaced with other dried fruit such as cranberries or cherries.
  • Bourbon can be swapped for whiskey, rum, Kahlua, or amaretto to change the flavor profile.
  • For an alcohol-free option, omit bourbon entirely - plump raisins in water and replace remaining bourbon in the recipe with milk or cream.

Make Ahead Instructions

  • Prepare the bread pudding through Step 5 (before baking). Cover and refrigerate up to 1 day, then bake as directed.
  • The bourbon sauce can be made up to 2 days in advance and refrigerated. Reheat gently before serving.
  • Alternatively, bake the bread pudding fully, cool, cover, and refrigerate up to 2 days. Reheat covered in a 350°F oven before serving.

Freezing Instructions

  • Bake the bread pudding and allow it to cool completely. Refrigerate at least 2 hours, then freeze if desired.
  • Cover the pan tightly with foil and freeze up to 3 months.
  • Thaw overnight in the refrigerator.
  • Reheat covered in a 350°F oven until warmed through, about 15 minutes.
