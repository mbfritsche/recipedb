Jalapeño Poppers

Ingredients

  • 12 jalapeño peppers (fresh, halved lengthwise, stems, seeds and membranes removed)
  • 6 oz cream cheese (softened)
  • 1½ C jack cheese (grated Monterrey)
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp cayenne (or less to taste)
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tbs milk
  • 8 tsp Cajun seasoning or Tony Chacherie
  • 1 C panko crumbs or fine dry breadcrumbs
  • ½ C all-purpose flour

Directions

  1. Prep Oven & Pan: Preheat oven to 350°F. Lightly grease a baking sheet and set aside.
  2. Make Filling: In a bowl, cream together cream cheese, Monterey Jack, cumin, and cayenne until smooth.
  3. Egg Wash: In a separate bowl, beat eggs with milk and 2 tsp Cajun seasoning.
  4. Crumb Coating: In a shallow dish, combine panko crumbs with 4 tsp Cajun seasoning.
  5. Flour Coating: In another shallow dish, combine flour with remaining 2 tsp Cajun seasoning.
  6. Fill Peppers: Spread 1 tbs cheese mixture into the center of each jalapeño half.
  7. Bread: Dredge each pepper in flour, dip into egg wash, then coat in panko, pressing to adhere. Repeat if needed for a thicker crust.
  8. Bake: Place peppers cut-side up on the prepared baking sheet. Bake ~30 minutes, until the crust is golden and the filling is hot and runny.
  9. Serve: Remove from oven and serve immediately.
