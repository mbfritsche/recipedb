Crabmeat Eggplant Dressing

Ingredients

  • 2 lb shrimp, peeled and diced
  • 2 lb crabmeat
  • 3 eggplant, sliced in half
  • 1 onion
  • 1 stalk celery
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • ¼ C chicken stock
  • Bread crumbs
  • Salt, pepper, herbes des Provence, red pepper flakes, and allspice, to taste

Directions

  1. Sauté Aromatics: Sweat onion, celery, and garlic in olive oil until softened.
  2. Roast Eggplant: Halve eggplant, rub with olive oil, and bake @400°F until very soft and collapsing. Trim ends, scrape out pulp, and roughly chop.
  3. Season Seafood: Add crabmeat and shrimp to the sautéed vegetables. Season with salt, pepper, herbes de Provence, red pepper flakes, and allspice.
  4. Build Filling: Add chopped eggplant pulp to the seafood mixture over medium heat. Season with salt, pepper, Worcestershire Sauce, Louisiana hot sauce, and chicken stock.
  5. Bind: Stir in ~½ C breadcrumbs and 3 tbs butter to bring the mixture together.
  6. Assemble: Transfer to a buttered casserole dish. Top with breadcrumbs and drizzle with olive oil or dot with butter.
