Cheese Soufflé

Ingredients

  • 6 tbs unsalted butter, plus additional to butter a 5- to 6-cup gratin dish
  • ¼ C finely grated Parmesan cheese
  • 6 tbs all-purpose flour
  • 2 C whole milk
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 5 extra-large eggs (or 6 large ones)
  • 2½ C grated Swiss cheese, preferably Gruyère (about 6 ounces), plus three more optional slices for garnish (roughly 2x3")
  • 3 tbs minced chives

Directions

  1. Prep Oven & Dish: Preheat oven to 400°F. Butter a 5-6 cup gratin dish and coat with half the Parmesan.
  2. Make White Sauce: Melt 6 tbd butter, whisk in flour, cook 10 seconds, then whisk in cold milk all at once. Stir until thick and smooth (~2 minutes). Season with salt and pepper; remove from heat.
  3. Add Eggs & Cheese: Beat eggs. When sauce has cooled about 10 minutes, quickly stir in eggs, cheese, and chives.
  4. Assemble: Pour into prepared dish. Sprinkle with 2 tbs Parmesan and arrange Gruyère in a circle on top.
  5. Bake: Bake 30-40 minutes, until puffed and well browned.
  6. Serve: Best served immediately while inflated.

Chef's Notes

  • This dish can be prepared ahead:
    • If baking within 3-4 hours, it can remain at room temperature.
    • If assembling a day ahead, refrigerate and bring back to room temperature before baking.
