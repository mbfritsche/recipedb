Smoked Mushrooms (V)

Ingredients

  • 1 lb mushrooms
  • 1 tbs liquid smoke

Directions

  1. Prep: Clean mushrooms thoroughly with a stiff brush.
  2. Set Up: Add liquid smoke to the bottom of a Dutch oven. Place mushrooms on a rack set above the liquid.
  3. Smoke: Cover and cook in the oven on high heat for 5 minutes. Turn off heat and let mushrooms sit covered for 20-25 minutes.
  4. Serve: Remove and serve or use in other recipes.
