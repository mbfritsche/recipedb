Crawfish Stew

Ingredients

  • ¾ C of oil
  • 1 C all-purpose flour
  • 2 onions
  • 1 green bell pepper
  • 2 sticks of celery
  • 1 bunch of green onions
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 4 C of hot water or vegetable stock
  • 1 tbs of Cajun seasoning
  • 1 tsp of pepper
  • 1 tsp of garlic powder
  • 1 tbs of parsley flakes
  • 2 lbs crawfish tails

Directions

  1. Prep Pot: Heat a large pot over medium-low heat.
  2. Prep Vegetables: Chop onions, bell pepper, celery, and garlic; set aside.
  3. Make Roux: Cook flour and oil together, stirring constantly, until a dark brown chocolate-colored roux forms. This takes time - be patient.
  4. Add Vegetables: Stir chopped vegetables into the roux and cook 10 minutes, stirring frequently.
  5. Add Liquid: Pour in hot water or stock and mix well to fully incorporate the roux.
  6. Season: Stir in Cajun seasoning, pepper, garlic powder, and parsley flakes.
  7. Simmer Base: Increase heat to a boil, cover, then reduce to a simmer. Cook 1 hour, stirring every 15 minutes.
  8. Add Crawfish: Stir in 2 lb crawfish, cover, and simmer 30 minutes more.
