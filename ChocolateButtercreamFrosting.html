Chocolate Buttercream Frosting

Ingredients

  • 1 C unsalted butter, softened
  • 3 C confectioners' sugar
  • ½ C unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 4 tbs heavy whipping cream
  • 2 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • ¼ tsp salt

Directions

  1. Cream Butter: Beat butter on high speed about 3 minutes until light and creamy.
  2. Add Dry Ingredients: Gradually mix in confectioners' sugar and cocoa powder.
  3. Finish: Stir in heavy cream, vanilla, and salt. Beat on medium-high about 3 minutes, scraping the bowl and adding more cream as needed for a smooth, spreadable texture.
