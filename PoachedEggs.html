Poached Eggs

Ingredients

  • 8 large eggs
  • 1½ qt water
  • 2 C vinegar

Directions

  1. Boil Water: Bring a large saucepan of water to a boil.
  2. Add Eggs: Crack eggs one at a time into a small cup, then gently slip into the boiling water without breaking the yolks.
  3. Simmer: Reduce to a gentle simmer and cook 3-4 minutes, nudging eggs occasionally for even cooking.
  4. Hold: Remove with a slotted spoon and place in cold water until ready to serve.
  5. Use: Ideal for Eggs Hussarde and Eggs Benedict.
