Banana Cupcakes

Ingredients

  • 2½ C flour
  • ½ C shortening
  • 1½ C sugar
  • 1 C mashed banana (3-4 medium bananas)
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 160 ml buttermilk
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Directions

  1. Prep Dry: Combine dry ingredients in a bowl; set aside.
  2. Mash Bananas: Peel and mash 3-4 very ripe bananas (black skins are fine).
  3. Mix Base: Add bananas and shortening to dry ingredients and mix until crumbly.
  4. Add Wet: Beat in buttermilk, vanilla, and eggs one at a time, mixing about 1 minute each.
  5. Bake: Spoon batter into lined cupcake pan, filling ½-¾ full. Bake at 350°F for 17-19 minutes.
  6. Finish: Cool completely, then frost or serve plain.

Chef's Notes

  • In case you do not have buttermilk, mix one tablespoon vinegar with enough milk to make one cup and allow it to sit for 5-10 minutes
