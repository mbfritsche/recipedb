Totchos

Ingredients

  • 1 28 oz bag frozen tater tots, ~90 tots
  • 1 lb lean ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 3 tbs tomato paste
  • ¼ C water
  • ¾ C black beans, drained and rinsed
  • ½ C corn
  • ½ C Mexican cheese, shredded
  • 4 oz queso blanco
  • 2 tbs milk

Directions

  1. Bake Tots: Preheat oven to 425°F. Arrange tater tots in a single layer on a lined sheet pan and bake until golden and crisp, about 20-25 minutes. Season lightly with salt.
  2. Cook Beef: In a large pan over medium heat, cook ground beef, breaking it up finely. Stir in taco seasoning, tomato paste, and water. Simmer until well coated.
  3. Warm Cheese: Heat cheese dip with a little milk until smooth and pourable.
  4. Assemble: Top crispy tots with taco meat, then add black beans and corn. Drizzle with cheese dip and sprinkle with shredded cheese.
  5. Melt: Return to oven and bake until cheese is melted, about 3-5 minutes.
  6. Serve: Finish with your preferred toppings and serve hot.
