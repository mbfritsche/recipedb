Turkey Sausage Rolls

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 1 egg
  • ½ C breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 2 tsp sage
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 package puff pastry
  • Egg wash for brushing on top (1 egg with a splash of milk)

Directions

  1. Prep Oven & Pan: Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Mix Filling: In a bowl, combine turkey, egg, breadcrumbs, and seasonings until evenly mixed; set aside.
  3. Prep Pastry: Roll dough out on a floured surface and cut into 3 long strips per sheet (6 strips total).
  4. Fill & Seal: Divide meat mixture into 6 equal portions. Roll each portion along the center of a pastry strip. Fold pastry over filling and seal edges with a fork.
  5. Finish: Brush with egg wash. Cut each long strip into 6 pieces, yielding 36 pieces total.
  6. Bake: Arrange on the prepared baking sheet 1-2" apart. Bake 20-25 minutes, until pastry is golden brown.
  7. Rest & Serve: Let cool 5 minutes, then serve.
