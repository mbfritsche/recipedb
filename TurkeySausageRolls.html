Turkey Sausage Rolls

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 1 egg
  • ½ C breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 2 tsp sage
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 package puff pastry
  • Egg wash for brushing on top (1 egg with a splash of milk)

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F, line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Mix all ingredients, turkey, egg, breadcrumbs, and seasonings and set aside.
  3. Roll out the dough on a floured surface and cut each piece into 3 long strips, giving 6 strips in total.
  4. Separate the meat mixture into 6 equal portions and roll out in the middle of each pastry strip. Fold over the pastry and use a fork to seal the ends.
  5. Brush with egg wash and cut each long piece into 6 smaller pieces, 36 pieces in total.
  6. Place on a cookie sheet lined with parchment paper keeping them an inch or two apart.
  7. Bake for 20-25 minutes or until the pastry is golden brown, let cool for 5 minutes and serve
