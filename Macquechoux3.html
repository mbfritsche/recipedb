Macquechoux #3

Ingredients

  • 1 dz ears fresh corn
  • 1 stick unsalted butter
  • 1½ C onions, chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 3 ribs celery, chopped
  • 1 C evaporated milk
  • 3 tomatoes, peeled, seeded and chopped
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 4 tbs sugar

Directions

  1. Prep Corn: Shuck corn, remove silk, and cut kernels off in layers into a bowl. Scrape cobs to extract the milk.
  2. Sauté Base: Melt butter in a large saucepan over medium-high heat. Add onions, bell peppers, celery, and tomatoes. Cook until onions are translucent, about 10 minutes.
  3. Build: Stir in salt and peppers. Add corn, reserved milk, sugar, and evaporated milk. Mix well.
  4. Simmer: Reduce heat to medium and cook until corn is tender, about 10-15 minutes.
  5. Finish & Serve: Adjust seasoning to taste and serve hot. Yield 8–10 servings.
