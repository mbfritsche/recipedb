Play Dough

Ingredients

  • 1 C flour
  • 1 C water
  • ½ C salt
  • 1 tbs cooking oil
  • 2 tsp cream of tartar

Directions

  1. Mix flour, water, cooking oil, salt and cream of tartar in a large pot.
  2. Heat slowly, stirring constantly while adding food coloring to get desired color. Continue until mixture forms a ball.
  3. Remove from heat and knead. Store in an airtight container.

Chef's Notes

  • FOR USE AS A CHILD'S TOY ONLY, NOT FOR CONSUMPTION.
