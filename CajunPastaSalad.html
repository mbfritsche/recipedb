Cajun Pasta Salad

Ingredients

  • 2 large avocado
  • 2 large tomato
  • ½ lb cooked rotini
  • 3 crowns of broccoli
  • 3 green onions, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • ½ C olive oil
  • 80 ml balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tbs Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbs Louisiana hot sauce
  • 2 tsp Chinese rice wine
  • Cajun seasoning
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tbs parmesan cheese
  • 24 black olives, chopped
  • 2 cans smoked oysters, halved
  • 1 tsp black pepper

Directions

  1. Cook Pasta & Broccoli: Cook rotini per package directions; drain and set aside. Steam broccoli florets 3 minutes until tender.
  2. Prep Vegetables: Peel, seed, and quarter avocado and tomato. Combine with green onions, broccoli, and red bell pepper.
  3. Add Mix‑Ins: Stir in olives and smoked oysters. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Add cooked pasta and toss.
  4. Make Dressing: Whisk olive oil, balsamic vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, hot sauce, and rice wine until emulsified.
  5. Combine: Pour dressing over pasta and vegetables; mix well.
  6. Finish: Add Parmesan cheese and Cajun seasoning; toss to combine.
  7. Chill: Refrigerate a couple of hours before serving.
