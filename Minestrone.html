Minestrone (V)

Ingredients

  • 3 zucchini, peeled diced
  • 8 oz cannellini beans, soaked
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 clove garlic, chopped
  • 4 sticks celery, chopped
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 1 small head cabbage
  • 4 oz green beans, cut into 1" pieces
  • 8 oz tomatoes
  • 4 tbs olive oil
  • 1 dried red chili
  • 6 C water
  • 1 sprig rosemary
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 12 oz pasta
  • 1 pound fresh basil
  • 1 pound fresh parsley
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Sauté Base: In a large pot, heat oil and sauté onion and garlic until onions are soft.
  2. Add Vegetables: Stir in celery, carrots, cabbage, and green beans. Drain beans and add to the pot along with tomatoes and chili.
  3. Simmer: Add water and bring to a boil. Stir in rosemary and bay leaf, then simmer uncovered ~1½ hrs.
  4. Add Pasta: Stir in pasta and cook 10 minutes. Add zucchini and cook 20 minutes more.
  5. Finish: Remove rosemary and bay leaf. Season with herbs, salt, and pepper to taste.
  6. Serve: Enjoy hot with crusty French bread and a nice Chianti - preferably with an old friend.
