Southern-style Macaroni and Cheese

Ingredients

  • 16 oz elbow macaroni
  • 2 large eggs
  • 12 oz evaporated milk
  • ¼ C butter, melted
  • 2 tbs prepared mustard
  • 1 tsp seasoned salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 8 oz Velveeta, melted
  • 16 oz shredded mild cheddar cheese, divided
  • 16 oz shredded sharp cheddar cheese, divided

Directions

  2. Prep: Cook macaroni according to package directions. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  3. Mix: In a large bowl, whisk together eggs, milk, butter, mustard, seasoned salt, and pepper. Stir in Velveeta and 12 oz each of the mild and sharp cheddar cheeses.
  4. Combine: Drain macaroni and add to the cheese mixture. Stir until evenly coated.
  5. Assemble: Transfer to a greased 3-qt baking dish and top with the remaining cheeses.
  6. Bake: Bake uncovered until cheese is melted and edges are bubbly, about 25-30 minutes.

Chef's Notes

  • A Southern classic, on every holiday table!
