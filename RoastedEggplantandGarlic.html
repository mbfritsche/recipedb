Eggplant and Roasted Garlic Spread (V)

Ingredients

  • 1 large eggplant, approximately 3 pounds
  • 2 tbs olive oil
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 tbs roasted garlic, to taste
  • 2 tbs sherry vinegar (or to taste)
  • 1½ C loosely packed basil leaves, chopped

Directions

  1. Prep Eggplant: Cut eggplant into 1" slices. Brush lightly with olive oil and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Roast: Roast in a 400°F oven ~20 minutes, until browned and tender.
  3. Purée: Once cool, scoop the flesh from the skins and process with the remaining ingredients until smooth.
  4. Finish: Adjust seasoning to taste and serve.

Chef's Notes

  • Can be stored under refrigeration for up to one week.
