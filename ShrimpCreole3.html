Shrimp Creole

Ingredients

  • 1 lb raw shrimp, peeled and deveined
Shrimp Seasoning
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp parsley flakes
  • 1 tsp Louisiana hot sauce
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • Salt, black pepper, and cayenne pepper to taste
Creole Sauce
  • 3 tbs butter or oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 celery ribs, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 14½-oz can stewed tomatoes, see notes
  • 1½ cups tomato sauce
  • ¼ C chicken broth (gluten free)
  • 1 dried bay leaf
  • rice or cauliflower rice for serving
  • Additional salt, black pepper, and cayenne pepper to taste, optional

Directions

  1. Season Shrimp: Toss shrimp with lemon zest, paprika, parsley flakes, hot sauce, onion powder, salt, black pepper, and cayenne to taste. Cover and refrigerate until needed.
  2. Brown Vegetables: Heat oil or butter in an enameled cast‑iron skillet over medium heat. Sauté onion, bell pepper, and celery until well browned.
  3. Add Garlic: Stir in garlic and cook 1 minute more until fragrant.
  4. Build Sauce: Add stewed tomatoes, tomato sauce, broth, and bay leaf. Reduce heat to low.
  5. Simmer: Simmer 20–30 minutes, stirring often, until the sauce darkens to a deep crimson.
  6. Cook Shrimp: Add seasoned shrimp and cook 7–10 minutes, stirring often, until shrimp are opaque and reach 145°F internal temperature.
  7. Serve: Serve as is or over rice or cauliflower rice.
