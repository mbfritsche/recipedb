Cajun Cole Slaw

Ingredients

  • 5 tbs mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Louisiana hot sauce
  • 2 tbs Creole mustard
  • 2 tbs ketchup
  • 2 tbs olive oil
  • 1 tbs apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp garlic salt
  • 1 tbs Worcestershire sauce
  • Juice of one medium lemon
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 4 Bell peppers, sliced
  • 2 Onions, medium, shredded
  • 1 Large cabbage, shredded

Directions

  1. Make Dressing: In a bowl, beat mayonnaise and mustard until combined. Slowly whisk in olive oil until the dressing thickens back to a mayo-like consistency.
  2. Season: Beat in Louisiana hot sauce, then ketchup. Add salt and garlic salt. Whisk in wine vinegar to thin, then lemon juice. Adjust salt and pepper to taste.
  3. Assemble Slaw: Place shredded cabbage, peppers, and onions in a large bowl. Pour dressing over and toss well.
  4. Chill: Refrigerate about 1 hour before serving to let flavors combine.

Chef's Notes

  • Blue Plate Mayonnaise is the local favorite, made in New Orleans
  • Zatarain makes a great Creole mustard
  • The dressing can also be made in a blender
  • You can reduce prep time by using a 1lb bag of cole slaw mix
