Shrimp and Crab Casserole

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb uncooked shrimp (31-40 per pound), peeled and deveined
  • 2 cans (6 ounces each) lump crabmeat, drained
  • 1 C shredded Colby-Monterey Jack cheese
  • 17.6 ounces ready-to-serve long grain and wild rice
  • ¼ C butter, cubed
  • 2 celery ribs, chopped
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 1½ C half-and-half cream
  • 1 teaspoon seafood seasoning
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp hot pepper sauce
  • ¼ tsp pepper

Directions

  1. Prep Oven & Rice: Preheat oven to 350°F. Spread cooked rice evenly in a greased 13x9" baking dish.
  2. Sauté Vegetables: In a large skillet over medium-high heat, melt butter. Add celery and onion and cook 6-8 minutes, stirring, until tender.
  3. Make Roux: Stir in flour until blended.
  4. Add Cream: Gradually whisk in cream. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly, and cook 1-2 minutes until thickened.
  5. Season & Add Seafood: Stir in seafood seasoning, salt, pepper sauce, and pepper. Gently fold in shrimp and crab.
  6. Assemble: Spoon the seafood sauce evenly over the rice. Sprinkle with cheese.
  7. Bake: Bake covered at 350°F for 40-45 minutes, until shrimp turn pink.
  8. Rest & Serve: Let stand 5 minutes before serving.

Chef's Notes

  • Make-Ahead Option - Prepare as directed, allowing the sauce to cool slightly before adding shrimp and crab. Cover and refrigerate overnight. Remove from refrigerator 30 minutes before baking and bake as directed.
