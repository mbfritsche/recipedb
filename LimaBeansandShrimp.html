Lima Beans and Shrimp

Ingredients

  • 2 lb fresh peeled Louisiana shrimp
  • 2 tsp concentrated liquid shrimp boil
  • 2 lb fresh (or frozen) baby lima beans or heirloom speckled beans
  • 2 tbs butter
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • ½ bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 rib celery, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 tbs olive oil
  • 1 tbs butter
  • 3 tbs (¼ C) flour
  • 1 tsp Louisiana Hot Sauce
  • Cajun seasoning to taste
  • Cooked brown rice for serving
  • Hot sauce

Directions

  1. Boil Shrimp: Bring shrimp boil and 2 cups water to a rolling boil. Add shrimp, cook for 1 minute, then turn off heat. Let shrimp sit in liquid until pink and mostly opaque, about 15 minutes.
  2. Reserve Stock: Drain shrimp through a sieve set over a bowl, reserving the stock.
  3. Sauté Base: In a large pot, melt butter and cook onion, bell pepper, and celery until translucent, about 6-8 minutes.
  4. Cook Beans: Add beans and enough reserved shrimp stock to cover. Season with salt and pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer, cover loosely, and cook for about 15 minutes.
  5. Thicken Beans: Mash some of the beans with a potato masher to thicken the mixture.
  6. Add Shrimp: Stir in shrimp and cook for about 5 minutes.
  7. Make Roux: In a small skillet, melt butter with olive oil. Add flour and cook, stirring, until lightly browned and nutty.
  8. Finish: Stir roux into the bean and shrimp mixture. Adjust seasoning with hot sauce and Cajun seasoning .
  9. Serve: Simmer on low for about 5 minutes, then serve warm over rice.

Chef's Notes

  • If you have shrimp stock left over, freeze for another use.
