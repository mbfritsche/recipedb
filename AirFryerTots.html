Air Fryer Tater Tots

Ingredients

  • 230 g tater tots, frozen (~25 tots)
  • Avocado oil
  • Salt, lemon pepper, and garlic powder, to taste

Directions

  1. Preheat: Heat air fryer to 400°F.
  2. Season: Toss tater tots with just enough oil to lightly coat. Season with salt, lemon pepper, and garlic powder; mix well.
  3. Air Fry: Cook @400°F for 10 minutes, tossing or shaking halfway through (around 5 minutes).
  4. Serve: Remove when crisp and evenly browned.

Chef's Notes

  • These are crispy, wonderful, and wildly addictive. Prepared in this way, they are a good substitute for canned biscuits in this breakfast casserole recipe
