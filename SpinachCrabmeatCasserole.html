Spinach and Crabmeat Casserole

Ingredients (12-16 servings)

  • 12 oz crabmeat, drained
  • 10 oz frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
  • 8 oz (1 C) Swiss cheese, shredded
  • 8 eggs
  • 2 C Half-and-half
  • 1 C breadcrumbs
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp pepper
  • ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  • 2 ribs celery
  • ½ C onion, chopped
  • ¼ C red pepper, chopped
  • 3 medium mushrooms, chopped
  • 2 tbs butter

Ingredients (6-8 servings)

  • 6 oz crabmeat, drained
  • 10 oz frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 C Half-and-half
  • ½ C breadcrumbs
  • 4 oz (½ C) Swiss cheese, shredded
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp pepper
  • ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 ribs celery
  • ½ C onion, chopped
  • ¼ C red pepper, chopped
  • 3 medium mushrooms, chopped
  • 2 tbs butter

Directions

  1. Mix Base: In a large bowl, beat eggs and cream until smooth.
  2. Add Fillings: Stir in crab, spinach, breadcrumbs, cheese, salt, pepper, and nutmeg; set aside.
  3. Sauté Vegetables: In a skillet, sauté celery, onion, red pepper, and mushrooms in butter until tender.
  4. Combine: Add the sautéed vegetables to the crab-spinach mixture and stir gently to combine.
  5. Assemble: Transfer to a buttered 2½-qt shallow baking dish.
  6. Bake: Bake uncovered @375°F for 30-35 minutes, until a thermometer inserted in the center reads 160°F.
  7. Rest & Serve: Let stand 10 minutes before serving.
