Potato Pancakes (v)

Ingredients

  • 2 C cubed potatoes (~1 lb)
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ medium onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 tbs potato starch
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Scallions (optional)

Directions

  1. Mix: Place all ingredients in a food processor and purée until smooth.
  2. Fry: Heat oil in a frying pan. Add about ¼ C of the potato mixture per portion and cook like pancakes until golden, about 2-3 minutes per side.
  3. Cool: Transfer to a wire rack to cool slightly.
  4. Serve: Serve with caviar, sour cream, and rinsed red onions.
