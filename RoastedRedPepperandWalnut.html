Roasted Red Pepper and Walnut Spread

Ingredients

  • 3 large (~1¼ lb) red bell peppers
  • 2 tbs olive oil
  • ¾ C chopped white onion
  • 2 tsp minced garlic
  • ½ C walnut pieces, lightly toasted
  • 3 tbs chopped basil or other favorite leafy herb such as cilantro
  • 1 slice white sandwich bread, crusts removed
  • Fresh lemon juice or white balsamic vinegar
  • Salt and freshly ground pepper

Directions

  1. Char Peppers: Char peppers over a gas flame or under a hot broiler until blackened all over. Transfer to a bowl, cover with plastic wrap, and let steam 10 minutes.
  2. Peel & Prep: Scrape off charred skins (do not wash). Remove and discard stems and seeds; set aside.
  3. Sauté Aromatics: In a sauté pan, heat oil and cook onion and garlic until soft but not browned.
  4. Process: Add the onion mixture, roasted red peppers, and remaining ingredients to a food processor. Process until mostly smooth with a little texture remaining.
  5. Season & Store: Season to taste with lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Store covered in the refrigerator up to 5 days.
