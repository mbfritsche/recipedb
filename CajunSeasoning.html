Cajun Seasoning

Ingredients

  • 4 tbs salt
  • 4 tbs onion powder
  • 4 tbs garlic powder
  • 2 tbs dried oregano leaves
  • 2 tbs dried sweet basil
  • 1 tbs dried thyme leaves
  • 1 tbs black pepper
  • 1 tbs white pepper
  • 1 tbs cayenne pepper
  • 5 tbs sweet paprika

Directions

  1. Combine in food processor and pulse until well-blended, or mix thoroughly in a large bowl. The recipe doubles or triples well.

Chef's Notes

  • If you don't have the time or the inclination to make your own, I use this one at home - it's very good.
