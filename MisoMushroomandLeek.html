Miso Mushroom and Leek Pasta

Ingredients

  • 2 tbs olive oil
  • 2 medium leeks, trimmed (~2 cups)
  • 1½ lbs mushrooms (cremini, shittake, oyster, etc), stemmed and sliced
  • 1 lb short pasta (ziti, cavatappi)
  • 2 tbs white miso
  • ¾ C grated parmesan
  • 1 tbs sherry or red wine vinegar
  • 1 tbs parsley, chopped

Directions

  1. Cook Pasta: Cook pasta al dente and reserve 1 cup pasta water.
  2. Sauté Leeks: Heat oil in a skillet and sauté leeks until softened, ~5 minutes.
  3. Brown Mushrooms: Add mushrooms, season with salt, and sauté until well browned, ~10 minutes.
  4. Build Sauce: Stir miso into the reserved pasta water and add to the mushrooms along with the pasta and cheese.
  5. Finish: Toss gently to coat. Garnish with parsley and Parmesan, then serve.
