Pancakes (Alton Brown)

Ingredients

Mix
  • 30 g sugar
  • 12 g baking powder
  • 9 g baking soda
  • 9 g salt
  • 840 g all-purpose flour
Pancakes
  • 50 g neutral oil
  • 2 eggs
  • 480 g buttermilk (2 C)
  • 280 g pancake mix

Directions

  1. Mix Batter: Emulsify oil, eggs, and buttermilk, then add to the pancake mix.
  2. Fold & Rest: Gently fold no more than 12 strokes with a rubber spatula. Let batter rest while griddle heats to 350°F.
  3. Cook: Pour ¼ C batter onto the hot griddle. Cook undisturbed 3-4 minutes, until edge bubbles set.
  4. Flip & Finish: Flip and cook 2 minutes more. Remove to a plate and top with butter.

Chef's Notes

  • To make buttermilk, add 1 tbs vinegar to enough milk to make 1 cup. This doubles easily for larger quantities.
  • This Alton Brown recipe is an alternative to the basic pancake recipe that uses a pre-made dry mix, making prep faster and simpler at cooking time
