Chicken Salt

Ingredients

  • 6 tbs salt
  • 3 tsp chicken stock
  • 3 tsp garlic powder
  • 3 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp white pepper
  • 1 tsp celery seed

Directions

  1. Mix ingredients together and store in an airtight container

Chef's Notes

  • Chicken salt is a standard seasoning for chips and other fried foods in Australia and other countries - a good pantry staple
