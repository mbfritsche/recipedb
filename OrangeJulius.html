Orange Julius

Ingredients

  • 1 12-oz can orange juice concentrate
  • 240 ml whole milk
  • 70 g sugar
  • 10 g vanilla extract
  • 325 g ice
  • 120-180 ml cold water

Directions

  1. Blend: In a blender, combine orange juice concentrate, milk, sugar, vanilla, ice, and a small amount of water.
  2. Adjust Texture: Blend until smooth, adding more water as needed until the desired consistency is reached.
  3. Finish: Blend on high until smooth and frothy.
  4. Serve: Divide among glasses and serve immediately.

Chef's Notes

  • This is an Orange Julius copycat recipe, a staple of the 1970s mall scene
