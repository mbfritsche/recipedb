1 1/4 C minced celery
1 bunch shallots, finely minced
1 green pepper, minced
3 C cooked crawfish tails + fat
2 cloves garlic, minced
1/2 tsp seafood seasoning
1 bay leaf
3 C cooked rice
1 1/4 C water
1 can golden mushroom soup
3 hard-boiled egg yolks
3 slices of bread, well buttered
salt and pepper to taste
- sautee celery, shallots and pepper, 5 min
- add crawfish tails + fat,garlic, seafood seasoning and bay leaf and sautee another 5 min
Add worcestershire and tabasco, salt and pepper and mix well
Add rice, water, and soup and pour into 2-qt buttered casserole
Grate egg yolks over top
Remove crusts from bread and cut into triangles
Arrange in a circle on top, sprinkle with paprika and bake
at 350 for 30 minutes
