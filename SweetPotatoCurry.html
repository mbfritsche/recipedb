Sweet Potato Curry (V)

Ingredients

  • 3 tbs vegetable oil
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 cloves
  • 4 dried chilies, whole
  • 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1" fresh ginger, peeled and julienned
  • 2 lbs sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into 1" cubes
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 1 stalk lemongrass, smashed and tied into a tight bundle
  • 14 oz light coconut milk
  • 1 tbs Thai fish sauce
  • Salt, to taste
  • Juice of ½ a lime
  • 2 tbs fresh cilantro, roughly chopped

Directions

  1. Toast Aromatics: Heat oil in a wok or large sauté pan over medium-high heat. Add cinnamon stick, cloves, and chilies and cook until fragrant. Add garlic and ginger and cook until the garlic is lightly golden.
  2. Cook Vegetables: Add sweet potatoes and onions and stir-fry until the onions soften and begin to brown.
  3. Simmer: Add lemongrass, coconut milk, and fish sauce or salt. Cover and simmer until the sweet potatoes are tender, about 20 minutes.
  4. Finish: Remove and discard the whole spices and lemongrass. Stir in lime juice, taste, and adjust seasoning as needed. Stir in cilantro.
  5. Serve: Let sit briefly, then serve with rice.
