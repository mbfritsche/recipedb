Beef and Chicken Canapés

Ingredients

  • 1 C shelled walnuts
  • 1 tbs bread crumbs
  • ½ lb chicken thighs, sliced
  • ¼ lb beef, thinly sliced
  • 1 C white sesame seeds
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 1 tbs water
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tbs cornstarch
  • 2 tbs flour
  • Salt and white pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Prep Nuts: Blanch walnuts, peel inner skins, coarsely chop while still damp, and mix with breadcrumbs.
  2. Make Batter: Whisk egg yolks, water, salt, sugar, cornstarch, and flour until smooth. Divide batter into two bowls.
  3. Season Meat: Slice chicken and season with salt and white pepper. Cut beef into small pieces and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Coat:
    • Dip chicken in first batter, then coat with walnut‑breadcrumb mixture.
    • Dip beef in second batter, then coat with sesame seeds.
  5. Fry: Deep‑fry chicken and beef in 325°F oil until crisp.
  6. Finish: Drain briefly on a wire rack, cut into bite-size pieces, and serve.

Chef's Notes

  • To get a really crisp crust, increase the temperature of the oil to 400°F and place the beef and chicken back in the oil for a couple of minutes.
