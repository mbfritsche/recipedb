Ragu Bolognese

Ingredients

  • 2 large carrots, peeled and diced
  • 2 stalks of celery, diced
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 6-8 oz thick cut bacon, cut into lardons (about 6 slices)
  • 1 C wine
  • 1½ lbs ground beef
  • 1½ lbs ground pork
  • 2 cans San Marzano peeled tomatoes (28-oz can), crushed
  • 12 oz evaporated milk (one small can)
  • 1 tbs anchovy paste
  • 1½ tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 3 branches fresh basil
  • 2 tbs parsley
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • ½ tsp oregano
  • 1 tbs Balsamic vinegar

Directions

  1. Render Bacon: In a large enameled steel stockpot over medium heat, render lardons until golden and crispy. Remove to paper towels.
  2. Sauté Mirepoix: Add mirepoix to the pot and sauté until soft.
  3. Brown Meat: Add meat in thirds, browning each batch completely to allow moisture to evaporate and build a deep fond.
  4. Deglaze: Return bacon to the pot. Add wine and scrape the bottom with a wooden spoon. Cook until the wine is completely reduced.
  5. Build Sauce: Add crushed tomatoes, milk, anchovy paste, herbs, and seasonings. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a bare simmer.
  6. Simmer: Cook 3½-4 hours, stirring every 30 minutes.
  7. Finish: During the final 30 minutes, adjust seasoning and finish with 1 tbs balsamic vinegar.

Chef's Notes

  • The finished sauce will have a loose oatmeal-like consistency and be deeply flavorful.
  • Ideal for spaghetti with meat sauce, lasagna, or baked ziti.
  • Freezes well for future use..
