Khao Kha Moo

Ingredients

Pork Stew
  • 1 pork leg
  • 1 pork foot (optional)
  • 1 tbs Golden Mountain Sauce
  • 3-4 tbs soy sauce
  • 3-4 tbs oyster sauce
  • 2 tbs dark soy sauce
  • 75 g palm sugar or rock sugar
  • 6-8 cilantro roots (see Notes)
  • 5 cloves garlic
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 2 star anise
  • 1 tsp Szechuan peppercorns
  • ½ tsp white peppercorns, cracked
  • 10 slices galangal
  • 2 shiitake mushrooms
  • 4-6 medium boiled eggs (boil for 8 minutes)
  • Pickled mustard greens (see Notes)
  • 200 g dark leafy greens, blanched or steamed (optional)
  • Jasmine rice for serving
Chili Vinegar
  • 2 spur chilies (see Notes)
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • ½ tsp sugar
  • Pinch of salt
  • ¼ - ½ C white vinegar, as needed

Directions

Pork Stew
  1. Prep Pork: Remove any remaining hair from the pork leg, then wash and dry thoroughly.
  2. Optional Sear: Brown the pork leg on all sides in a wok for additional color and flavor.
  3. Start Braise: Place pork leg and foot in a large stock pot and cover with water. Add sauces and palm sugar, then simmer for about 45 minutes.
  4. Add Aromatics: Skim any foam from the surface. Add cinnamon, star anise, peppercorns, shiitake mushrooms, cilantro roots, garlic, and galangal. Cover and simmer until the meat is fork‑tender, about 2½ hours.
  5. Add Eggs & Greens: About 20 minutes before the pork is finished, add eggs and pickled mustard greens and continue simmering.
  6. Finish Sauce: Remove pork, eggs, and mustard greens. Strain out aromatics and spices. Adjust seasoning and color as desired.
  7. Serve: Slice or pull the pork into bite‑size pieces. Serve over jasmine rice with eggs, pickled mustard greens, and blanched greens. Spoon sauce generously over the top and add chili vinegar if desired
Chili Vinegar
  1. Blend: Place all ingredients in a blender, starting with ¼ cup vinegar.
  2. Adjust: Blend until smooth, adding more vinegar as needed to achieve the desired consistency.
  3. Serve: Use immediately or refrigerate until ready to serve.

Chef's Notes

  • Use about ½ C roughly chopped cilantro stems if cilantro roots are unavailable
  • Use medium-heat chilies such as jalapeños, serranos, or Fresnos. For a spicier version, use Thai chilies or habaneros
  • Pigeon Brand is the traditional favorite and offers a number of other pickled vegetable alternatives, such as cucumber, mixed vegetables, or carrots
