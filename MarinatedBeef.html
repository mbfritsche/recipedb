Marinated Beef

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb sirloin tip
  • 12 oz pineapple chunks
  • ¼ C dry sherry
  • 2 tbs light soy sauce
  • ¼ C dark soy sauce
  • ¼ C hoisin sauce
  • ¼ C unsweetened pineapple juice
  • 2½ tbs sugar
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • ½ tsp white pepper
  • 2 tbs ginger, grated

Directions

  1. Marinate: Combine marinade ingredients and mix well. Add meat and coat thoroughly.
  2. Assemble: Thread meat onto skewers, alternating with pieces of pineapple.
  3. Chill: Cover and refrigerate to marinate overnight.
  4. Cook: Broil until cooked through, about 10 minutes, or grill over direct heat until done.
  5. Serve: Serve hot directly from the skewers.
