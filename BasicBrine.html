Basic Brine

Ingredients

  • 16 cups water (One gallon)
  • 150 g cup kosher salt
  • 135 g white sugar
  • 2 heads garlic, crushed and chopped
  • 4 g dried sage, or to taste
  • 5 g dried basil, or to taste
  • 1 tbs whole black peppercorns
  • 1 large bay leaf

Directions

  1. Bring water to a boil and add all ingredients, making sure that the sugar and salt are fully dissolved.
  2. Remove from heat and cool for 2 hours before using. Chicken should brine for 8-12 hours and turkey 12-24.
