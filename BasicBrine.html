Basic Brine

Ingredients

  • 16 C water
  • 150 g cup kosher salt
  • 135 g white sugar
  • 2 heads garlic, crushed and chopped
  • 4 g dried sage
  • 5 g dried basil
  • 1 tbs whole black peppercorns
  • 1 large bay leaf

Directions

  1. Make Brine: Bring water to a boil and add all ingredients, stirring until sugar and salt are fully dissolved.
  2. Cool: Remove from heat and let cool 2 hours before using.
  3. Brine: Submerge poultry and refrigerate - 8-12 hours for chicken, 12-24 hours for turkey.
