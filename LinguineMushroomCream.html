Linguine in Mushroom Cream Sauce (v)

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 6 large garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • ¼ C olive oil
  • 300g whipping cream
  • 1 lb linguine
  • 225g grated Parmesan cheese, divided
  • 60g chopped fresh parsley, divided
  • ½ tsp thyme
  • 2 sprigs fresh basil
  • 1 tsp balsamic vinegar
  • Dash ground nutmeg
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Sauté Mushrooms: Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add mushrooms, garlic, and nutmeg; sauté ~10 minutes until mushrooms are browned and tender.
  2. Finish Sauce: Season with salt and pepper, stir in cream, and remove from heat.
  3. Cook Pasta: Boil pasta in salted water until al dente. Drain, reserving ½ C pasta water.
  4. Combine: Return pasta to the pot. Add mushroom mixture, cheese, parsley, thyme, vinegar, basil, and reserved pasta water.
  5. Toss: Toss over medium-high heat until the sauce coats the pasta evenly.
  6. Serve: Season to taste with salt and pepper, transfer to a serving bowl, and garnish with parsley.

Chef's Notes

  • This can be made vegetarian by using a non-dairy substitute for the whipping cream and the Parmesan cheese
