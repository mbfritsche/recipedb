Tofu Meatballs

Ingredients

  • 1 tbs ground flaxseed
  • 2½ tbs water
  • 1 tbs oil
  • ½ onion, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • ½ C walnuts
  • 14 oz firm tofu
  • 1 C breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbs corn starch
  • 2 tbs vegan Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
  • 1 tbs nutritional yeast

Directions

  1. Make Flax Egg: Stir ground flaxseed and water until well combined; set aside to thicken.
  2. Cook Aromatics: Sauté diced onion in oil until soft and translucent, then add garlic and cook 2 minutes more. Remove from heat and let cool.
  3. Prep Nuts: Grind walnuts in a food processor until finely powdered.
  4. Prep Tofu: Press tofu to remove excess moisture, then break into small pieces by hand.
  5. Mix: Combine tofu, ground walnuts, cooked onion mixture, flax egg, and remaining ingredients until evenly mixed.
  6. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 350°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  7. Shape: Form 1-2 tbs portions into balls and place on the prepared tray.
  8. Bake: Lightly spray with oil and bake 25-30 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and slightly firm.
  9. Serve: Serve hot with pasta and sauce, or with vegan BBQ sauce on the side.

Chef's Notes

  • This is a vegan recipe that makes approximately 28 meatballs.
  • Meatballs are done when they feel slightly firm when gently pressed.
  • Excellent tossed in marinara sauce with your favorite pasta.
  • Remove excess water by pressing on an incline with a weighted plate or squeezing between paper towels.
  • Crumble tofu into pieces resembling ground meat or breadcrumbs.
  • Meatballs can also be pan-fried in a little oil for a crispier exterior.
