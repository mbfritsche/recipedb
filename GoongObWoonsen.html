Goong Ob Woonsen (Garlic Pepper Shrimp with Glass Noodles)

Ingredients

  • 80 g dry glass noodles
  • 8-10 medium shrimp,
  • 120 mL chicken stock or water
  • 2 tbs soy sauce
  • 2 tbs oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp black soy sauce or dark soy sauce
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 15 slices ginger
  • ½ tsp black peppercorns
  • ½ tsp white peppercorns
  • 6 cloves garlic
  • 8 cilantro stems or 3 cilantro roots
  • 2 tbs vegetable oil
  • 1 green onion, sliced on a bias, for garnish

Directions

  1. Soak Noodles: Soak glass noodles in room‑temperature water for 10 minutes until softened, then drain. Cut with scissors if desired.
  2. Prep Shrimp: Trim shrimp, open shells along the back, remove veins, rinse, and set aside.
  3. Mix Sauce: Combine sauce ingredients and stir until sugar dissolves. Toss noodles and shrimp with the sauce and let marinate briefly.
  4. Make Aromatic Base: Grind peppercorns, then crush and bruise garlic and cilantro stems or roots. Add to a heavy pot with ginger and oil.
  5. Cook Aromatics: Heat the pot until fragrant and sizzling.
  6. Steam: Add noodles, shrimp, and all of the sauce. Arrange shrimp on top, cover, and cook until the sauce begins boiling. Reduce heat and cook for 3 minutes.
  7. Finish: Stir noodles to redistribute the sauce, cover, and cook another 2-3 minutes until shrimp and noodles are done and most liquid has been absorbed. Add a splash of water or stock if needed.
  8. Rest: Transfer to a serving bowl, top with green onions or Chinese celery leaves, cover, and let stand for a minute.
  9. Serve: Serve hot with jasmine rice.

Chef's Notes

  • Glass noodles are sometimes labeled bean vermicelli or bean threads
  • The best glass noodles are Kaset and Tonson
  • For best results, use head-on shell-on shrimp if they are available
