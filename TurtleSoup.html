Turtle Soup

Ingredients

  • 1 lb turtle meat, cubed
  • 1 C celery, minced
  • 2 medium onions, minced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • ½ tsp thyme
  • 1½ C tomato purée
  • 10 oz unsalted butter (2½ sticks)
  • ¾ C all-purpose flour
  • 32 oz beef stock
  • ½ C lemon juice
  • 5 hard-boiled eggs, finely chopped
  • 1 tbs minced parsley
  • 6 tsp dry sherry
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Make Roux: Melt 8 oz butter in a heavy saucepan over medium heat. Add flour and cook, stirring frequently, until a light brown roux forms. Remove from heat and set aside.
  2. Brown Meat: In a 5-qt saucepan, melt remaining butter. Add turtle meat and cook over high heat until browned.
  3. Add Vegetables: Stir in celery, onions, garlic, and seasonings. Cook until vegetables are translucent.
  4. Build Base: Add tomato purée, lower heat, and simmer 10 minutes. Add stock and simmer 30 minutes.
  5. Thicken Soup: Stir in the roux and cook over low heat until smooth and thickened, stirring occasionally.
  6. Finish: Add lemon juice and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper. Remove from heat.
  7. Serve: Ladle soup into bowls. At the table, add 1 tsp sherry to each bowl and top with eggs and parsley. Serve immediately with French bread and butter.

Chef's Notes

  • If turtle bones are available, add them to the beef bones when making the stock for this dish.
  • This is a copycat recipe of the Turtle Soup at Commander's in New Orleans
