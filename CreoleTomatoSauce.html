Creole Tomato Sauce

Ingredients

  • 2 tbs oil
  • 4 oz onions, small dice
  • 4 oz celery, small dice
  • 2 oz green bell pepper, small dice
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 qt Tomato sauce
  • 1 bay leaf
  • pinch dried thyme leaves
  • Cajun seasoning to taste

Directions

  1. Sauté Vegetables: Sauté vegetables in oil until onions are translucent.
  2. Build Sauce: Add tomato sauce, bay leaf, thyme, and Creole seasoning. Bring to a boil.
  3. Simmer: Reduce heat immediately and simmer 15 minutes.
  4. Finish: Remove bay leaf, adjust seasoning to taste, and serve.
