Cheese Grits

Ingredients

  • 1 C grits
  • 2 C vegetable stock
  • 1 C milk
  • 1 C half and half
  • 4-8 oz cheese

Directions

  1. Start Grits: Bring stock to a boil and whisk in the grits.
  2. Add Dairy: As the grits thicken, gradually add the milk mixture, stirring continuously.
  3. Add Cheese: When about ¾ of the milk mixture is incorporated, stir in the cheese until melted.
  4. Finish: Add remaining milk mixture and continue stirring to prevent sticking. Grits should be rich and creamy, not overly thick.

Chef's Notes

  • You can use pepper jack or a cheddar blend.
  • Smoked Cajun cheese (available at Aldi and Trader Joe's) also works very well and adds a nice smoky depth.
