Japanese Plum Vinaigrette

Ingredients

  • 2 tbs Suehiro Vinegar (see Notes)
  • 2 tbs avocado oil
  • 2 tbs honey
  • 1 tbs soy sauce
  • ½ tbs sesame oil
  • ½ tbs plum vinegar (see Notes)
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp grated garlic
  • Salt & black pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Mix Base: In a mixing bowl, whisk together rice vinegar, plum vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, honey, grated garlic, and grated ginger until blended.
  2. Finish: Whisk in avocado oil until fully emulsified
