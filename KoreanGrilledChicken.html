Korean Grilled Chicken

Ingredients

  • 1 broiler or fryer chicken (cut into pieces)
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 1 clove garlic, crushed
  • ¼ tsp pepper
  • ¼ tsp ground ginger
  • ¼ C sesame seeds
  • ¼ C corn oil
  • ¼ C soy sauce
  • ¼ C dark corn syrup (Karo)

Directions

  1. Make Marinade: Mix all ingredients except the chicken until well combined.
  2. Marinate Chicken: Add chicken and turn to coat evenly. Cover and refrigerate, turning once, at least 3 hours.
  3. Grill: Grill over low coals, turning and basting frequently, ~50 minutes, until breasts reach 165°F and thighs reach 175°F.
  4. Serve: Remove from grill and serve hot.
