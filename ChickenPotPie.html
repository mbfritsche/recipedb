Chicken Pot Pie

Ingredients

  • 250 g cut-up cooked chicken
  • 250 g frozen mixed vegetables, thawed
  • 1 can condensed cream of chicken soup
  • 130 g Bisquick
  • 120 ml milk
  • 1 egg

Directions

  1. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Assemble Filling: In an ungreased 9" pie plate, combine vegetables, chicken, and soup.
  3. Add Topping: Mix remaining ingredients until blended and pour over the chicken and vegetables.
  4. Bake: Bake about 30 minutes, until golden brown and set.
