Classic Cole Slaw

Ingredients

  • ½ head green cabbage, finely shredded
  • ¼ head red cabbage, finely shredded
  • 3 medium carrots, grated
  • ¾ C mayonnaise
  • 2 tbs apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tbs Dijon mustard
  • ¼ C sour cream
  • 1 tbs honey
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp celery salt
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Directions

  1. Make Dressing: In a large bowl, beat together mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar, sour cream, honey, and dry seasonings until smooth.
  2. Add Vegetables: Stir in cabbage and carrots until evenly coated.
  3. Chill & Serve: Cover and refrigerate 2 hours, then mix again, adjust seasoning, and serve.
