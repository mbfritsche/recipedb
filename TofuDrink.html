Tofu Drink

Ingredients

  • 1 pkg Dried soybean sheets
  • 1 C rock sugar
  • 2 C water
  • 3" knob of ginger, smashed
  • 1 egg, beaten (Optional)

Directions

  1. Prep Tofu: Soak dry tofu skin in water 10-15 minutes or until softened, then drain.
  2. Make Broth: Bring water and smashed ginger to a boil over medium heat and simmer for 10-15 minutes.
  3. Finish: Reduce heat and add tofu. Gently stir in beaten egg.
  4. Serve: Turn off heat and serve.
