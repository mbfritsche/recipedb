Mousseline Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 C heavy cream
  • 1 qt Hollandaise sauce

Directions

  1. Fold Sauce: Whip heavy cream to stiff peaks, then gently fold into Hollandaise until combined.
  2. Ratio Guide: For other quantities, maintain a 1:4 ratio of whipped cream to Hollandaise.

Chef's Notes

  • The sauce can be further flavored with fresh herbs, citrus juices, or other aromatics to taste.
