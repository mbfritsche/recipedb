Mousseline Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 C heavy cream
  • 1 qt Hollandaise sauce

Directions

  1. Whip heavy cream until it forms stiff peaks and fold in Hollandaise.
  2. For other quantities, maintain a 1:4 ratio of whipped cream to Hollandaise.

Chef's Notes

  • Can be flavored further with herbs, juices, etc.
