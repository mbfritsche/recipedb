Neck Bones

Ingredients

  • 3-4 lbs pork neck bones
  • 2 package frozen greens (turnips or collards)
  • 1 can Rotel tomatoes
  • 3-4 onions
  • 3 stalks celery
  • 2 carrots
  • 3 jalapenos
  • 2 bell pepper
  • 1-2 tbs garlic
  • Pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, Cajun seasoning, Seasoned Salt, to taste
  • 4-5 C vegetable stock (with vegetable scraps added)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ¼ C apple cider vinegar
  • ½ C all-purpose flour
  • 2-3 tbs Colman's Dry mustard
  • Bacon grease

Directions

  1. Clean & Soak: Rinse neck bones thoroughly under cold water, drain, and place in a large bowl. Cover with apple cider vinegar and soak 30-45 minutes. Drain and rinse again.
  2. Season: Spread neck bones on a sheet pan and season with pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, Cajun seasoning, and seasoned salt.
  3. Brown: Melt 2-3 tbs bacon grease in a large pot over medium-high. Brown neck bones in batches; remove to a colander.
  4. Cook Vegetables: Add diced vegetables to the pot and cook until softened. Deglaze with vegetable stock or white wine if dry. Stir in Rotel tomatoes about halfway through cooking.
  5. Build Base: Once most liquid has evaporated and vegetables begin to fry, add the flour‑mustard mixture. Stir well to eliminate raw flour taste.
  6. Simmer: Add stock, bring to a boil, then return neck bones and any collected juices to the pot. Reduce to a simmer, cover, and cook ~2 hours.
  7. Uncover: Remove lid and continue cooking, stirring occasionally and scraping the bottom of the pot.
  8. Finish: Add greens and cook 10-15 minutes more.
  9. Serve: Serve over rice with French bread and hot sauce.

Chef's Notes

  • For more spice, add the ribs and seeds of the jalapenos to the stock
