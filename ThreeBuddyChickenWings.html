Three Buddy Chicken Wings

Ingredients

  • 2-3 lbs chicken wings (about 12)
  • 8 garlic cloves
  • 20 cilantro stems
  • 2 tsp white peppercorns
  • 1 tbs soy sauce
  • 1 tbs fish sauce
  • 1 ½ tsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbs water
  • ½ tsp sugar
  • 2 tbs Mr Hung Seasoned Batter mix

Directions

  1. Grind Spices: Grind white peppercorns and garlic together until well combined.
  2. Add Herbs: Add cilantro stems and process until fully blended - these are the Three Buddies.
  3. Marinade Prep:Combine soy sauce, oyster sauce, fish sauce, water, and sugar, stirring until the sugar is fully dissolved
  4. Wing Prep & Marinate: Cut wings into three sections. Toss with the Three Buddies and the marinade. Marinate at least 30 minutes at room temperature or refrigerated for longer.
  5. Coat: Heat 4 C peanut oil in a wok or deep pot. Toss wings Mr Hung until evenly coated.
  6. Fry: Fry wings in small batches for about 10 minutes, until golden and crisp.
  7. Drain & Serve: Drain on a wire rack and serve hot.
