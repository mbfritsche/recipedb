Broccoli Banchan (Beurokolli-namul) (V)

Ingredients

  • 1 head of broccoli, cut into florets
  • 1 tbs sesame oil
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • 2 cloves minced garlic
  • Toasted sesame seeds and gochugaru, to taste

Directions

  1. Blanch: Cook broccoli in boiling water for 1½ minutes, then transfer immediately to an ice bath. Season: Drain well and toss with sesame oil, salt, garlic, sesame seeds, and chili flakes. Serve: Serve as a side dish with rice.

Chef's Notes

  • For softer broccoli, blanch for an additional minute
