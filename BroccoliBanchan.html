Broccoli Banchan

Broccoli Banchan (Beurokolli-namul) (V)

Ingredients

  • 1 head of broccoli, cut into florets
  • 1 tbs sesame oil
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp gochugaru Lil>1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 2 cloves minced garlic

Directions

  1. Seasoning Mix: Combine the kosher salt, garlic powder, and gochugaru and set aside.
  2. Blanch: Cook broccoli in boiling water for 1½ minutes, then transfer immediately to an ice bath.
  3. Season: Drain well and toss with sesame oil, the seasoning mix, and sesame seeds.
  4. Serve: Serve as a side dish with rice.

Chef's Notes

  • Cooking Tip: For softer broccoli, increase the blanching time by about 1 additional minute.
  • Technique Note: Raw minced garlic is the traditional choice for this dish. However, a seasoning blend made with garlic powder and gochugaru provides more even flavor distribution while preserving the intended flavor balance.
  • Convenience: Keeping the seasoning blend prepared in advance makes it easier to adjust the flavor profile to suit individual preferences or local tastes.
