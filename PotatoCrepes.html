Potato Crepes

Ingredients

  • 500 g potatoes, peeled
  • Milk
  • 3 tbs flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 4 egg whites
  • 2 tbs heavy cream
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Clarified butter

Directions

  1. Cook Potatoes: Boil potatoes in salted water until tender, then mash with a small amount of milk and let cool.
  2. Mix: Add flour, whole eggs, egg whites, and heavy cream. Mix until the batter is smooth with a consistency between pastry cream and custard.
  3. Cook: Heat a flat pan over high heat and add clarified butter. Spoon batter into the pan and cook until set and lightly browned.
  4. Flip & Finish: Turn with a spatula and cook the other side. Transfer to a wire rack to drain slightly.
  5. Serve: Serve warm.
