Potato Crepes

Ingredients

  • 500 g potatoes, peeled
  • Milk
  • 3 tbs flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 4 egg whites
  • 2 tbs heavy cream
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Clarified butter

Directions

  1. Cook the potatoes in salted water, then mash them with some milk (~50ml) and let cool.
  2. To the potato mixture, add the flour, then incorporate whole eggs, egg whites and heavy cream. Mix until the batter has a consistency between pastry cream and custard.
  3. Place a flat pan over high heat, then add clarified butter.
  4. When it's very hot, pour a tablespoon of the batter.
  5. Flip with a spatula and drain on a wire rack.
