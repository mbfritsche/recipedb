The Dugas Cheesecake

Ingredients

  • 4 8oz blocks cream cheese
  • 1½ C sugar
  • 1 pint sour cream
  • 5 extra large eggs (6 large)
  • 2 tbs flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 2 tbs melted butter
  • Juice of ½ lemon (optional)
Crust
  • 2 tbs sugar
  • 1 package graham crackers (12 or so)
  • 2 tbs melted butter
  • dash cinnamon

Directions

  1. Prep Pan: Line the bottom of a springform pan with waxed paper or foil; butter and flour the pan.
  2. Make Crust: Crush crackers and mix with sugar, butter, and cinnamon. Press firmly into the bottom of the pan.
  3. Prepare Filling: Let sour cream and cream cheese come to room temperature. Mix flour with sugar.
  4. Beat Cheesecake: Beat cream cheese until fluffy. Gradually add sugar/flour mixture, then beat in eggs one at a time. Add vanilla, then fold in sour cream and butter.
  5. Bake: Pour batter into pan. Bake at 500°F for 12 minutes, then reduce heat to 250°F and bake 1 hour.
  6. Cool: Turn off oven and leave cheesecake inside overnight to cool slowly.
  7. Chill: Remove and refrigerate before serving.
