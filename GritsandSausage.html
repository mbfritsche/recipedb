Grits and Sausage Casserole

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs bulk pork sausage, cooked and drained
  • 1 cup quick-cooking grits
  • ¾ tsp salt, divided
  • 2 C shredded cheddar cheese, divided
  • 3 C water
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1½ C whole milk
  • 2 tbs butter, melted
  • Pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Cook Grits: In a saucepan, bring water to a boil. Slowly whisk in grits and ½ tsp salt. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  2. Mix Base: Transfer cooked grits to a large bowl. Stir in sausage and 1½ C cheese.
  3. Add Eggs & Dairy: Beat eggs with milk and stir into the grits mixture. Add butter, pepper, and remaining salt; mix well.
  4. Assemble: Transfer mixture to a greased 13x9" baking dish.
  5. Bake: Bake uncovered @350°F for 1 hour, until a knife inserted in the center comes out clean.
  6. Finish: Sprinkle with remaining cheese and bake 15 minutes more, until melted.
  7. Rest & Serve: Let stand 5 minutes before cutting and serving.
