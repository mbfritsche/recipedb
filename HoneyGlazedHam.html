Honey-Glazed Ham

Ingredients

  • Pre-cooked ham
  • 1 C brown sugar
  • ¼ honey
  • 2 tbs apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp dry mustard

Directions

  1. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Make Glaze: Combine brown sugar, honey, mustard, and apple cider vinegar until well mixed. Microwave ~10 seconds if needed to help combine.
  3. Glaze Ham: Brush glaze over all sides of the ham and place uncovered in the oven.
  4. Bake: Bake 10-15 minutes per pound, until the ham is heated through.
