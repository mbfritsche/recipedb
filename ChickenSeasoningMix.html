Chicken Seasoning Mix

Ingredients

  • 2 tbs salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp white pepper
  • ½ tsp red pepper
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp onion powder
  • ¼ tsp ground bay leaves
  • ¼ tsp file powder
  • ¾ tsp sweet basil
  • ½ tsp paprika

Directions

  1. Mix all ingredients together and store in a glass jar. Use as needed in chicken recipes as a replacement for salt and pepper.
