Herbes de Provence

Ingredients

  • 2 tbs dried rosemary
  • 1 tbs fennel seed
  • 2 tbs dried savory
  • 2 tbs dried thyme
  • 2 tbs dried basil
  • 2 tbs dried marjoram
  • 2 tbs dried lavender flowers
  • 2 tbs dried Italian parsley
  • 1 tbs dried oregano
  • 1 tbs dried tarragon
  • 1 tsp bay powder

Directions

  1. Grind rosemary and fennel seed in a spice grinder; transfer to a mixing bowl.
  2. Stir savory, thyme, basil, marjoram, lavender, parsley, oregano, tarragon, and bay powder with the rosemary and fennel.
  3. Store in an air-tight container between uses.
