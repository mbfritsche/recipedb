New Orleans Spinach and Crabmeat Gratin

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lump crab meat
  • ½ lb cooked shrimp
  • 1 can (14 ounce size) artichoke hearts, drained
  • 20 ounces frozen chopped spinach, thawed and drained
  • 1 cup chopped onions
  • ¼ C chopped green onions
  • 6 tbs butter
  • 1 pint sour cream
  • ½ C grated Parmesan cheese
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • ¼ tsp Tabasco sauce

Directions

  1. Sauté Base: In a heavy skillet over medium heat, melt butter. Add onions and green onions and sauté until soft but not browned.
  2. Add Dairy & Greens: Stir in spinach, sour cream, and cheese. Reduce heat and simmer until heated through.
  3. Finish Filling: Add artichoke hearts, salt, pepper, Worcestershire sauce, and Tabasco. Simmer 2-3 minutes.
  4. Fold Seafood: Gently fold in crabmeat and shrimp.
  5. Bake: Transfer to a buttered 2-qt casserole and bake @350°F for 20-30 minutes, until hot and bubbly.

Chef's Notes

  • 8 oz sour cream, 8 oz half and half
  • 1 tbs Dijon mustard
  • ½ C parmesan + ½ C Swiss cheese
  • 4 oz mushrooms, added after the mirepoix
  • Marinate shrimp in salt, white pepper, soy sauce and Cajun seasoning
  • Cut the shrimp and artichokes into smaller pieces
  • 1 lb rotini pasta, cooked about half-way and baked with the casserole
  • Top with parmesan and plain bread crumbs
  • If using any other vegetables, add them with the artichokes
