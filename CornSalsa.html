Corn Salsa (V)

Ingredients

  • 16 oz frozen white corn kernels
  • 1 poblano pepper stemmed, roasted and chopped
  • ½ C fresh cilantro chopped
  • ½ red onion finely chopped
  • 1 jalapeno pepper finely diced
  • 2 tbs lime juice fresh or bottled
  • 1 tbs lemon juice fresh or bottled
  • Salt to taste

Directions

  1. Prep Corn: Cook corn per package directions, drain, and let cool.
  2. Roast Pepper: Char poblano over an open flame or under the broiler until skin is blackened; chop.
  3. Combine: In a large bowl, mix corn, poblano, cilantro, onion, jalapeño, lime juice, and lemon juice.
  4. Season & Serve: Toss to combine and season to taste.
