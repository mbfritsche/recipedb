Basic Bread

Ingredients

  • 410 g bread flour
  • 240 ml warm water
  • 70 g sugar
  • 7 g active dry yeast
  • 5 g salt
  • 9 g vegetable oil

Directions

  1. Proof Yeast: Stir sugar and yeast into warm water until foamy.
  2. Mix Dough: Stir in salt and oil, then add flour one cup at a time until a dough forms.
  3. Knead: Knead by hand or with a mixer 8-10 minutes until smooth.
  4. First Rise: Place in an oiled bowl, turn to coat, cover, and let rise about 1 hour until doubled.
  5. Shape: Punch down, knead briefly, divide in half, and shape into loaves.
  6. Second Rise: Place in oiled 9x5" loaf pans and let rise 30 minutes, until dough rises about 1 inch above the pan.
  7. Bake: Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes.
  8. Cool: Remove from pans and cool on a wire rack.
