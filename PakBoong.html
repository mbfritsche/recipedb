Pak Boong (v)

Ingredients

  • 1 large bunch Thai morning glory or water spinach
  • 1 tbs oyster sauce
  • 1 tbs soy bean paste
  • ½ tbs soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp white pepper
  • 2 Thai chilies
  • 3-6 garlic

Directions

  1. Make Paste: Crush garlic and chilies together using a mortar and pestle or grind briefly.
  2. Prep Vegetables: Cut morning-glory stems into 2" pieces; halve the leaves.
  3. Stir‑Fry: Fry the garlic-chili paste in hot oil until fragrant, taking care not to burn.
  4. Finish: Add all vegetables and sauce ingredients at once. Deglaze with a splash of stock and finish with a sizzling pour of fish sauce.
  5. Serve: Serve immediately

Chef's Notes

  • Substitute mushroom sauce for oyster sauce to make this dish vegan
  • You can use this recipe with spinach or kale.
  • Put all sauces and spices on top of the veg and put them all in at once with the stems on the bottom
