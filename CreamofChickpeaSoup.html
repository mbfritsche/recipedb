Cream of Chickpea Soup (v)

Ingredients

  • 45 oz chickpeas, drained and rinsed (3 cans)
  • 15 oz diced tomatoes, un-drained (1 can)
  • 1 tbs extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 red onion, finely chopped
  • 8 garlic cloves, minced
  • 4 C vegetable broth or water
  • 1 tsp minced fresh rosemary or ¼ tsp dried
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp ground black pepper
  • 2 tbs balsamic vinegar
  • 45 g grated fresh Parmesan cheese
  • 170 g fresh baby spinach, chopped, for additional greens

Directions

  1. Sauté Base: Heat olive oil in a large stockpot over medium heat. Add onion and garlic and cook ~10 minutes, stirring frequently, until softened.
  2. Simmer Soup: Stir in stock, tomatoes, chickpeas, rosemary, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil. If using spinach, add it now. Reduce heat and simmer 20 minutes.
  3. Blend: Purée the soup with an immersion blender. Stir in vinegar, bring briefly to a boil, then remove from heat.
  4. Serve: Ladle 1½ C into each of 6 bowls and sprinkle with 1 tbs Parmesan per serving.

Chef's Notes

  • This is a vegetarian recipe - replace or omit the Parmesan cheese to make it vegan
