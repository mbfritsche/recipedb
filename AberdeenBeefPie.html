Aberdeen Beef Pie

Ingredients

  • 2 lb very lean salt pork or bacon, cut into 2-inch cubes
  • ¼ lb sliced bacon, diced
  • 3 lb beef stew meat, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 1 C chopped onion
  • 1½ C halved fresh baby carrots
  • 6 tbs all-purpose flour
  • 1 C beef broth
  • 1 tbs Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 package (10 ounces) frozen peas
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 1 refrigerated pie crust
  • 1 large egg, lightly beaten, optional

Directions

  1. Combine the flour, broth and Worcestershire sauce until smooth and set aside
  2. In a Dutch oven, cook bacon over medium heat until crisp. Remove to a colander to drain.
  3. Brown the beef in drippings in batches, drain in a colander, and set aside.
  4. Add onion to the pan and sauté until crisp-tender.
  5. Add carrots, bacon, the browned beef, and the flour mixture
  6. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, cover, and and simmer until meat is tender, 1 to 1½ hours.
  7. Stir in peas and season with salt and pepper.
  8. Transfer to an ungreased 11-in. x 7-in. baking dish.
  9. On a lightly floured surface, roll out crust into a 12-in. x 8-in. rectangle.
  10. Cut slits in crust and place over the beef filling
  11. Trim, seal the edges, and brush with beaten egg.
  12. Bake at 375° until crust is golden and filling is bubbly, 35-40 minutes.
  13. Let stand for 15 minutes before serving. Refrigerate leftovers.
