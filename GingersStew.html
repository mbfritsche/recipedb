Ginger's Stew

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb stew meat, trimmed
  • ½ red onion, chopped
  • ½ white onion, chopped
  • 3 tbs garlic, chopped
  • 2 tbs basil
  • 4-5 potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 3 carrots, peeled and diced
  • 1 yellow squash, diced
  • 1 large container V8 juice
  • 1 large can tomatoes
  • 1 can spinach
  • 1 can green beans
  • 2 cans corn
  • To taste: Cajun seasoning, garlic powder, onion powder, celery salt, salt, black pepper, and MSG

Directions

  1. Sauté Base: Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add garlic and onions and sauté until translucent.
  2. Add Tomatoes: Stir in tomatoes and season with salt, pepper, and Cajun seasoning. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer 30 minutes.
  3. Add Vegetables: Stir in basil, potatoes, and carrots. Simmer 20 minutes.
  4. Add Liquid: Add ½ container V8 juice and 1 can water (using the tomato can).
  5. Season: Add garlic powder, onion powder, and celery salt to taste. Simmer 30 minutes.
  6. Finish: Add spinach, green beans, corn, and diced squash. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer 1½ hours.
  7. Serve: Serve hot with crusty French bread and a hearty red wine.

Chef's Notes
