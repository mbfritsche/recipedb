Corn Dog Casserole

Ingredients

  • 2 C thinly sliced celery
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1½ C sliced green onions
  • 1½ lbs hot dogs
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1½ C 2% milk
  • 2 tsp rubbed sage
  • ¼ tsp pepper
  • 2 packages (8½ oz each) cornbread/muffin mix
  • 2 C shredded sharp cheddar cheese, divided (1 lb)

Directions

  1. Sauté Veg: Sauté celery in butter 5 minutes. Add onions and cook 5 minutes more until tender; transfer to a large bowl.
  2. Prep Oven & Hot Dogs: Preheat oven to 400°F. Slice hot dogs into ½" pieces. In the same skillet, sauté hot dogs ~5 minutes until lightly browned.
  3. Combine: Add hot dogs to vegetables, reserving 1 cup. In a bowl, whisk eggs, milk, sage, and pepper; stir into remaining hot dog mixture.
  4. Assemble: Mix in cornbread mixes and 1½ C cheese. Spread into a buttered 3-qt baking dish. Top with reserved hot dogs and remaining cheese.
  5. Bake: Bake uncovered ~30 minutes, until golden brown.
