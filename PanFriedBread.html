Pan-Fried Bread

Ingredients

  • 2 C cornmeal
  • 1 C buttermilk
  • 1 C water
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 C vegetable oil

Directions

  1. In large bowl combine all ingredients except the oil.
  2. In frying pan heat half the oil to 350° degrees.
  3. Using a large soup spoon or ladle, put the batter in the hot oil and fry until done, about 1 - 2 minutes on each side. Add more oil as needed.
  4. Drain dough on a wire rack and serve hot.
