Pan-Fried Bread

Ingredients

  • 2 C cornmeal
  • 1 C buttermilk
  • 1 C water
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 C vegetable oil

Directions

  1. Mix Batter: In a large bowl, combine all ingredients except the oil and mix until smooth.
  2. Heat Oil: Heat ½ of the oil in a frying pan to 350°F, adding more as needed while cooking.
  3. Fry: Spoon batter into the hot oil using a soup spoon or ladle. Fry 1-2 minutes per side, until cooked through and golden.
  4. Drain & Serve: Transfer to a wire rack to drain and serve hot.
