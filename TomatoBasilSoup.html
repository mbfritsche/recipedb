Tomato Basil Soup (v)

Ingredients

  • 2 28-oz cans San Marzano tomatoes
  • 3 large carrots, peeled and chopped
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 1 stalk celery, chopped
  • 2 tbs tomato paste
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 32 oz vegetable stock
  • 3 tbs olive oil
  • 3 tbs butter
  • ¼ tsp crushed red pepper flakes
  • 2 tsp dried basil
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 1 C whipping cream (Optional)
  • Fresh basil leaves, thinly sliced

Directions

  1. Heat Base: In a Dutch oven over medium heat, heat oil, butter, and red pepper flakes until butter is melted.
  2. Cook Vegetables: Add carrots, onion, celery, and tomato paste. Cook uncovered 8-10 minutes, stirring frequently, until vegetables are soft.
  3. Add Aromatics: Stir in garlic and dried basil; cook ~1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Simmer Soup: Add tomatoes, vegetable stock, sugar, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer uncovered 20-25 minutes.
  5. Blend: Remove from heat and purée until smooth using an immersion blender or blender.
  6. Finish & Serve: Stir in heavy cream if desired. Garnish with fresh basil and serve with garlic croutons.

Chef's Notes

  • This is a vegetarian recipe, and vegan if the whipping cream is omitted
  • Pairs well with grilled cheese sandwiches to make an American comfort food classic.
