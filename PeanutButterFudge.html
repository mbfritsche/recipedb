Peanut Butter Fudge

Ingredients

  • 1 16-oz. package 10X confectioner's sugar
  • 2 C marshmallow cream
  • 1 C peanut butter
  • ½ C milk

Directions

  1. Cook Base: In a saucepan, blend confectioners' sugar and milk. Cook over low heat, bringing to a boil and continue boiling 2 minutes.
  2. Add Marshmallow: Beat in marshmallow cream until fully incorporated.
  3. Finish: Add peanut butter and blend until smooth.
  4. Set: Spread mixture into a shallow, buttered pan and refrigerate until firm.
