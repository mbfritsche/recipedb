Peanut Butter Fudge

Ingredients

  • 1 16-oz. package 10X confectioner's sugar
  • 2 C marshmallow cream
  • 1 C peanut butter
  • ½ C milk

Directions

  1. Blend confectioner's sugar and milk and bring to a boil for 2 minutes over low heat.
  2. Beat in marshmallow cream, then add peanut butter and blend well.
  3. Spread mixture into a shallow buttered pan and chill until firm.
