Senate Bean Soup

Ingredients

  • 1 lb dried Navy beans
  • 1 ham bone with plenty of meat
  • 12 C water
  • 3 onions, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves fresh garlic, minced
  • 3 stalks celery, finely chopped
  • ¼ C fresh parsley, finely chopped
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 C mashed potatoes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp pepper
  • ¼ C fresh chives, chopped

Directions

  1. Sort & Soak: Wash beans and discard any that float. Soak overnight in enough water to cover by several inches. Drain and discard soaking water.
  2. Start Soup: Add 3 quarts cold water to a large kettle. Add drained beans and ham bone. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer partially covered for 2 hours, skimming any scum from the surface.
  3. Build Body: Add onions, garlic, celery, parsley, and mashed potatoes. Continue simmering until beans are tender, about 1 hour. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  4. Finish Meat: Remove ham bone, pull off remaining meat, cut into small pieces, and return to the soup.
  5. Serve: Sprinkle with chopped fresh chives and serve hot with French bread.

Chef's Notes

  • Great Northern beans may be used in place of Navy beans.
