Grilled Chicken Marinade

Ingredients

  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Accent
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tbs soy sauce
  • 1 tbs Louisiana hot sauce
  • 1 tbs Chinese rice wine
  • 1 tbs parsley

Directions

  1. Apply seasonings liberally to both sides of chicken and allow to marinate for at least two hours, preferably overnight.
  2. Remove chicken to grill and reserve marinade for basting. Seasons about 4 chicken breasts, 6 thighs, or about 12 wings.
