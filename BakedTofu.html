Baked Tofu

Ingredients

  • 16 oz firm or extra-firm tofu
  • 2 tbs soy sauce or teriyaki sauce
  • 1 tbs olive oil
  • 1 tbs toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • ¾ C cornstarch
  • Sliced green onions, optional

Directions

  1. Prep Tofu: Preheat oven to 400°F. Blot tofu dry, cut into ¾" cubes, and press between towels with weight for 10 minutes.
  2. Season: Whisk soy sauce, olive oil, sesame oil, salt, garlic powder, and pepper in a shallow dish. Place cornstarch in another dish.
  3. Coat: Toss tofu in soy mixture, then coat with cornstarch. Arrange on a parchment‑lined baking sheet.
  4. Bake: Bake 25-30 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and crispy.
  5. Finish: Garnish with green onions and serve.
