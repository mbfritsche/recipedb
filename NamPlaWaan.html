Nam Pla Waan

Ingredients

  • 150 g palm sugar
  • 2-3 tbs fish sauce
  • 1 tbs fermented shrimp paste (gapi)
  • 2 tbs water
  • 2-3 tbs shallots
  • 2-3 Thai chilies, thinly sliced or minced
  • 3 tbs dried shrimp

Directions

  1. Make Sauce: Combine palm sugar, fish sauce, shrimp paste, and water in a small pot. Cook over medium‑low heat until the sugar dissolves and the mixture comes to a boil.
  2. Prep Shrimp: Grind 2 tbs of the dried shrimp until fine and chop the remainder into small pieces.
  3. Finish Sauce: Remove from heat and stir in both portions of dried shrimp and the chilies. Add shallots while hot for a milder flavor, or after cooling for a sharper more pungent flavor. Taste and adjust sweetness or saltiness as needed.
  4. Cool: Let cool to room temperature.
  5. Serve: Serve immediately or refrigerate in a jar for later use.

Chef's Notes

  • Tart, firm fruit for dipping: green mango, green apple, underripe pineapple, firm tart strawberries, cape gooseberries, or Korean pear.
  • Adjust fish sauce, shallots, and Thai chilies to your taste.
