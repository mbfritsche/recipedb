Haricots Verts Amandine (V)

Ingredients

  • 1 lb haricots verts (French green beans), ends trimmed
  • 2 tbs extra-virgin olive oil or clarified butter
  • ¼ C slivered almonds
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Prep: Prepare a large bowl of ice water and set aside.
  2. Blanch: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add beans and cook until tender-crisp and bright green, about 3-4 minutes. Transfer immediately to ice water.
  3. Toast Almonds: In a dry skillet over medium heat, toast almonds, stirring frequently, until golden and fragrant. Remove and set aside.
  4. Sauté: Heat olive oil in the skillet over medium heat. Add garlic and cook briefly, stirring to prevent burning.
  5. Heat Beans: Drain beans and add to the skillet. Increase heat to medium‑high and sauté until heated through, 2-3 minutes, stirring frequently.
  6. Finish: Remove from heat. Add most of the toasted almonds and lemon zest. Toss, then season with salt and pepper to taste.
  7. Serve: Transfer to a platter and top with remaining almonds and lemon zest. Serve immediately.
