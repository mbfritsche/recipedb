Crawfish Pie

Ingredients

  • 1 lb peeled crawfish
  • ½ C green onions, chopped
  • ¼ C celery, chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, crushed
  • 3 tbs butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 C milk
  • 1 C Bisquick
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ lb C Swiss cheese
  • ½ tsp white pepper
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • ¼ tsp red pepper

Directions

  1. Sauté Base: Melt butter in a black iron skillet. Add onions, garlic, and celery and sauté until limp. Stir in crawfish and seasonings; simmer 5 minutes.
  2. Prep Batter: Grease a pie plate. In a bowl, beat eggs and milk together, add Bisquick, and beat until smooth.
  3. Assemble: Spoon crawfish mixture into the prepared pie plate. Sprinkle evenly with cheese, then pour Bisquick mixture over the top.
  4. Bake: Bake at 350°F for 30-40 minutes, until set and lightly browned.
  5. Serve: Garnish with additional cheese if desired and serve hot.
