Tequila Salsa (V)

Ingredients

  • ½ C tomato, cubed
  • ½ C red onion, chopped
  • 4 jalapeños, seeded and chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ C gold or white tequila

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients and let stand for at least 30 minutes at room temperature. Taste and adjust seasonings. Makes about 1½ cups. Seeding the jalapeños will reduce their spicy nature.
  2. Serve with seafood, chicken, pork, veal, or lamb chops.

Chef's Notes

  • Source: Jane Butel's Hotter Than Hell Revised Edition.
