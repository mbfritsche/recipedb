Tequila Salsa (V)

Ingredients

  • ½ C tomato, cubed
  • ½ C red onion, chopped
  • 4 jalapeños, seeded and chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ C gold or white tequila

Directions

  1. Mix Sauce: Combine all ingredients in a bowl and mix well.
  2. Rest: Let stand at least 30 minutes at room temperature.
  3. Adjust: Taste and adjust seasoning as needed. (Seeding the jalapeños will reduce heat.)
  4. Serve: Serve with seafood, chicken, pork, veal, or lamb chops.
  5. Yield: About 1½ cups

Chef's Notes

  • Source: Jane Butel's Hotter Than Hell Revised Edition.
