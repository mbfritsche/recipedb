Pot Roast

Ingredients

  • 3-4 lb chuck roast
  • ½ C all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbs Colman’s dry mustard
  • 1 tbs oil or bacon dripping
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 stalks celery, chopped
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 8 oz mushrooms, quartered
  • 1 tbs tomato paste
  • 1 tbs anchovy paste
  • 1 tbs red wine vinegar
  • 1 tbs Worcestershire sauce
  • 1½ - 2 C vegetable stock
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder and onion powder, to taste
  • 4-6 potatoes, cubed
  • 3-5 carrots, ½" slices
  • 1 lb frozen mixed vegetables
  • 1 lb frozen corn
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1 tsp rosemary, crushed
  • 1 tsp thyme, crushed
  • Tabasco or Louisiana Hot Sauce, to taste

Directions

  1. Prep Roast: Preheat oven to 300°F. Rinse chuck roast, pat dry, and let sit at room temperature until fully dry.
  2. Season: Season roast with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder. Let sit to absorb seasoning.
  3. Coat: Combine flour with mustard and coat the roast on all sides.
  4. Brown: Heat oil over medium heat. Brown roast on all sides until well seared.
  5. Sauté Vegetables: Remove roast and add onions, celery, carrots, bell pepper, and mushrooms to the pan. Cook until softened.
  6. Build Sauce: Add any remaining flour mixture and cook briefly. Stir in vegetable stock, season, and let reduce slightly.
  7. Braise: Return roast to the pan, cover with vegetables and sauce, and cook covered in the oven until tender, stirring occasionally.
  8. Add Vegetables: Add potatoes and additional vegetables. Continue cooking until all are tender.
  9. Finish: Remove meat, chop, and return to the pot. Keep covered with liquid to stay moist.
