Tofu Bulgogi

Ingredients

  • 14 oz extra-firm tofu
  • ½ C shredded pear
  • ¼ C light soy sauce
  • 1½ tbs gochujang
  • 2 tbs dark brown sugar
  • 2 tbs toasted sesame oil
  • 2 tbs rice vinegar
  • 2 tsp grated ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 6 oz broccolini
  • 6 radishes, thinly sliced
  • 4 scallions, thinly sliced
  • 1 medium carrot, cut into matchsticks
  • 3 cups cooked long-grain brown rice
  • sesame seeds, for garnish

Directions

  1. Press Tofu: Press tofu at least 20 minutes to remove excess moisture (use a tofu press or towel‑and‑plate method).
  2. Make Sauce: In a large bowl, mix pear, soy sauce, gochujang, sugar, sesame oil, rice vinegar, ginger, and garlic. Set aside.
  3. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 350°F and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  4. Grate Tofu: Grate pressed tofu using the coarse side of a grater.
  5. Bake Tofu: Spread tofu in a thin layer and bake 25-30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until evenly golden.
  6. Blanch Veg: Blanch broccolini ~2 minutes until tender. Set aside, then add radishes, scallions, and carrots.
  7. Coat Tofu: Add baked tofu to the sauce mixture and stir to coat.
  8. Finish: Transfer to a frying pan and cook over medium heat ~3 minutes, until sauce is absorbed.
  9. Serve: Spoon tofu over rice and top with broccolini, radishes, carrots, scallions, and sesame seeds.

Chef's Notes

  • As written, this is a vegan dish
