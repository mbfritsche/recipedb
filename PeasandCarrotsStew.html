Peas and Carrots Stew (v)

Ingredients

  • 1 lb frozen petite peas
  • ½ lb carrots, chopped
  • 2 tbs butter
  • 1 tbs flour
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 3 scallions, chopped
  • 1 tbs butter
  • 1¼ C vegetable stock or water
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • ½ tsp thyme

Directions

  1. Melt Butter: In a large saucepan over moderately high heat, melt butter.
  2. Sauté Aromatics: Add onion and scallions and cook, stirring, until softened.
  3. Build Soup: Stir in flour, then add stock, carrots, salt, pepper, and thyme. Bring to a boil.
  4. Simmer: Cover, reduce heat to moderate, and cook ~8 minutes, until carrots are just tender.
  5. Add Peas: Stir in peas, cover, and simmer ~5 minutes more, until vegetables are tender.

Chef's Notes

  • This is a vegetarian dish. Substitute the butter with a plant‑based alternative to make it vegan.
  • Add 3-4 chopped button mushrooms and small fingerling potatoes for extra heartiness. If using potatoes, add them with the carrots so they cook through fully.
  • Excellent comfort food for a cold, rainy winter evening.
