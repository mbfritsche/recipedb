Arabic Spice Blend

Ingredients

  • 2 tbs ground coriander
  • 2 tbs ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground allspice
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1 tsp ground fenugreek
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper
  • ½ tsp ground ginger
  • ½ tsp ground turmeric

Directions

  1. Mix ingredients together and store in an airtight container
