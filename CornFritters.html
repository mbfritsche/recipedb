Corn Fritters (v)

Ingredients

  • 145 g corn kernels, fresh or drained from a can
  • 1 egg
  • 120 ml full-fat milk
  • 85 g plain flour
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • 1 pinch salt and freshly ground pepper
  • 2 tbs butter
  • 4 tbs vegetable oil
  • 4 poached eggs and 4 slices of lox (or smoked salmon), for serving (optional)

Directions

  1. Make Batter: Whisk egg and milk. Gradually whisk in flour and baking powder until smooth. Stir in corn; season with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat Oil: Heat oil in a cast-iron skillet over medium-high. Test with a drop of batter - if it sizzles, the oil is ready.
  3. Fry: Spoon about 60 ml (2 fl oz) batter into the oil to form fritters, cooking 2 at a time. Fry ~2 minutes per side until golden.
  4. Drain: Transfer fritters to a wire rack to drain. Repeat with remaining batter.
  5. Serve: Top fritters with lox and poached eggs and serve immediately.
