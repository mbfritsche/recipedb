Crawfish Cornbread Dressing

Ingredients

  • ½ lb crawfish tails, chopped
  • 3 C crumbled cornbread (one box of cornbread mix)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 stalks celery, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 Jalapeño, chopped
  • 4 tbs butter
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp dry mustard
  • ½ tsp thyme
  • Salt, pepper, and Cajun seasoning to taste
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 C chicken stock
  • 2 cloves garlic, chopped

Directions

  1. Prepare the cornbread according to the package directions and set aside
  2. Melt butter and sauté onion, celery, bell pepper, and garlic for 2 minutes.
  3. Add salt, pepper, paprika, thyme and dry mustard and simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Add stock and crawfish and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Stir in cornbread and simmer another 2-3 minutes.
  6. Turn into a buttered casserole and bake 15 minutes @400°

Chef's Notes

  • If the mixture is too thin, you may need to add one or two eggs to bind it
  • Andouille is also a good addition to this recipe, if it is available in your area
