Crawfish Cornbread Dressing

Ingredients

  • ½ lb crawfish tails, chopped
  • 3 C crumbled cornbread (one box of cornbread mix)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 stalks celery, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 Jalapeño, chopped
  • 4 tbs butter
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp dry mustard
  • ½ tsp thyme
  • Salt, pepper, and Cajun seasoning to taste
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 C chicken stock
  • 2 cloves garlic, chopped

Directions

  1. Prepare Cornbread: Prepare the cornbread according to package directions; set aside.
  2. Sauté Vegetables: Melt butter and sauté onion, celery, bell pepper, and garlic 2 minutes.
  3. Season & Simmer: Add salt, pepper, paprika, thyme, and dry mustard. Simmer 5 minutes.
  4. Add Crawfish: Stir in stock and crawfish; simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Combine: Crumble cornbread into the mixture and simmer 2-3 minutes more, stirring gently.
  6. Bake: Transfer to a buttered casserole dish and bake @400°F for 15 minutes.

Chef's Notes

  • If the mixture is too thin, you may need to add one or two eggs to bind it
  • Andouille is also a good addition to this recipe, if it is available in your area
