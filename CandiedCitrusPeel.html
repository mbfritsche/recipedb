Candied Citrus Peel

Ingredients

  • 10 navel oranges
  • 5 grapefruit or 14 lemons
  • 6 C sugar
  • Chocolate Dipping Sauce

Directions

  1. Prep Peel: Make 6 lengthwise cuts in the peel and remove fruit (reserve fruit for another use).
  2. Blanch: Boil peels 30 seconds, drain, rinse under cold water. Repeat 2 more times to remove bitterness.
  3. Candy: Return peel to pot with 4 C sugar and 2 C water. Bring to a gentle boil and simmer 1-1½ hours, stirring occasionally.
  4. Drain: Remove peel with tongs and drain skin-side up on a wire rack 1 hour.
  5. Sugar Coat: Toss peel with remaining 2 C sugar until evenly coated. Spread on a wax-paper-lined sheet and let dry overnight.
  6. Store: Keep in airtight jars up to 2 weeks.
  7. Optional: Dip in chocolate; chill about 1 hour until set.

Chef's Notes

For Use with Chocolate Dipping Sauce
  • Chocolate-Dipping Finish - dip the dried, sugar-coated peel in chocolate and place on waxed paper until the chocolate sets.
