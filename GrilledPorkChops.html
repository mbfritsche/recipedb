Grilled Pork Chops with Oyster Dressing

Ingredients

  • 4 (10 ounce) double cut pork chops
  • 4 tbs olive oil
  • Cajun seasoning
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • ½ green pepper, chopped
  • ½ red pepper, chopped
  • ¼ C green onions, chopped
  • 1 tbs minced shallots
  • 1 tbs minced garlic
  • 1 lb shucked oysters, chopped
  • 1 qt heavy cream
  • 1 tbs Louisiana hot sauce
  • 2 tbs Worcestershire sauce
  • ¼ C grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese
  • 3 to 4 C day-old bread, cubed
  • 6 eggs, slightly beaten
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Garnish
  • ¼ C chopped green onions
  • ¼ C grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese
  • ½ C sizzled leeks
  • 1 tbs brunoise red peppers
  • 1 tbs brunoise yellow peppers

Directions

  1. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Season Chops: Coat pork chops with 2 tbs olive oil and Creole seasoning. Grill ~8 minutes per side; set aside.
  3. Cook Vegetables: In a large pot, heat 2 tbs olive oil. Sauté vegetables 2-3 minutes until wilted; season with Creole seasoning.
  4. Add Oysters: Stir in oysters and cook ~1 minute, just until edges begin to curl.
  5. Build Dressing: Whisk in cream, hot sauce, and Worcestershire.
  6. Add Bread: Fold in bread cubes until evenly moistened.
  7. Finish Mixture: Remove from heat; whisk in eggs, season with salt and pepper, then fold in green onions and cheese.
  8. Bake Dressing: Transfer to a buttered 9x13" baking dish. Cover with foil and bake 30 minutes, then uncover and bake 15 minutes more.
  9. Rest: Let sit 5 minutes before serving.
  10. Plate: Mound dressing in the center of the plate, lean chop against it, and garnish with leeks, cheese, green onions, and peppers.
