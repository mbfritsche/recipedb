Champagne Vinaigrette

Ingredients

  • 120 ml olive oil
  • 80 ml Champagne vinegar
  • 10 g Dijon mustard
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 small shallot, finely diced
  • 7 g honey
  • 3 g salt
  • 3 g tsp pepper

Directions

  1. Add all ingredients except the olive oil to a mixing bowl and whisk together.
  2. Slowly add the olive oil and whisk vigorously until the dressing is combined.

Chef's Notes

  • Use an immersion blender with a mixing cup for this, if you have one - fast, easy, and extremely effective
