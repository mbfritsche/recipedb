Roasted Sesame Dressing

Ingredients (6 oz)

  • 27 g White sesame seeds (3 tbs)
  • 45 g Japanese Kewpie mayonnaise (3 tbs)
  • 41 g Neutral-flavored oil (3 tbs)
  • 23 g Japanese soy sauce (1½ tbs)
  • 15 g Rice vinegar (1 tbs)
  • 10 g Mirin (2 tsp)
  • 21 g Honey or brown sugar (2-3 tsp)
  • 2 g Toasted sesame oil (½ tsp)
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Toast Sesame: Toast sesame seeds in a dry pan over medium-high, stirring constantly. Once golden, lower heat and continue until deep brown. Immediately transfer to a plate to cool.
  2. Grind: Grind toasted seeds with a mortar and pestle until mostly fine, leaving some whole seeds.
  3. Finish: Add remaining ingredients, mix well until honey is fully dissolved, and adjust seasoning.
  4. Store: Refrigerate and shake before using. Keeps for several weeks.

Chef's Notes

  • You can grind the sesame seeds in a spice grinder, but don't over-grind.
  • Avocado oil is ideal for its neutral flavor, though any neutral oil will work.
  • Honey or brown sugar can be swapped for another sweetener of your choice.
  • Kewpie mayonnaise gives the most authentic flavor.
  • Choose Mirin without corn syrup or added sugar.
  • This is a Kewpie copycat recipe
