Crab and Spinach Egg Casserole

Ingredients

  • 2 tbs butter
  • 8 large eggs
  • 2 C half-and-half cream
  • 2 cans (6 oz each) crabmeat, drained
  • 1 package (10 oz) frozen chopped spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
  • 1 C dry bread crumbs
  • 1 C shredded Swiss cheese
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp pepper
  • ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  • 2 celery ribs, chopped
  • ½ cup chopped onion
  • ½ cup chopped sweet red pepper
  • 3 medium fresh mushrooms, chopped
  • 2 tbs butter

Directions

  1. In a large bowl, beat eggs and cream.
  2. Stir in the crab, spinach, bread crumbs, cheese, salt, pepper and nutmeg and set aside
  3. In a skillet, saute the celery, onion, red pepper and mushrooms in butter until tender.
  4. Add to the spinach mixture and stir to combine
  5. Transfer to a buttered shallow 2-1/2-qt. baking dish.
  6. Bake uncovered at 375° for 30-35 minutes or until a thermometer reads 160°.
  7. Let stand for 10 minutes before serving.

Chef's Notes

  • Upgrade thought: Melt 2 tbs butter and add to 2 tbs plain bread crumbs, add onion and garlic powder, mix well, and add to the top of the dish at the halfway point
