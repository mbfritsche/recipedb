Eggplant Goulash (V)

Ingredients

  • ¼ C olive oil
  • 1 medium eggplant, cut into ½-1" pieces
  • 1 red bell pepper, roughly chopped
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbs all-purpose flour
  • 2½ C vegetable broth
  • 2 tbs sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp caraway seed (optional)
  • 1 bay leaf
  • ¾ lb Yukon gold potatoes, diced into ½-1" pieces
  • 1 C canned diced tomatoes
  • 2 tbs soy sauce
  • 2 tbs organic granulated sugar
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Sauté: Coat the bottom of a large pot with olive oil and heat over medium heat. Add eggplant and cook until browned and tender, about 10 minutes.
  2. Cook Vegetables: Add bell pepper and onion and cook until beginning to soften, about 5 minutes.
  3. Build Base: Stir in flour and garlic. Cook briefly, stirring constantly, until fragrant.
  4. Simmer: Add broth, sweet paprika, smoked paprika, caraway, bay leaf, and potatoes. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer. Cover and cook until potatoes are tender, about 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Finish: Stir in tomatoes, soy sauce, and sugar. Continue simmering until slightly thickened, about 5-10 minutes.
  6. Season: Remove bay leaf and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper
  7. Serve: Serve hot with steamed rice or noodles

Chef's Notes

  • If the eggplant is older or shows brown spots when cut, salt it before cooking. Toss the chopped eggplant with salt, let it drain in a colander for about 30 minutes, then rinse and pat dry before proceeding with the recipe.
