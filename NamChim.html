Thai Seafood Sauce (Nam Chim)

Ingredients

  • 6-8 Thai chili peppers, minced
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
  • ½ tsp sugar
  • 2 tbs Thai fish sauce
  • 2 scallions, minced
  • Juice of 2-3 limes (3-4 tbs)

Directions

  1. Mix Sauce: Combine all ingredients in a bowl and mix well.
  2. Adjust Balance: Taste and adjust seasoning as needed. Some limes are very tart and may require a little extra sugar. Add sparingly, as over-sweetening is difficult to correct.
  3. Serve: Use as a dipping sauce for boiled or steamed crab, lobster, shrimp, clams, or mussels.
