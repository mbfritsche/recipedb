Ratatouille (V)

Ingredients

  • 1 large eggplant, cubed
  • 2 large zucchini, cubed
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 large onions, chopped
  • 3 large peeled seeded tomatoes
  • 2 tsp basil
  • ½ C olive oil
  • 1 tbs salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp white pepper

Directions

  1. Prep Eggplant: Cut eggplant into ½" cubes. Toss with 1 tsp salt and lemon juice, then place in a colander to drain for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Sauté Base: Heat olive oil in a large pot and cook garlic until lightly browned. Add onions and cook until translucent.
  3. Cook Vegetables: Add zucchini, peppers, eggplant, and remaining seasonings. Cook, stirring, until vegetables begin to soften, about 10-15 minutes.
  4. Simmer: Add tomatoes and cook until the mixture has reduced and thickened, about 20 minutes.
  5. Serve: Serve as a vegetable side dish or relish.

Chef's Notes

  • Fresh basil adds a bright flavor boost to this dish.
