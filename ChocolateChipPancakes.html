Chocolate Chip Pancakes

Ingredients

Full Recipe
  • 2 C Bisquick
  • 1½ C milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbs sugar
  • 2 tsp cinnamon-sugar (Equal parts)
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 C semi-sweet chocolate mini morsels
Half Recipe
  • 1 C Bisquick
  • ¾ C milk
  • 1 eggs
  • 1 tbs sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon-sugar (Equal parts)
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • ½ C semi-sweet chocolate mini morsels

Directions

  1. Mix Batter: Stir Bisquick and milk until smooth. Add eggs one at a time, mixing well.
  2. Flavor: Add vanilla, sugar, cinnamon-sugar mixture, and chocolate chips; mix until combined.
  3. Heat Griddle: Preheat a nonstick griddle to 325-350°F.
  4. Cook Pancakes: Pour batter using a ¼ C measure. When bubbles rise and pop, flip (or check edges with a spatula).
  5. Finish: Cook both sides until golden brown and serve.
