Baked Redfish

Ingredients

  • 1 cleaned redfish (5 lb or more)
  • 3 medium onions, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 small can tomato sauce
  • 1 dash Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 stick butter
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • pinch of sugar
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Prep Fish: Season fish with salt and pepper and let sit at room temperature while preparing the sauce.
  2. Make Sauce: Melt butter and sauté onions and bell pepper until onions are translucent. Stir in tomato sauce, Worcestershire, sugar, and garlic powder. Cover and simmer 45 minutes.
  3. Bake: Place fish in a baking dish and spoon sauce over the fish and into the body cavity. Bake at 325°F for 45 minutes to 1 hour, until the internal temperature reaches 145°F.
  4. Serve: Spoon extra sauce over the fish and serve hot.

Chef's Notes

  • Add equal parts of cream of mushroom, cream of shrimp, and cream of chicken soup to the vegetable sauce while it is simmering.
