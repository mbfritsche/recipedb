Basic Seafood Stock

Ingredients

  • 2 qt cold water
  • 1 med onion, unpeeled and quartered
  • 2 large cloves garlic, whole
  • 1 rib celery
  • 1½ - 2 lb fish trimmings

Directions

  1. Place all ingredients in a large stock pot.
  2. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce heat and simmer gently, fully or partially covered, for 4 - 8 hours, replenishing the water as needed to keep about 1 qt of liquid in the pot.
  3. Strain, cool, and refrigerate until ready to use. Makes about 1 qt.

Chef's Notes

  • You may use rinsed shrimp heads and/or shells, crab shells (2½ - 3 qt), rinsed fish carcasses, oyster liquor, or any combination of these.
