Grilled Chicken with Black Olive Relish

Ingredients

  • 2 chickens, quartered
  • ¼ C olive oil
  • ½ C coarsely chopped garlic
  • ½ C coarsely chopped fresh oregano leaves
  • 1 tbs sherry vinegar
  • 2 tbs lime juice
  • 1 tbs honey
  • 2 tsp ancho powder
  • 1 C Black Olive Relish

Directions

  1. Combine olive oil, garlic, oregano, sherry vinegar, line juice, honey, and ancho powder in a large mixing bowl. Marinate chicken in this mixture for at least one hour.
  2. Grill chicken for 10 minutes on each side or until well done. Top with Black Olive Relish and serve. Makes four servings.
