Grilled Chicken with Black Olive Relish

Ingredients

  • 2 chickens, quartered
  • ¼ C olive oil
  • ½ C coarsely chopped garlic
  • ½ C coarsely chopped fresh oregano leaves
  • 1 tbs sherry vinegar
  • 2 tbs lime juice
  • 1 tbs honey
  • 2 tsp ancho powder
  • 1 C Black Olive Relish

Directions

  1. Make Marinade: Combine olive oil, garlic, oregano, sherry vinegar, lime juice, honey, and ancho powder in a large bowl.
  2. Marinate Chicken: Add chicken, coat well, and marinate at least 1 hour.
  3. Grill: Grill chicken ~10 minutes per side, or until fully cooked.
  4. Serve: Top with Black Olive Relish and serve.
