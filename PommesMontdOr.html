Pommes Mont d'Or

Ingredients

  • 4 C mashed potatoes
  • ½ C Gruyere cheese
  • 3 eggs
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Mix: Combine eggs with mashed potatoes in a food processor. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  2. Assemble: Transfer mixture to a buttered gratin dish and spread evenly. Top with cheese.
  3. Bake: Bake at 400°F until heated through and golden on top, about 30 minutes.
