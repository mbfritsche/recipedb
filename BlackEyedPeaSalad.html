Black-eyed Pea Salad

Ingredients

  • 1½ C cooked or canned black-eyed peas
  • ¾ C bell pepper, chopped
  • ½ C celery, chopped
  • ½ C red onion, chopped
  • ¼ C onion, chopped
  • ¼ C vegetable oil
  • ¼ C sugar
  • 2 tbs cider vinegar
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ black pepper
  • ½ tsp hot sauce

Directions

  1. Combine Vegetables: In a large bowl, mix peas, peppers, and onions.
  2. Make Dressing: Whisk together oil, sugar, vinegar, salt, pepper, hot sauce, and garlic.
  3. Toss & Chill: Pour dressing over vegetables, toss to coat thoroughly, and refrigerate at least 2 hours to let flavors combine
