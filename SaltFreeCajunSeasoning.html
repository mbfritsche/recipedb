Salt-Free Cajun Seasoning

Ingredients

  • 14 g garlic powder
  • 14 g onion powder
  • 14 g paprika
  • 3 g cayenne pepper
  • 2 g black pepper
  • 1 g thyme
  • 1 g oregano
  • 2 g red pepper flakes

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients thoroughly and store in an airtight container. Makes about ¾ cup.
