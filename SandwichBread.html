Sandwich Bread

Ingredients

  • 430 g bread flour (~1lb))
  • 245 g warm water
  • 20 g oil
  • 7 g yeast
  • 9 g kosher salt
  • 6 g sugar

Directions

  1. Mix Dry & Wet: Combine flour and salt in one bowl. In mixer bowl, mix yeast, sugar, oil, and water; proof 5 minutes.
  2. Make Dough: Add flour mixture, mix until combined, then knead for 10 minutes.
  3. First Rise: Form dough into a ball, place in greased bowl, cover, and let rise 30 minutes.
  4. Shape Loaf: Grease loaf pan(s). Punch down dough, flatten into rectangle, roll into loaf shape, and place in pan(s).
  5. Second Rise: Cover and let rise another 30 minutes.
  6. Bake: Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes, rotating after 20.

Baking Timeline

Step Direction Time (min) Notes
1 Proof yeast 5
2 Combine and knead 10
3 Ball, oil, cover, and rest 30 Proof #1
4 De-gas, shape, and rest in loaf pan 30 Proof #2
5 Bake @350°F 30 Roate at 20 minutes
