Corned Beef Reuben Sliders

Ingredients

  • 12 pull-apart Hawaiian rolls
  • ½ C Thousand Island dressing
  • ½ lb thin deli-sliced corned beef
  • 6 slices Swiss cheese
  • 1 C sauerkraut, drained
  • 1 tbs butter
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds

Directions

  1. Prep: Preheat oven to 350°F. Slice Hawaiian rolls in half horizontally to form one large bottom and top.
  2. Assemble Base: Place bottom bun on a sheet tray and spread dressing evenly over it.
  3. Layer & Melt: Add corned beef and Swiss cheese. Bake about 5 minutes, until cheese melts.
  4. Finish Assembly: Remove from oven, add sauerkraut, place top bun on, brush with butter, and sprinkle with caraway seeds.
  5. Warm: Return to oven for 5 minutes, just until buns are warmed.
  6. Serve: Pull apart into sliders and serve.
