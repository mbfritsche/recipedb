New England Clam Chowder

Ingredients

  • 2 cans baby clams (20 oz)
  • 3 slices bacon, small dice
  • 2 C clam juice
  • 1 C water or vegetable stock
  • 2 potatoes, diced
  • 1 leek, chopped
  • 1 jalapeno, diced
  • 1 ear corn (~1 C)
  • ½ tsp fresh thyme
  • 1-2 cloves garlic, minced (or grated)
  • 2 tbs flour
  • 1 tbs oil
  • ½ C heavy cream
  • ½ tsp ground pepper

Directions

  1. Render the bacon, add the leek, garlic, thyme, and jalapeno
  2. Add flour and singe for 1-2 minutes
  3. Add clam juice and water
  4. Add potatoes, salt and pepper to taste
  5. Cover and simmer 20-30 minutes
  6. Add the corn and the clams, simmer a bit, and finish with the heavy cream

Chef's Notes

  • If you forget the flour or want a thicker chowder, add a little stock to the flour, make a smooth slurry, and add it back
