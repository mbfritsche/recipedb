New England Clam Chowder

Ingredients

  • 2 cans baby clams (20 oz)
  • 3 slices bacon, small dice
  • 2 C clam juice
  • 1 C water or vegetable stock
  • 2 potatoes, diced
  • 1 leek, chopped
  • 1 jalapeno, diced
  • 1 ear corn (~1 C)
  • ½ tsp fresh thyme
  • 1-2 cloves garlic, minced (or grated)
  • 2 tbs flour
  • 1 tbs oil
  • ½ C heavy cream
  • ½ tsp ground pepper

Directions

  1. Render Bacon: In a large pot, render bacon until fat is released. Add leek, garlic, thyme, and jalapeño; sauté until softened.
  2. Make Roux: Stir in flour and cook 1-2 minutes, stirring, to lightly toast the flour.
  3. Add Liquids: Pour in clam juice and water, stirring to combine.
  4. Simmer Potatoes: Add potatoes and season with salt and pepper. Cover and simmer 20-30 minutes, until potatoes are tender.
  5. Finish Chowder: Stir in corn and clams and simmer briefly. Finish by stirring in heavy cream.
  6. Serve: Serve hot.

Chef's Notes

  • If you forget the flour or want a thicker chowder, add a little stock to the flour, make a smooth slurry, and add it back
