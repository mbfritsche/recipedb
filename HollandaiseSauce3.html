Hollandaise Sauce #3

Ingredients

  • 2 large egg yolks
  • 1 tbs cold water
  • 1 stick butter, melted and skimmed
  • 1 tbs fresh squeezed lemon juice
  • Pinch cayenne, salt
  • Dash Worcestershire sauce, Louisiana hot sauce

Directions

  1. Set Up Bain-Marie: Place a medium heatproof bowl over (but not touching) a saucepan with 2" of simmering water.
  2. Warm Yolks: Whisk egg yolks with cold water and salt until the mixture is warm to the touch.
  3. Emulsify Butter: Slowly drizzle in half of the melted butter in a thin stream, whisking constantly until incorporated.
  4. Finish Sauce: Continue whisking in the remaining butter until the sauce is thick and fully emulsified. Whisk in lemon juice, hot sauce, and Worcestershire.
  5. Serve: Serve warm.
  6. Yield: About 1 cup

Chef's Notes

  • Keep the sauce warm in a bain‑marie (warm water bath) or a Thermos.
  • Do not hot‑hold longer than 90 minutes. If longer holding is needed, discard after 90 minutes and prepare a fresh batch.
