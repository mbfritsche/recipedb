French-Fried Eggplant

Ingredients

  • 1 large, long eggplant
  • 1 egg
  • ¾ C milk
  • Flour
  • Oil for deep frying
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Powdered sugar (Optional)

Directions

  1. Prep Eggplant: Peel eggplant and cut into pieces the size of large French fries. Soak in salted water for 30 minutes, then rinse and pat dry.
  2. Make Coating: Whisk egg and milk together and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Coat: Dip eggplant in egg mixture, then roll in flour to coat.
  4. Fry: Deep fry until golden brown.
  5. Serve: Sprinkle with additional salt and serve with powdered sugar.
