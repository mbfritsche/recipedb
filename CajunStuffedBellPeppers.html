Cajun Stuffed Bell Peppers

Ingredients

  • ½ lb ground beef
  • ½ lb ground pork
  • 3 C cooked rice (1 C raw)
  • 4 bell peppers
  • 1 shallot, finely chopped
  • 1 stalk celery, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 jalapeno, seeded and diced
  • 1 can Rotel tomatoes
  • 1½ C spicy Bloody Mary mix
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • ¾ tsp black pepper
  • Cajun seasoning and Tabasco to taste
  • 2 tbs of bacon fat or vegetable oil

Directions

  1. Brown Meats: Brown meats in 2 tbs bacon fat with Cajun seasoning. Remove to a bowl.
  2. Cook Veg: Add vegetables to the pan and sauté until softened and lightly colored. Stir in Rotel tomatoes, salt, and pepper; cook a few minutes.
  3. Combine Filling: Return meat to the pan and cook until most liquid evaporates. Remove from heat and mix in cooked rice.
  4. Stuff & Bake: Fill peppers with the mixture and place in an ungreased gratin dish with 1½ cups Bloody Mary mix. Set dish on a sheet pan and bake at 350°F for 30 minutes.
  5. Baste & Finish: Baste peppers with the sauce and bake 30 minutes more.
  6. Serve: Transfer peppers to a warm platter and spoon sauce from the dish over the top.
