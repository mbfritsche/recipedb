Oysters Gabie

Ingredients

  • 16 to 20 large oysters and their liquor
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 4 tbs olive oil
  • 2 large artichokes
  • 4 ounces pancetta, finely diced
  • 1 tbs butter
  • ¼ C + 2 tbs dry bread crumbs
  • ¼ C + 2 tbs Parmesan cheese, grated
  • ¼ C green onions, chopped (white portion only)
  • 1 tbs garlic, minced
  • 2 tbs fresh parsley, minced
  • Salt to taste
  • Pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Cook Artichokes: Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add lemon halves and 2 tbs olive oil, then add artichokes and cook ~20 minutes, until tender. Drain and cool slightly.
  2. Prep Artichokes: Pull off leaves and scrape the tender flesh from their ends. Remove and discard the fuzzy choke. Dice the artichoke hearts and combine with the reserved scraped flesh; set aside.
  3. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 450°F.
  4. Cook Pancetta: In a medium sauté pan, heat 1 tbs olive oil and cook pancetta until browned.
  5. Sauté Aromatics: Add remaining 1 tbs olive oil and butter, then green onions, garlic, and parsley. Sauté 3-4 minutes, until tender.
  6. Add Artichokes: Stir in diced artichokes and lemon juice. If the mixture seems dry, add a little oyster liquor to moisten. Sauté ~2 minutes and adjust seasoning.
  7. Finish Dressing: Remove from heat and fold in ¼ C breadcrumbs and ¼ C cheese.
  8. Assemble: Place 4-5 oysters in individual casseroles or ramekins. Top with the artichoke dressing. Sprinkle with remaining cheese and breadcrumbs.
  9. Bake: Bake 10-15 minutes, until browned and bubbling.
  10. Serve: Drizzle with Hollandaise sauce and serve immediately with French bread.
