Agedashi Tofu

Ingredients

  • 1 block (16-ounces) super firm tofu
  • 1 tsp Hondashi (dashi stock flakes)
  • 1 tbs mirin
  • 1 tbs soy sauce
  • 1 C water
  • ¼ C bonito flakes
  • 4 scallions, chopped
  • 2-inch piece of ginger, grated
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ C potato starch
  • 1 C oil

Directions

  1. Make Sauce: In a small pot, combine water, dashi flakes, mirin, soy sauce, and salt. Simmer on low 10 minutes, then cover and remove from heat.
  2. Prep Tofu: Pat super-firm tofu dry. If using extra-firm, press for 15 minutes to remove excess moisture.
  3. Coat: Cut tofu into 6 large pieces and coat evenly with potato starch.
  4. Fry: Heat oil in a deep skillet over medium-high. Fry tofu 6 minutes per side until golden brown.
  5. Drain: Remove tofu and rest on a wire rack.
  6. Serve: Spoon sauce into bowls, top with tofu, and garnish with scallions, bonito flakes, and grated ginger.

Chef's Notes

  • A delicate balance of flavors
