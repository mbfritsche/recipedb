Air Fryer Honey Dijon Salmon

Ingredients

  • 2 skin-on salmon steaks, about 300-400 grams
  • 30 g Avocado oil
  • 25 g Honey
  • 30 g Dijon
  • To taste: kosher salt, black pepper, garlic powder, seasoned salt, paprika, and parsley flakes

Directions

  1. Prep Glaze: Whisk honey, Dijon, and avocado oil into an emulsion; brush over both sides of the salmon.
  2. Season: Sprinkle both sides with kosher salt, black pepper, garlic powder, paprika, seasoned salt, and parsley flakes.
  3. Air Fry: Cook @400°F for 8-10 minutes.
  4. Serve: Transfer to a serving platter and serve on your favorite salad.
