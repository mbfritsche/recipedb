Basic Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 C flour
  • 1 tbs sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp baking soda
  • 1 C milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbs melted butter

Directions

  1. Make Batter: Mix dry ingredients, then add wet ingredients and stir just until combined. Add more milk if batter is too thick (especially if using buttermilk). Rest batter 20-30 minutes.
  2. Heat Griddle: Heat griddle over medium until water droplets sizzle. Lightly grease with oil or shortening.
  3. Cook Pancakes: Ladle batter onto griddle. When bubbles form and break on the surface (about 3 minutes), flip.
  4. Finish & Serve: Cook 2 minutes more, then remove to a plate, top with butter, and serve immediately.
