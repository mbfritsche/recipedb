Pimiento Cheese

Ingredients

  • 8 oz Philadelphia cream cheese
  • 4 oz diced pimentos (with juice)
  • 1 C shredded cheddar
  • 1 C shredded Monterey Jack
  • ¾ C mayonnaise
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼ tsp cayenne
  • Salt/pepper and hot sauce to taste

Directions

  1. Mix cheeses in a stand mixer on low for 2-3 minutes. Add mayo, spices and (drained) pimentos. Refrigerate after use
