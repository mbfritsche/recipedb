Baked Chicken

Ingredients

  • 4 skin-on boneless chicken thighs
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp MSG
  • 2 tbs rice wine
  • 2 tbs hot sauce
  • 2 tbs soy sauce
  • 112 g stick butter

Directions

  1. Prep Chicken: Wash chicken and pat dry. Season both sides of the thighs with dry seasoning and place in a shallow baking dish.
  2. Make Sauce: Melt butter and stir in soy sauce, hot sauce, and rice wine.
  3. Bake: Pour sauce over the chicken and bake at 375°F, basting frequently, for about 1 hour, or until internal temperature reaches 165°F.
  4. Serve: Spoon pan juices over the chicken and serve hot.
