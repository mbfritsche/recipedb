Romano Vinaigrette

Ingredients

  • ½ C vegetable oil
  • ¾ C white vinegar
  • ¼ C + 1 tbs water
  • 70 g shredded Romano cheese
  • 20 g Parmesan cheese, grated
  • 6-8 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbs chopped green onion
  • 3 tbs granulated garlic
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbs sugar

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients together, whisk until well incorporated, and chill

Chef's Notes

  • This is a copycat version of the house salad dressing at Mike Anderson's in Baton Rouge
