Polish Mistakes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 lb ground breakfast sausage (See Chef's Notes)
  • 1 lb Velveeta, cubed
  • 1½ tbs Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp minced onion
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 loaf sliced Jewish cocktail rye

Directions

  1. Cook Meat: In a nonstick pan, brown ground beef and sausage over medium heat. Drain well and return to the pan.
  2. Melt Cheese: Add cheese cubes and seasonings. Heat over medium-low, stirring frequently, until cheese is fully melted.
  3. Adjust Seasoning: Taste and season with salt, pepper, and Cajun seasoning as desired.
  4. Bake: Arrange sliced bread on a baking sheet. Top each slice with 1 tbs of the cheese-meat mixture and bake at 400°F for 10 minutes.

Chef's Notes

  • Hot or Italian sausage adds great flavor; if that's too strong, mild breakfast sausage works well.
  • This freezes well. Freeze assembled bites on a sheet pan until solid, then transfer to an airtight freezer-safe container.
  • Bake from frozen at 350°F for 20 minutes.
