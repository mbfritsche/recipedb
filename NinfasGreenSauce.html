Ninfa's Green Sauce

Ingredients

  • 3 medium avocados
  • 3 medium green tomatoes
  • 4 fresh tomatillos
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 4 sprigs coriander
  • 1-2 jalapeños
  • 1½ C sour cream
  • Salt to taste

Directions

  1. Prep Avocados: Peel avocados and place them in a blender.
  2. Boil Vegetables: In a medium saucepan, boil tomatoes, tomatillos, garlic, and jalapeños 15 minutes.
  3. Blend: Transfer the boiled mixture to the blender with the avocados.
  4. Finish: Add sour cream and blend ~5 minutes, until smooth and creamy.
  5. Serve: Serve with tortilla chips and margaritas.
