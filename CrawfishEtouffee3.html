Crawfish Etouffee #3

Ingredients

  • 1 lb crawfish tails, with fat
  • 1 stick butter
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 tbs bell pepper, minced
  • ½ stalk celery, minced
  • Green onion tops
  • Fresh parsley
  • 2 tsp tomato paste
  • Cayenne pepper
  • Freshly ground black pepper Salt
  • 2 tbs flour
  • 1-2 C water

Directions

  1. Make Roux: Cook butter and flour together until a smooth roux forms.
  2. Add Trinity: Stir in onion, bell pepper, celery, and garlic. Cook until vegetables are tender.
  3. Build Sauce: Add crawfish fat (or additional butter if unavailable) and cook 15-20 minutes. Stir in crawfish tails and tomato-paste water.
  4. Simmer: Simmer 20 minutes, allowing flavors to meld.
  5. Finish: Season with salt and pepper to taste. Add green onion tops and parsley; simmer 5 minutes more.
  6. Serve: Serve hot over rice.
