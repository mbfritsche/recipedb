Calas (Rice Fritters)

Ingredients

  • 6 tbs flour
  • 3 tbs sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 C cooked rice
  • 2 eggs
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp vanilla
  • pinch nutmeg
  • Cooking oil for deep frying powdered sugar

Directions

  1. Mix Batter: Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, salt, and vanilla. In a separate bowl, mix rice and eggs, then stir into the dry ingredients until well combined.
  2. Fry: Drop batter by spoonfuls into 350°F oil and fry until golden brown.
  3. Drain & Serve: Drain on a wire rack, dust with powdered sugar, and serve hot.

Chef's Notes

  • These are also excellent served with honey or cane syrup instead of powdered sugar.
