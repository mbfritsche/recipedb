Calas

Ingredients

  • 6 tbs flour
  • 3 tbs sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 C cooked rice
  • 2 eggs
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp vanilla
  • pinch nutmeg
  • Cooking oil for deep frying powdered sugar

Directions

  1. Combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, salt, and vanilla.
  2. Thoroughly mix the rice and eggs together in a separate bowl.
  3. Add the dry ingredients to the rice and egg mixture and mix well.
  4. Drop by spoonfuls into 350° cooking oil, fry until brown, and drain on a wire rack.
  5. Sprinkle with powdered sugar and serve hot.

Chef's Notes

  • These are also very good with honey or cane syrup instead of powdered sugar.
