Green Bean Artichoke Casserole (v)

Ingredients

  • ½ lb artichoke hearts, diced
  • 2 lbs fresh French-style green beans
  • ½ C extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 C Italian bread crumbs
  • 1 C grated Parmesan cheese
  • ½ large onion, minced
  • 8 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and ground black pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Blanch Beans: Blanch green beans ~2 minutes in boiling water. Drain and set aside.
  2. Sauté Aromatics: Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté onions and garlic until softened.
  3. Combine: In a large bowl, mix green beans, breadcrumbs, cheese, artichoke hearts, and the sautéed onion-garlic mixture with the oil.
  4. Season: Season generously with black pepper and salt to taste.
  5. Assemble: Transfer to a 9x13" baking dish. Sprinkle the top with additional breadcrumbs and cheese.
  6. Bake: Bake @350°F for 20-30 minutes, until heated through and lightly browned.

Chef's Notes

  • This is a vegetarian recipe, vegan with a plant-based substitute for Parmesan cheese
