Cheddar Biscuits

Bisquick Alternative

Ingredients

  • 2 C buttermilk baking mix (small box equals 2 cups, Bisquick or Jiffy Mix)
  • 160 ml milk
  • ½ C shredded cheddar cheese
  • ¼ C butter
  • ½ tsp garlic powder

Directions

  1. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. Mix Dough: Combine biscuit mix, milk, and cheese until a soft dough forms; beat vigorously 30 seconds.
  3. Bake: Drop heaping tablespoonfuls onto an ungreased baking sheet. Bake 8-10 minutes until golden.
  4. Finish: Mix butter with garlic powder and brush over warm biscuits.
  5. Serve: Serve warm.
Flour Alternative

Ingredients

  • 1 C all-purpose flour
  • 1 C cake flour
  • 1½ tsp baking powder
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp garlic powder, divided
  • ¼ tsp baking soda
  • 4 tbs cold butter, divided
  • ½ C finely shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 C buttermilk
  • ½ tsp dried parsley flakes

Directions

  1. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Mix Dry: In a large bowl, combine flours, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and half the garlic powder.
  3. Cut Butter: Cut in 3 tbs butter until mixture resembles coarse crumbs. Stir in cheese, then add buttermilk just until moistened.
  4. Bake: Drop heaping tablespoonfuls 2" apart onto greased baking sheets. Bake 10-12 minutes until golden.
  5. Finish: Melt remaining butter, mix with parsley and remaining garlic powder, and brush over warm biscuits.
  6. Serve: Serve warm.
