Jalapeño Cornbread

Ingredients

  • 2 jalapeños
  • 2 tsp veg oil
  • 2¼ C all-purpose flour
  • 1 C yellow cornmeal
  • 1 C sugar
  • 3 tbs baking powder
  • 2 tbs kosher salt
  • ¼ tsp ground pepper
  • 2 large eggs + 1 egg white, lightly beaten together
  • 1½ C whole buttermilk
  • ¾ C unsalted butter, melted and cooled
  • 2 medium onions, chopped
  • 2 tbs bacon fat
Pure Cane Syrup Butter
  • ½ C unsalted butter, room temperature
  • 2 tbs Steen's cane syrup
  • 2 tsp kosher salt

Directions

  1. Roast Jalapeños: Preheat oven to 450°F. Wrap ñ in foil and bake 15 minutes, turning once. Let steam 15 minutes, then peel, seed, and finely chop.
  2. Mix Batter: Whisk flour, cornmeal, sugar, baking powder, salt, and pepper. Add eggs and egg white, buttermilk, and melted butter; mix just until combined (do not overmix).
  3. Add Veg: Stir chopped jalapeños and onions into the batter.
  4. Heat Pan: Preheat a cast-iron skillet or Dutch oven with bacon fat until hot.
  5. Bake: Pour batter into hot pan and bake on the center rack until golden and a toothpick comes out clean (~30 minutes).
  6. Cool: Remove and cool ~30 minutes before serving.
  7. Finish Butter: Mix butter, cane syrup, and salt until smooth; serve alongside or spoon over warm cornbread.
