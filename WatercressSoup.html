Watercress Soup

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs Pork spare ribs
  • 5 cups of water
  • 2 tbs Better Than Bouillon Vegetable base
  • Salt, white pepper, MSG and seasoning soy sauce (to taste)
  • 2 bunches of watercress

Directions

  1. Season: Season spare ribs with salt, white pepper, and soy sauce to taste.
  2. Start Soup: In a large pot, bring water, vegetable base, and ribs to a boil over high heat. Reduce heat and simmer for 30 minutes, skimming the surface as fat renders.
  3. Adjust: Taste broth and add more soy sauce if needed.
  4. Add Greens: Wash watercress thoroughly and add to the pot. Simmer until tender, about 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.

Chef's Notes

  • Pork neck bones or other meaty soup bones may be substituted.
