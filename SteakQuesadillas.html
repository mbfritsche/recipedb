Steak Quesadillas

Ingredients

  • ½ lb pound of thin-cut steak (e.g., top sirloin)
  • 1 large bell pepper
  • 1 yellow onion
  • 8-10 oz sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
  • Flour tortillas
  • butter for cooking steak
  • salt and pepper
  • sour cream

Directions

  1. Sear Steak: Heat oil in a cast iron pan over medium‑high heat. Sear steak on both sides until browned, about 3 minutes per side. Remove and let rest.
  2. Prep Vegetables: Dice pepper and onion.
  3. Sauté: Add a little oil or butter to the pan. Cook vegetables with salt and pepper until softened and lightly browned, about 5 minutes.
  4. Combine: Cut steak into bite-size pieces and return to the pan with vegetables. Cook briefly, then remove from heat.
  5. Assemble: Place half a tortilla in the pan and sprinkle with cheese. Add steak and vegetable mixture, then more cheese. Fold tortilla over.
  6. Cook: Cook over medium‑low heat until cheese melts and tortilla is lightly browned on both sides.
  7. Serve: Slice into wedges and serve with sour cream.
