Spicy Baked Beans

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (28 ounces each) baked beans
  • 1 can (20 ounces) unsweetened crushed pineapple, drained
  • 1 C spicy barbecue sauce
  • ½ C molasses
  • 2 tbs prepared mustard
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 can (6 ounces) french-fried onions, crushed, divided
  • 5 bacon strips, cooked and crumbled, divided

Directions

  1. Mix: In a large bowl, combine the first seven ingredients.
  2. Add & Transfer: Stir in half of the onions and bacon. Transfer mixture to a greased 13x9" baking dish.
  3. Bake Covered: Cover and bake at 350°F for 45 minutes.
  4. Finish: Sprinkle with remaining onions and bacon. Bake uncovered until bubbly, 5-10 minutes.
