Spicy Baked Beans

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (28 ounces each) baked beans
  • 1 can (20 ounces) unsweetened crushed pineapple, drained
  • 1 C spicy barbecue sauce
  • ½ C molasses
  • 2 tbs prepared mustard
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 can (6 ounces) french-fried onions, crushed, divided
  • 5 bacon strips, cooked and crumbled, divided

Directions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the first 7 ingredients.
  2. Stir in half of the onions and bacon. Transfer to a greased 13x9" baking dish.
  3. Cover and bake at 350° for 45 minutes.
  4. Sprinkle with remaining onions and bacon.
  5. Bake, uncovered, 5-10 minutes longer or until bubbly.
