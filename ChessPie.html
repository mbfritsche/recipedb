Chess Pie

Ingredients

  • ½ C butter, melted and cooled
  • 2 C white sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 large eggs
  • ¼ C evaporated milk
  • 1 tbs cornmeal
  • 1 tbs distilled white vinegar
  • 1 prepared pie shell

Directions

  1. Prep: Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Mix Filling: In a large bowl, whisk melted butter, sugar, and vanilla until smooth. Beat in eggs one at a time, then stir in evaporated milk, cornmeal, and vinegar until fully combined.
  3. Assemble: Pour filling into the prepared pie shell and place on a baking sheet.
  4. Bake: Bake until center is set and top is golden brown, about 40-50 minutes.
  5. Cool & Serve: Let cool completely before slicing and serving.
