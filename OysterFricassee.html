Oyster Fricassee

Ingredients

  • 1 qt shucked oysters
  • ¾ C butter, divided
  • 2 medium onions, chopped
  • 1½ C chopped celery
  • ½ C all-purpose flour
  • 2 C half-and-half
  • 2 tsp minced fresh parsley
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp minced fresh thyme or ½ tsp dried thyme
  • ¼ tsp pepper
  • Pinch cayenne pepper
  • 4 large egg yolks, lightly beaten
  • 2 C crushed Ritz crackers (about 50 crackers)
  • Lemon wedges
  • Fresh thyme sprigs

Directions

  1. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Prep Oysters: Drain oysters, reserving the oyster liquor; set oysters aside.
  3. Cook Base: In a large saucepan over medium heat, melt ½ C butter. Add onions and celery; cook 4-6 minutes, stirring, until tender.
  4. Make Roux: Stir in flour until blended. Gradually whisk in cream, bring to a boil whisking constantly, and cook ~2 minutes until thickened.
  5. Build Sauce: Reduce heat and add the next 5 ingredients along with the reserved oyster liquor. Cook and stir ~2 minutes until smooth.
  6. Temper Yolks: Remove from heat. Stir a small amount of hot sauce into egg yolks to temper, then return yolks to the pan, stirring constantly.
  7. Layer: Pour ½ of the sauce into a greased 13x9" baking dish. Top with ½ of the oysters and ½ of the cracker crumbs. Repeat layers.
  8. Top & Bake: Melt remaining butter and drizzle over the top. Bake uncovered 23-28 minutes, until golden brown.
  9. Rest & Serve: Let stand 10 minutes. Serve with lemon wedges and thyme sprigs.
