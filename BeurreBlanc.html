Beurre Blanc

Ingredients

  • ¼ C cream
  • ¼ C shallots, finely minced
  • ¼ C white wine or vermouth
  • ¼ C lemon juice or white wine vinegar
  • 4 oz unsalted butter, cubed
  • Salt and white pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Reduce Base: In a non-aluminum saucepan, combine shallots and liquid ingredients. Warm over medium-low heat and reduce by half.
  2. Add Cream: Stir in cream and continue reducing until syrupy.
  3. Finish with Butter: Remove from heat and whisk in butter one piece at a time until fully emulsified.
  4. Serve or Hold: Serve immediately, or hold gently in a double boiler over barely simmering water or in a thermos.
