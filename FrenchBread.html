French Bread

Ingredients

  • 690 g all-purpose flour
  • 532 g warm water
  • 1 tbs yeast
  • 2 tbs sugar
  • 2¼ tsp salt
  • 2 tbs olive oil

Directions

  1. Proof Yeast: In a small bowl, combine warm water, yeast, and sugar; let sit until foamy.
  2. Mix Dough: In a stand mixer with a dough hook, add 3 cups flour, salt, oil, and yeast mixture. Mix on medium, gradually adding 2½-3 more cups flour until a soft dough forms.
  3. Knead: Knead on medium-high about 10 minutes, until dough pulls away from the bowl. Add a little flour if sticky.
  4. First Rise: Place dough in a greased bowl, cover, and proof 90 minutes.
  5. Shape Loaves: Turn dough onto a floured surface, divide in half. Roll each into a 14-16" rectangle, press out air, roll tightly, pinch seams, and place seam-side down on a parchment-lined baking sheet, spaced apart.
  6. Second Rise: Score loaves diagonally with a serrated knife, cover, and let rise until doubled (~60 minutes).
  7. Bake: Preheat oven to 400°F. Bake on the middle rack 20-25 minutes until golden brown.
  8. Finish: Remove from oven and brush with melted butter if desired. Slice and serve warm.

Chef's Notes

  • Instant yeast can replace active dry yeast directly. Rapid-rise yeast does not need to be proofed.
  • Store bread wrapped in a cloth or inside a paper bag to keep the crust crisp without drying out.
  • Plastic bags trap moisture and will soften the crust.
