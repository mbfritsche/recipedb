Spicy Pork with Peas

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 tsp Szechuan peppercorn ground (optional)
  • 1 tbs cornstarch
  • 3 C fresh or canned chicken stock or broth
  • 2 tsp sesame oil
  • 3 C soy sauce
  • 2 tbs oyster sauce
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 3 tbs vegetable oil
  • 2¼ pounds pork, sliced and cut into thin strips
  • 1 onion, halved and sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, peeled and chopped
  • 4 quarter-size slices fresh ginger, julienned
  • 2 carrots, julienned
  • 5 green onions, sliced on the diagonal
  • ½ C frozen green peas, thawed, or fresh uncooked
  • 2 C cooked rice
  • 2 tbs chopped fresh parsley (optional)
  • 2 canned plum tomatoes or 1 medium fresh tomato, chopped

Directions

  1. Mix Sauce: In a small bowl, combine red pepper flakes, Szechuan pepper, cornstarch, chicken stock, sesame oil, soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sugar. Set aside.
  2. Heat Wok: Heat 2 tbs oil in a large wok over high heat until very hot.
  3. Brown Pork: Add pork and stir-fry 3-4 minutes until browned. Remove to a plate.
  4. Sauté Aromatics: Add remaining 1 tbs oil, then onion, garlic, ginger, and carrots. Stir-fry 2-3 minutes until crisp-tender.
  5. Thicken Sauce: Add sauce mixture and stir until it comes to a boil and thickens.
  6. Finish: Return pork and add peas; heat through ~1 minute.
  7. Serve: Garnish with parsley and chopped tomato if desired, and serve over steamed rice.
