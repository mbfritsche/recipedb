Prime Rib

Ingredients

  • 4-6 lb boneless prime rib
  • 3 tbs kosher salt
  • 2 tbs pepper corns, freshly cracked
  • 2 tbs beef fat or beef tallow

Directions

  1. Prep Roast: Wash and dry the roast. Wrap in cheesecloth and refrigerate uncovered for 3-5 days. On cooking day, unwrap and bring to room temperature.
  2. Trim & Season: Trim fat cap to about ¼" and score the top. Coat with beef fat or oil and season generously with salt and pepper.
  3. Slow Roast: Place on a rack in a cold oven. Heat oven to 250°F and roast until internal temperature reaches about 118–120°F, about 3 hours.
  4. Rest & Sear: Remove roast and rest briefly. Sear on all sides in a hot pan.
  5. Finish: Transfer to a 500°F oven and cook until internal temperature reaches about 128°F.
  6. Serve: Rest, slice, and serve.

Chef's Notes

  • Mix salt and cracked black pepper together before seasoning to ensure even distribution.
  • Rub roast with beef fat, then apply seasoning mixture evenly, pressing it into the surface.
  • This method allows fond to develop in the pan to create a rich base for an excellent pan sauce
  • Cook to preferred temperature; about 128°F yields a perfect medium, but adjust as desired.
