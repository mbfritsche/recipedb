Miso Black Cod

Ingredients

  • 4 150 g Black cod filets
  • 1 C white miso
  • 135 g sugar
  • 85 ml Mirin
  • 60 ml Sake
  • 3 tbs soy sauce
  • 1 tbs Sesame oil

Directions

  1. Marinate Cod: Combine all marinade ingredients in a bowl. Add cod fillets, turning to coat, and marinate 8 hours.
  2. Prep Pan: Remove cod from the marinade, leaving a light coating on the fillets. Place cod on a rack set over a sheet pan lined with oiled foil for easy cleanup.
  3. Broil: Broil 8-12 minutes, positioning the pan about 8 inches from the heat source, until just cooked through.
  4. Finish: Remove any pin bones before serving.

Chef's Notes

  • This will also work in an Air Fryer - 6 minutes @400 (check @4min)
