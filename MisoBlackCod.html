Miso Black Cod

Ingredients

  • 4 150 g Black cod filets
  • 1 C white miso
  • 135 g sugar
  • 85 ml Mirin
  • 60 ml Sake
  • 3 tbs soy sauce
  • 1 tbs Sesame oil

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients, add the cod filets, and marinate 8 hours
  2. Remove the cod from marinade, leaving some redidual marinade on the filets
  3. Place the cod on a rack on a sheet pan covered with oiled foil (to make cleanup easier)
  4. Broil 8-12 min about 8" from heat source. Remove pin bones before service

Chef's Notes

  • This will also work in an Air Fryer - 6 minutes @400 (check @4min)
