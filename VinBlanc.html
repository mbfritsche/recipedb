Vin Blanc Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 qt fish velouté
  • ½ C dry white wine
  • ½ C heavy cream, hot
  • 2 tbs cold butter
  • Salt, white pepper, and lemon juice to taste

Directions

  1. Reduce Wine: Place wine in a small saucepan and reduce by half over medium‑low heat.
  2. Add Velouté: Stir in velouté and simmer until reduced to the desired consistency.
  3. Incorporate Cream: Slowly stir in hot or tempered cream until fully combined.
  4. Finish: Remove from heat and swirl in raw butter, adding it in chunks until melted.
  5. Season & Strain: Season to taste with salt, white pepper, and lemon juice. Strain through cheesecloth and serve.
