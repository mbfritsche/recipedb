Poppy Seed Dressing

Ingredients

  • 300 g sugar
  • 3 g dry mustard
  • 12 g salt
  • 160 ml C white vinegar
  • 45 ml onion juice
  • 480 ml vegetable oil
  • 27 g poppy seeds

Directions

  1. Mix Base: Combine sugar, mustard, salt, and vinegar. Stir in onion juice until fully blended.
  2. Emulsify: Slowly add oil while beating constantly until the dressing thickens.
  3. Finish: Add poppy seeds and beat a few minutes more.
  4. Store: Refrigerate until ready to use. Makes about 3½ cups.

Chef's Notes

  • Any neutral oil will work, but never olive oil
  • Best made with an electric mixer or blender on medium speed, though it can be done by hand.
  • For the onion juice, grate a large white onion on a fine grater or blend it, then strain.
  • To prevent graininess, heat vinegar and sugar together until sugar dissolves, then cool before mixing.
  • If the dressing separates, pour off the clear liquid and re-emulsify by beating again, slowly adding the mixture back in.
