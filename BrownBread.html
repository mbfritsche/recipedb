Brown Bread

Ingredients

  • 295 ml warm water
  • 7 g instant dry yeast
  • 4 g sugar
  • 220 g bread flour
  • 230 g whole wheat flour
  • 13 g brown sugar
  • 7.5 g cocoa powder
  • 6 g salt
  • 40.5 ml canola oil
  • 85 g honey
  • 63 g molasses
  • 4 g white cornmeal
  • Oats for garnish

Directions

  1. Activate Yeast: Mix water, yeast, and sugar; let sit 3-5 minutes until frothy.
  2. Make Dough: In a stand mixer, combine flours, brown sugar, cocoa, and salt. Add yeast mixture, oil, honey, and molasses. Mix with dough hook for ~5 minutes, adding flour as needed until smooth and non-sticky.
  3. First Rise: Shape into a ball, spray lightly, and let rise in a warm spot for 1-1½ hours until doubled.
  4. Prep Pan: Line baking sheet or French bread pan with parchment and sprinkle cornmeal.
  5. Shape Loaves: Punch down dough, divide into 4 pieces, roll into rectangles, then shape into 8-9" logs. Pinch ends to seal.
  6. Second Rise: Place on prepared sheet, score tops, brush with water, sprinkle oats. Cover and rise 30-40 minutes until nearly doubled.
  7. Bake: Preheat oven to 350°F with an empty sheet on the bottom rack. Add ice cubes to bottom sheet for steam when placing loaves on the middle rack. Bake 25-30 minutes (internal temp 190-200°F).
