Bacon and Butternut Squash Mac & Cheese

Ingredients

  • ½ box whole grain elbow macaroni (about 2.5 cups dry)
  • 1 cup canned butternut squash puree
  • ¼ C + 2 tablespoons evaporated milk
  • ¼ C chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 oz low fat cream cheese
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 C shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • ½ C onions, sliced thinly
  • 1 large crispy apple, chopped or grated
  • 4 tablespoons crumbled bacon

Directions

  1. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Caramelize Onions: Melt butter in a skillet over low to low‑medium heat. Add onions and cook at least 30 minutes until deeply caramelized.
  3. Cook Pasta: Cook pasta per package directions, drain, and return to the pot over medium-low heat.
  4. Build Sauce: Add butternut squash, chicken broth, evaporated milk, and cream cheese to the pasta. Stir until combined; season with salt.
  5. Combine: Stir in caramelized onions and apples. Add a little more broth or milk if needed.
  6. Bake: Mix in cheese, transfer to the oven, and bake uncovered for 20 minutes.
  7. Serve: Top with bacon and serve hot.
