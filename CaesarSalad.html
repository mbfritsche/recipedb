Caesar Salad

Ingredients

  • ½ head chilled romaine lettuce
  • ¼ C onions, finely chopped
  • 1 tbs garlic (2-3 cloves, minced)
  • 2-3 anchovy filets
  • 2 raw eggs
  • ¼ C red vinegar
  • ¼ C olive oil
  • ¼ C croutons
  • ¼ C parmesan cheese
  • 2 tsp salt

Directions

  1. Season Bowl: Add salt to a salad bowl. Add onions and crush with two forks against the bowl until broken down and fragrant.
  2. Build Base: Crush garlic and anchovies into the onions, one at a time, using the same method.
  3. Assemble: Add eggs, salad oil, vinegar, and lettuce. Toss well to coat the leaves.
  4. Finish: Add Parmesan and croutons; toss again.
  5. Serve: Serve immediately, finishing each portion with freshly ground black pepper.

Chef's Notes

  • If cholesterol is a concern, one egg is sufficient for this recipe
