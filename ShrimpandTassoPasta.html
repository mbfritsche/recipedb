Shrimp and Tasso Pasta

Ingredients

  • ½ lb tasso, diced
  • 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1 pint heavy cream
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  • ¼ tsp white pepper
  • ½ tsp basil
  • ½ tsp thyme
  • 1 lb spaghetti
  • ½ C green onions, chopped
  • ½ C parsley, chopped

Directions

  1. Make Cream Sauce: Pour cream into a large, heavy skillet over medium heat. Stir when the cream begins to rise to prevent boiling over.
  2. Season & Simmer: When the cream comes to a boil, add tasso, salt, peppers, and herbs. Reduce heat and simmer 8-10 minutes, until the sauce is fairly thick. (Sauce can be made ahead to this point.)
  3. Cook Pasta: Prepare spaghetti according to package directions, drain, and divide among serving bowls.
  4. Finish Sauce: Return sauce to a gentle simmer. Add shrimp, green onions, and parsley; cook 3-4 minutes, until shrimp turn pink.
  5. Serve: Ladle sauce over pasta, toss gently, and finish with freshly grated Parmesan cheese.

Chef's Notes

  • You can substitute 1 lb crawfish tails, 1 lb scallops, or 2 dz fresh shucked oysters for the shrimp in this recipe.
  • If you can't get tasso, you can substitute a cured smoked ham.
