Grilled Pepper and Black Olive Relish (V)

Ingredients

  • ¼ C pitted black olives, chopped
  • ¼ C grilled red bell peppers, seeded and diced
  • ¼ C grilled yellow peppers seeded and diced
  • 2 tbs crushed red pepper flakes
  • 1 tbs fresh garlic
  • 2 tbs fresh thyme
  • ¼ C chopped fresh parsley
  • 2 tbs sherry vinegar
  • 1 tbs honey
  • 3 tbs olive oil
  • Salt and freshly ground pepper to taste

Directions

  1. In a mixing bowl combine all ingredients and season to taste with salt and freshly ground pepper. Serve with grilled chicken or fish.
