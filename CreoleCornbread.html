Creole Cornbread

Ingredients

  • 2 qt water
  • 2 C cooked rice
  • 1 C yellow cornmeal
  • ½ C chopped onion
  • 1 to 2 tbs seeded chopped jalapeno peppers
  • 1 tbs salt
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1 C whole milk
  • ¼ C canola oil
  • 1 can (16½ oz) cream-style corn
  • 3 C shredded cheddar cheese
  • Additional cornmeal, as needed

Directions

  1. Mix Dry: In a large bowl, combine rice, cornmeal, onion, peppers, salt, and baking soda.
  2. Mix Wet: In a separate bowl, whisk eggs, milk, and oil together.
  3. Combine: Stir corn into the wet mixture, then add to the rice mixture and blend well. Gently fold in the cheese.
  4. Prep Pan: Grease a 10" ovenproof skillet and sprinkle lightly with cornmeal. Pour in the batter.
  5. Bake: Bake at 350°F for 45-50 minutes, until a knife inserted in the center comes out clean.
  6. Serve: Cut into wedges and serve warm.
