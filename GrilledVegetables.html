Grilled Vegetables (V)

Ingredients

  • 1 large eggplant
  • 1 large yellow squash
  • 1 large zuchini
  • 4 tbs teriyaki sauce
  • 8 oz fat-free Italian dressing
  • 2 tbs Worcestershire sauce (see Chef's Notes)
  • 1 tbs Cajun seasoning

Directions

  1. Prep Vegetables: Wash vegetables, trim tops, and slice lengthwise into ¼" slices. Place in a large bowl.
  2. Marinate: Add dressing, teriyaki sauce, Worcestershire sauce, and Cajun seasoning. Mix well to coat. Let marinate for at least 2 hours or overnight.
  3. Grill: Place vegetable slices directly on the grill. Baste with remaining marinade while cooking.
  4. Serve: Grill until golden and slightly crisp, then serve immediately as an appetizer or side dish.

Chef's Notes

  • This recipe can be vegan by substituting umami sauce, tamari, or tamarind sauce for the Worcestershire sauce.
