Sweet Potato Chili (V)

Ingredients

  • 2 lb sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into bite-sized chunks (about 4 cups)
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 medium carrots, peeled and thinly sliced
  • 1 medium green bell pepper, seeded and chopped
  • 1 medium red bell pepper, seeded and chopped
  • 2 tbs canola oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1½ C unsalted roasted peanuts
  • 28 oz Roma tomatoes, crushed
  • 6 oz tomato paste
  • 8 oz diced mild green chiles with liquid
  • 4-6 chili powder, to taste
  • 1 tbs ground cumin, to taste
  • 1 tbs sugar
  • Salt and freshly ground pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Sauté Base: In a large pot, heat canola oil over medium heat. Add onion, carrots, and bell peppers and cook until lightly golden, stirring occasionally.
  2. Add Garlic: Stir in garlic and cook briefly until fragrant.
  3. Build: Add sweet potatoes, peanuts, tomatoes with juices, tomato paste, chiles, chili powder, cumin, and sugar. Stir to combine.
  4. Simmer: Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer gently until sweet potatoes are tender, about 15-25 minutes.
  5. Season & Serve: Adjust seasoning with additional spices, salt, and pepper as needed. Serve hot.
