Beef Seasoning Mix

Ingredients

  • ¼ C salt
  • ¼ C paprika
  • 1½ tbs garlic powder
  • 1½ tbs onion powder
  • 1¼ tbs red pepper
  • 1 tbs white pepper
  • 1 tbs black pepper
  • 1 tsp dry mustard
  • 1 tsp sweet basil
  • ½ tsp file powder
  • ¼ tsp ground bay leaves
  • dash thyme

Directions

  1. Mix all ingredients together and store in a glass jar. Use as needed in beef recipes as a replacement for salt and pepper.
