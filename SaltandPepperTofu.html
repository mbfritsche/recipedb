Salt and Pepper Tofu

Ingredients

  • 16 oz firm tofu, drained and cubed
  • 3 stalks green onion (white part only), diced
  • 2 oz mini bell pepper, diced
  • 2 clove garlic, minced
  • 4 C boiling water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp garlic salt
  • ¼ tsp white pepper
  • ½ egg
  • 6 tbs cornstarch
  • 16 oz oil (for deep-frying)
  • 2 tbs oil (for stir-frying; can re-use oil from deep-fry step)

Directions

  1. Parboil Tofu: Add cubed tofu to salted boiling water. Cover and cook 3 minutes, then lower heat, uncover, and cook 2 minutes more. Drain in a colander.
  2. Dry: Allow tofu to cool, then dry thoroughly with paper towels.
  3. Coat: Toss tofu with beaten egg to coat evenly, then dredge completely in cornstarch.
  4. Seasoning Mix: Combine garlic salt and ¼ tsp white pepper; set aside for finishing.
  5. Deep‑Fry: Heat oil to 400°F / 200°C. Fry tofu in batches, undisturbed at first, until golden and crisp (~5 minutes). Remove to a wire rack.
  6. Aromatics: Heat 2 tbs oil in a wok on high. Fry dried chili briefly (~8 seconds), then remove. Add garlic and fry 15 seconds, then bell peppers and green onion whites for 20–25 seconds.
  7. Finish: Add fried tofu and stir-fry 10 seconds. Season with the garlic-pepper mix until evenly coated.
  8. Serve: Serve immediately

Chef's Notes

  • A vegetarian recipe as written - using a plant-based alternative to the egg will make it vegan
