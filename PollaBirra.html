Polla Birra

Ingredients

  • 2 medium boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 medium onion, small dice
  • 12 oz. imported beer (preferably dark)
  • 2 tbs olive oil
  • 2 tbs capers
  • 2 small carrots, thinly sliced (OPTIONAL)
  • black pepper (to taste)
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 lemon, thinly sliced
  • 12 oz. fettuccine

Directions

  1. Cook Pasta: Prepare pasta according to package directions and set aside.
  2. Sauté Onion: Heat olive oil in a medium saucepan and sauté onion until softened; halfway through, add oregano and stir. Cook until onions are translucent.
  3. Brown Chicken: Add chicken and brown well on both sides. Season with pepper, add beer and capers. (If using carrots, add them now.)
  4. Simmer: Bring beer to a boil, reduce heat, cover, and simmer 20 minutes, turning chicken occasionally.
  5. Finish: Uncover and cook 10 minutes more.
  6. Serve: Serve immediately over pasta, garnished with Parmesan, parsley, and lemon slices.
