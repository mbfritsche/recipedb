Corn Relish (V)

Ingredients

  • 1 C grilled corn kernels
  • 1 red bell pepper, finely diced
  • 1 green pepper, finely diced
  • 2 tbs red onion, finely diced
  • 1½ tbs balsamic vinegar
  • 1½ tbs coarsely chopped cilantro
  • ½ jalapeno minced
  • 1½ tbs olive oil
  • Salt and freshly ground pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Grill Corn: Remove all silk and all but one layer of husk. Dip briefly in water (do not soak), then grill until cooked.
  2. Cut & Mix: Cut kernels from the cob and combine with peppers, onion, vinegar, cilantro, jalapeño, and oil.
  3. Season & Serve: Season with salt and pepper to taste.
