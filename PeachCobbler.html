Peach Cobbler

Ingredients

  • 4 oz butter (1 stick)
  • 1 C flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 C sugar
  • 1 C milk
  • 29-oz can peaches with juice

Directions

  1. Prep: Melt butter in a 9x13" pan in the oven as it preheats to 400°F.
  2. Mix: Stir flour, baking powder, sugar, and milk directly into the melted butter in the pan.
  3. Add Fruit: Layer sliced peaches and their juice over the batter without stirring.
  4. Bake: Bake until golden brown, about 35 minutes.
  5. Serve: Serve warm with whipped cream or a scoop of vanilla ice cream for a special treat.
