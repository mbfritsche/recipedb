Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 2¼ C flour
  • ¾ C sugar
  • ¾ C brown sugar
  • ½ lb butter (2 sticks)
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 12 oz chocolate chips
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ C chopped nuts (optional)

Directions

  1. Mix Wet: Melt butter. Stir in sugars, then add eggs one at a time, mixing well. Add vanilla and blend thoroughly.
  2. Add Dry: Mix in flour, salt, and baking soda until smooth.
  3. Fold In: Stir in chocolate chips, peanut butter chips, mint chips, and nuts to taste.
  4. Bake: Scoop dough onto a baking sheet in 2-tbs mounds, spacing about ½" apart. Bake at 3757degF for 7-9 minutes.
  5. Cool: Transfer to a wire rack to cool.
