Split Pea Soup

Ingredients

  • 1 package dry green split peas
  • ¼ C vegetable oil
  • ½ lb bacon, cut into small dice
  • ½ lb ham, cut into small dice
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 1 shallot, chopped
  • 2 stalks celery, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 carrot, cut into small dice
  • 2 small yellow potatoes, cut into small dice
  • 4 C vegetable stock
  • 1 can of light beer
  • 1 C water
  • 1 tbs English mustard (Colman's, if possible)
  • Salt, pepper and creole seasoning to taste

Directions

  1. Prep Peas: Wash split peas thoroughly and drain well.
  2. Render Bacon: Heat oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add bacon and fry until fully rendered.
  3. Sauté Base: Add onions, celery, garlic, and ham. Cook until vegetables are wilted and ham is browned. Season with English mustard and Creole seasoning to taste.
  4. Simmer Soup: Add split peas, water, and chicken stock. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low.
  5. Cook Down: Simmer 1–1½ hrs, until peas are nearly dissolved.
  6. Add Vegetables: Add carrots and potatoes ~30 minutes before finishing. Add water if soup becomes too thick.
  7. Finish: Season with salt and pepper to taste and serve hot.

Chef's Notes

  • This is an American comfort food classic I made once a month when I lived in Seattle.
