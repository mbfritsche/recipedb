Veal Seasoning Mix

Ingredients

  • ¼ C salt
  • 2¼ tbs paprika
  • 2¼ tbs onion powder
  • 1½ tsp red pepper
  • 1½ tsp black pepper
  • 1¼ tsp white pepper
  • 1¼ tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp dry hot mustard
  • ¼ tsp file powder
  • ¼ tsp ground bay leaves
  • ¼ tsp celery seed
  • ¼ tsp rosemary
  • ¼ tsp coriander

Directions

  1. Mix all ingredients together and store in a glass jar. Use as needed in veal recipes as a replacement for salt and pepper.
