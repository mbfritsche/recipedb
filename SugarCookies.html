Sugar Cookies

Ingredients

  • 130 g shortening
  • ½ tsp grated orange peel
  • ¾ C sugar
  • 2 C sifted flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 4 tsp milk

Directions

  1. Cream Base: Cream sugar and shortening until light and fluffy. Beat in egg, vanilla, and orange peel.
  2. Add Dry & Milk: Combine dry ingredients with milk and mix until a soft dough forms.
  3. Chill Dough: Divide dough in half, wrap, and refrigerate 30 minutes.
  4. Roll & Cut: Roll dough to ½" thickness on a floured surface. Cut into shapes.
  5. Bake: Bake at 375°F for 12 minutes, until set and lightly golden.
