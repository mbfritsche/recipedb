Michael's Seafood Gumbo

Ingredients

  • 1 C flour
  • 1 C oil
  • 3-4 lbs head-on shrimp
  • 1 lb crawfish tails (peeled)
  • 2 onion, chopped
  • 1 stalk celery, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 can Rotel tomatoes
  • 6 green onion
  • 1 tbs parsley
  • 2-3 tsp salt
  • ½-1 tsp red, blk, white pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • To taste: Tabasco sauce, Louisiana hot sauce, Crab boil

Directions

  1. Make Shrimp Stock: Peel shrimp, reserving heads and shells. Cover shells and heads with water and boil ~2 hours to yield about 2 quarts stock. Strain twice to remove sediment.
  2. Make Roux: Cook oil and flour to a dark brown roux. Add onions, bell pepper, and celery; remove from heat and stir regularly until the mixture cools slightly.
  3. Build Gumbo Base: In a stockpot, heat shrimp stock with garlic powder. Whisk in the roux, then add Rotel tomatoes and ½ tsp each Louisiana hot sauce, Tabasco, and crab boil. Simmer 45 minutes.
  4. Add Seafood: Stir in shrimp, crawfish, and remaining seasonings. Simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Finish: Add parsley and green onions and simmer 5 minutes more.
  6. Serve: Top with gumbo filé and serve hot over rice.
