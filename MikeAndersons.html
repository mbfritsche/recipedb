Mike Anderson's House Dressing (V)

Ingredients

  • ½ C vegetable oil
  • ¼ C water
  • ¾ white vinegar
  • 2 tbs sugar
  • 2 tbs garlic, minced
  • 2 tbs green onions, chopped
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 3 tbs granulated garlic
  • 3 tbs Parmesan cheese, grated
  • ¾ C Romano cheese, shredded

Directions

  1. Mix: Combine all ingredients until evenly blended.
  2. Chill: Refrigerate until thoroughly chilled before serving.

Chef's Notes

  • This is a copycat recipe of Mike Anderson's house dressing
