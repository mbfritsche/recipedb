Lemon Bars

Ingredients

Shortbread Crust
  • 1 C all-purpose flour
  • ½ C unsalted butter at room temperature
  • ¼ C confectioners' sugar
  • ¼ tsp vanilla extract
  • ¼ tsp salt
Lemon Custard
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 large egg yolks
  • 1 C white sugar
  • 2 tbs all-purpose flour
  • ¼ C freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • 1 tbs freshly grated lemon zest
  • 1 tsp confectioners' sugar, or to taste (garnish)

Directions

Shortbread Crust
  1. Prep Oven & Pan: Set oven rack to the middle position and preheat oven to 350°F. Lightly grease an 8x8" baking dish.
  2. Make Crust: In a large bowl, mash flour and butter together using the back of a spatula or wooden spoon until fully combined. Mix in confectioners' sugar, vanilla, and salt until the mixture resembles slightly crumbly cookie dough.
  3. Press & Dock: Lightly moisten fingers and press the dough evenly into the bottom of the prepared dish. Prick the crust all over with a fork.
  4. Bake: Bake on the middle rack ~22 minutes, until the edges are just barely golden brown. Set aside to cool.
Lemon Custard
  1. Make Filling: Beat whole eggs and egg yolks until combined. Whisk in white sugar and flour until smooth.
  2. Flavor Custard: Add lemon juice and zest; whisk about 2 minutes until well incorporated.
  3. Bake Filling: Pour filling over the warm crust. Bake on the center rack ~25 minutes, until the custard is set and a thin white sugary crust forms on top.
  4. Cool: Remove from oven and let cool completely before cutting.
  5. Slice: Dip a knife in very hot water, wipe dry, run around the edges, and cut into 16 squares.
  6. Finish: Dust bars with confectioners' sugar and serve.

Chef's Notes

  • Line the bottom of your 8x8 pan with parchment paper and give it a light coat of butter for the best release
