Bread Pudding

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 1 can evaporated milk
  • ½ C milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp nutmeg
  • 1 C sugar
  • 4 C cubed French bread
  • 1 C raisins Directions

Directions

  1. Make Custard: Whisk eggs with milk products, vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg, and sugar until well combined.
  2. Assemble: Spread bread cubes and raisins evenly in a buttered 9x9x2" baking dish.
  3. Soak: Pour custard over the bread, ensuring everything is well soaked.
  4. Bake: Bake at 350°F for 45 minutes, until set and lightly browned.

Chef's Notes

  • Four cups of French bread cubes can be made from a 1 lb loaf of French bread
