Air Fryer Three Buddy Salmon

Ingredients

  • 400 g salmon filet
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 30 g cilantro stems
  • 3 bird's eye chilis
  • 1½ tsp white pepper corns
  • 1 tbs Seasoning soy sauce
  • 1 tbs fish sauce
  • 1 tbs oyster sauce
  • 1½ tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp Mirin
  • 1 tbs Avocado oil
  • 2 tsp lime juice (optional)

Directions

  1. Make Marinade: Mix soy sauce, oyster sauce, fish sauce, sugar, Mirin, oil, and lime juice.
  2. Add Aromatics: Crush peppercorns, garlic, cilantro, and chilis into a coarse paste; stir into marinade.
  3. Marinate Salmon: Place salmon in a bowl and coat thoroughly with marinade while the air fryer heats.
  4. Preheat Air Fryer: Heat air fryer to 425°F on Air Crisp with the baking vessel inside so it gets fully hot.
  5. Cook: Place salmon in the preheated vessel (it should sizzle). Spoon remaining marinade over the top and cook for 7 minutes.
  6. Serve: Transfer to a serving dish and garnish with cilantro leaves and finely chopped bird's eye chili

Chef's Notes

  • The thinner edges should turn dark brown after about 7 minutes, mostly due to the sugar. The sweetness balances the salty, savory sauces.
  • Butterfly thicker fillets if you want more browned edges.
  • A teaspoon of maple syrup is an excellent optional addition.
  • Total time from start to finish is about 15-20 minutes.
