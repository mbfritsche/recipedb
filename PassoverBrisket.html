Passover Brisket

Ingredients

  • 4-5 lb beef brisket
  • 15 small red potatoes
  • 1½ lb baby carrots
  • ¾ C dark brown sugar
  • ¾ C chili sauce
  • ¾ C ketchup
  • 1 envelope dry onion soup mix
  • 12 oz beer

Directions

  1. Prep Oven & Sauce: Preheat oven to 325°F. Mix brown sugar, chili sauce, ketchup, and dry onion soup mix; gently stir in beer.
  2. Assemble: Place brisket fat-side up in a Dutch oven or roasting pan and pierce several times with a fork. Arrange carrots and potatoes around it and pour sauce over the top.
  3. Bake Covered: Cover and bake 3½-4 hours, basting occasionally.
  4. Rest: Remove from oven, uncover, and rest 20 minutes.
  5. Finish: Slice brisket, return slices to the pan with vegetables, and bake 30 minutes more.
  6. Serve: Serve hot with vegetables and sauce.
