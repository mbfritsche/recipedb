Horseradish Sauce

Spinach

Ingredients

  • ½ C sour cream
  • 2 tbs prepared horseradish, drained
  • 2 tbs mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
  • &fra14 tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbs chives, finely chopped

Directions

  1. Mix: Stir all ingredients together in a small mixing bowl until well combined.
  2. Chill: Refrigerate before serving to allow the flavors to blend. Serve immediately if desired.
  3. Storage: Covered and refrigerated, the mixture will keep for about 2 weeks.

Chef's Notes

  • Typically served cold but can be served at room temperature
