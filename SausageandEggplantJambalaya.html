Sausage and Eggplant Jambalaya

Ingredients

  • ½ lb ground pork sausage
  • ½ lb smoked sausage
  • 4 medium eggplant, peeled and chopped
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 stalk celery, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, chopped
  • ½ bunch green onions, chopped
  • 2 tbs parsley
  • 4 tbs oil
  • 2 C uncooked rice
  • 3 C chicken stock or water
  • Salt, pepper and hot sauce to taste

Directions

  1. Brown Meats: In a black iron pot, heat 2 tbs oil and brown the ground pork sausage. Remove and set aside. Repeat with the smoked sausage.
  2. Sauté Vegetables: Add onions, celery, bell pepper, garlic, and remaining oil to the pot. Sauté until vegetables are wilted, then add eggplant and cook until well softened.
  3. Add Rice & Liquid: Return meats to the pot. Stir in rice, seasonings, green onions, and stock.
  4. Simmer: Bring to a boil, reduce heat to lowest setting, cover, and cook 25-30 minutes, until rice is tender.
  5. Important: Stir only once during cooking to avoid gummy rice.
  6. Serve: Fluff gently and serve hot.
