Chicken Rochambeau

Ingredients

  • 4 slices toast
  • 4 large slices roast chicken
  • 4 large slices ham
  • ¼ lb fresh mushrooms
  • 1 tbs butter
  • 1 C beef stock
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • 1 tbs parsley, minced
  • 1 C Hollandaise sauce
  • 1 C Marchand du Vin
  • Dash Worcestershire sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Fry the ham and warm the chicken slices. As an alternative to roast chicken, you may grill ½ a chicken breast pounded thin for each person.
  2. Place the toast or an English muffin on the plate, then the ham, then a generous spoonful of Marchand du Vin, then the chicken, then top with the Hollandaise sauce
