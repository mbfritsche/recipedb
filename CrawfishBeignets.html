Crawfish Beignets

Ingredients

  • 1 C flour
  • 1 C water
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 6 green onion tops, chopped
  • 2 pimentos, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp Louisiana hot sauce
  • 1 lb peeled crawfish tails

Directions

  1. Make Batter: In a bowl, combine flour, water, and baking powder to form a smooth paste. Add remaining ingredients, mix well, cover with a damp towel, and rest ~30 minutes.
  2. Heat Oil: Heat 4 cups peanut oil in a medium pan to 325°F.
  3. Fry: Drop the crawfish mixture into the hot oil by tablespoonfuls and fry until golden brown.
  4. Drain & Serve: Transfer to a wire rack to drain. Serve hot as an appetizer with sweet-and-sour or ranch-style dipping sauce.
