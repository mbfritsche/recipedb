Marchand du Vin

Ingredients

  • 1 stick salted butter
  • 3 tbs flour
  • ¼ C minced lean baked ham
  • ½ C finely minces green onion tops
  • ½ C mushrooms, minced
  • 3 tbs finely minced onion
  • 4 tbs finely minced garlic
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • pinch cayenne pepper
  • ½ C dry red wine
  • 1 C rich beef stock from soup bones
  • Marrow from the soup bones

Directions

  1. Start Roux: In a large, heavy saucepan over low heat, melt butter. Gradually whisk in flour, stirring constantly, until the roux turns light brown.
  2. Add Aromatics: Quickly stir in ham, green onion tops, mushrooms, onion, and garlic. Cook ~5 minutes, stirring constantly.
  3. Season: Add salt, pepper, and cayenne; blend thoroughly.
  4. Build Sauce: Keep the mixture gently simmering and gradually add beef stock, bone marrow, and red wine, stirring constantly to keep the sauce smooth.
  5. Simmer: Cook over very low heat ~30 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent scorching.
  6. Yield: About 2 cups
