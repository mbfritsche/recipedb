Remoulade Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 C mayonnaise
  • 4 tbs brown mustard
  • 2 tbs fresh lemon juice
  • 1-2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbs capers, finely chopped
  • 2 tbs cornichons or dill pickles, finely chopped
  • 1 tbs fresh parsley, finely chopped
  • 1 tbs fresh chives, finely chopped
  • 1 tbs prepared horseradish
  • 1-2 tsp Tabasco
  • 1 tsp paprika (smoked or regular)
  • 1 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • ½ tsp sugar, or more to taste
  • Salt, if needed, and black pepper to taste

Directions

  2. Mix: Combine all ingredients and mix well, adjusting to taste. Add more sugar or Tabasco, if desired, but additional salt is often unnecessary.
  3. Optional Blend: For a smoother texture, pulse everything in a small food processor until evenly incorporated, scraping down the sides as needed.
  4. Chill or Use: Use immediately or refrigerate; the sauce will thicken slightly as it chills.
  5. Store: Keep refrigerated in an airtight container for 1-2 weeks.

Chef's Notes

  • A classic South Louisiana sauce
