Chili Rotel Dip

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Velveeta cheese
  • 1 can Rotel tomatoes
  • 1 10-oz can Wolf Brand Chili without beans

Directions

  1. Melt Cheese: Microwave cheese in a microwave-safe container on high until melted, about 3 minutes.
  2. Mix & Serve: Stir in Rotel tomatoes and chili until well combined. Serve immediately with tortilla chips, crackers, or toasty French bread

Chef's Notes

  • Although the recipe mentions name‑brand ingredients, it works just as well with store brands and still turns out great.
