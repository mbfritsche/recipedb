Chili Rotel Dip

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Velveeta cheese
  • 1 can Rotel tomatoes
  • 1 10-oz can Wolf Brand Chili without beans

Directions

  1. Microwave the cheese until melted in a microwaveable container, about 3 minutes on high.
  2. Add the Rotel tomatoes and the chili and stir thoroughly. Serve immediately with tortilla chips or crackers.
