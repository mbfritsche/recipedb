LA Galbi (Korean Beef Short Ribs)

Ingredients

  • 1 kg LA galbi ribs
  • 1 small shallot, diced
  • 6 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 2" knob of ginger, peeled and chopped
  • 140 g Asian pear, peeled and chopped
  • 240 ml soy sauce or coconut aminos
  • 120 ml Mirin
  • 30 ml rice vinegar
  • 30 ml sesame oil
  • 30 ml honey
  • 25 g brown sugar
  • 4 g black pepper
  • 5 g toasted sesame seeds
  • 2 green onion, finely chopped

Directions

  1. Prep Ribs: Rinse ribs in cold water with a little baking soda, then pat dry.
  2. Make Marinade: Add shallot, garlic, ginger, and pear to an immersion blender cup. Add soy sauce, mirin, rice vinegar, sesame oil, sugar, and honey; blend until smooth.
  3. Marinate: Place ribs in an airtight container, pour marinade over top, seal, and refrigerate at least 1 hour (up to 24 hours).
  4. Air Fry: Arrange ribs in a single layer in the air-fryer tray. Cook at 350°F for 7 minutes per side, adding extra marinade halfway through.
  5. Serve: Transfer ribs to a platter, spoon the remaining cooking marinade over top, and garnish with chopped green onions and sesame seeds.

Chef's Notes

  • Coconut aminos is a less salty soy-free alternative to soy sauce
  • Apple or Bosc pear works well in place of Asian pear.
  • The recipe halves easily for smaller batches.
  • A regular blender works fine if making a larger quantity.
  • Extra marinade can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for future use.
  • Don't overcrowd the air fryer - cook ribs in batches if needed and keep finished ribs warm in a low oven.
