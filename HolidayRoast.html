Holiday Roast

Ingredients

  • 3½ lb French roast or Chuck roast
  • 360 g Tomato, diced
  • 250 g Carrot, cut into ½" sticks
  • 300 gGolden baby potatoes, halved
  • 300 g Purple baby potatoes, halved
  • 300 g Yellow onion, quartered
  • 300 g Red onion, quartered
  • 200 g Celery, cut into ½" sticks
  • 4 tbs paprika
  • 2 tbs salt
  • 1 tbs black pepper
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 6 apple wedges
  • ½ C Olive oil
  • Drizzle of silan (date molasses)
  • Drizzle of honey
  • A few sprigs each of thyme, rosemary, and sage

Directions

  1. Prep Roast: Wash the roast and pat completely dry. Peel and cut all vegetables.
  2. Season: Add spices and olive oil. Using your hands, massage the seasonings over the roast and vegetables until evenly coated.
  3. Assemble: Place the roast in the center of a roasting pan and arrange the vegetables around it.
  4. Add Sweetness: Tuck apple wedges among the vegetables. Drizzle silan and honey over the roast and vegetables.
  5. Add Herbs: Place the herb bouquet on top of the roast.
  6. Cover: Cover first with parchment paper, then with foil.
  7. Bake: Bake at 325°F until tender, about 3 hours.
  8. Finish: Remove the covers and continue baking for about 30 minutes, basting once or twice with the pan juices.
  9. Optional: Broil briefly at the end for additional color.
  10. Serve: Slice the roast and serve with the vegetables and pan juices.

Chef's Notes

  • Recipe works for many cuts of beef. Feel free to use your favorite
  • Silan may not be readily available. Good substitutes include dark maple syrup, agave nectar, or pomegranate molasses.
