Air Fryer Three Buddy Chicken

Ingredients

  • 600-700g boneless chicken thighs
  • 20g coriander roots or stems
  • 20g garlic
  • 1 tbs white peppercorns
  • Thinly sliced lemongrass (Optional)
  • 4½ tbs regular soy sauce
  • 2 tbs fish sauce
  • 1 tbs oyster sauce
  • 1 tbs black soy sauce
  • 20g cane sugar or palm sugar

Directions

  2. Blend the marinade: Pound or blend the coriander roots, garlic, and lemongrass into a paste, then mix with the soy sauces, white pepper, and sugar.
  3. Marinate: Coat the chicken thighs thoroughly and let them rest in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes to absorb the flavors. Overnight is best.
  4. Air fry: Preheat your air fryer to 375°F-390°F. Cook the chicken for 16 to 25 minutes, depending on thickness, flipping or shaking the basket halfway through until the edges are nicely browned and caramelized.
  5. Serve: Slice the warm chicken and serve with sticky rice

Chef's Notes

  • Bone-in thighs take longer to cook than boneless thighs. Check doneness with a meat thermometer inserted into the thickest part of the meat near the bone. Chicken is done when it reaches an internal temperature of 165°F.
  • If your air fryer requires multiple batches, save any pan juices by adding 2-3 ounces of water to the basket or drip pan after each batch and scraping up the browned bits. Add the collected liquid to the finished dish to boost flavor and help keep the chicken moist.
