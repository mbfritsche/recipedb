Liver with Chili and Garlic

Ingredients

  • 500 g Chicken liver
  • 30 g garlic
  • 10 g bird's eye chili (3:1 with garlic)
  • 1 whole spring of basil
  • 20 g spur chilies (red) – Anaheims will substitute
  • 2 tbs oyster sauce
  • 1 tbs seasoning soy sauce
  • ½ tbs sugar
  • ½ C basil

Directions

  1. Clean Livers: Salt chicken livers, rinse thoroughly under cold water, drain, then add 2 tbs vinegar, wash again, and rinse completely.
  2. Parboil: Cut livers into evenly sized pieces. Place in a heat-proof colander and parboil by pouring boiling water over them. Drain and let cool.
  3. Make Paste: Pound garlic and bird's-eye chilies in a mortar and pestle. Add spur chilies and basil stems and crush into a coarse paste.
  4. Heat Wok: Heat 2 tbs oil in a wok until smoking.
  5. Fry Paste: Add chili paste and stir-fry until it darkens slightly and starts to stick.
  6. Cook Livers: Add parboiled livers, oyster sauce, seasoning soy sauce, and sugar. Stir-fry just until the livers are cooked through.
  7. Finish: Add ½ C basil leaves and stir until just wilted.
  8. Serve: Transfer to a serving plate and serve hot with jasmine rice.
