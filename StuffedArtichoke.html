Stuffed Artichoke

Ingredients

  • 6 large artichokes
  • 1½ lb smoked bacon, sliced
  • 1 C green onion, chopped
  • ½ C parsley, chopped
  • 10 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbs salt
  • 1 tbs black pepper
  • 1 tbs cayenne pepper
  • 2½ C olive oil
  • 6 slices lemon
  • 1½ C Peccorino Romano cheese
  • 1½ C Parmagiano Reggiano cheese
  • 10 C Italian-seasoned bread crumbs

Directions

  1. Prep Artichokes: Trim pointed tops of artichokes and rub cut ends with lemon. Trim stems so they sit flat.
  2. Make Filling: Cook bacon until crisp and drain. Crumble and mix with bread crumbs, cheeses, green onion, parsley, garlic, and seasonings.
  3. Stuff: Spread artichoke leaves and pack filling between layers. Tap lightly to remove excess.
  4. Assemble: Stand artichokes upright in a casserole or roasting pan. Add water to about 1½" deep.
  5. Season: Drizzle generously with olive oil and top each with a lemon slice.
  6. Steam: Bring water to a boil, cover, reduce heat, and steam until leaves pull off easily, at least 1 hour. Check water level after about 25 minutes and add more if needed.

Chef's Notes

  • Optional Finish - Sprinkle with grated Parmigiano Reggiano and broil until melted and lightly browned.
