Philly Cheese Steak Dip

Ingredients

  • 1 Lb beef top sirloin steaks
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • 1 tbs olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 1 tbs butter
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 1 green bell pepper
  • ½ C pepperoncini peppers
  • 7 pickled red peppers
  • 3 jalapeno peppers
  • 2 (8 ounce) packages cream cheese, softened
  • ½ lb provolone cheese
  • ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper

Directions

  1. Prep Steak: Slice steak into thick pieces and season generously on both sides with salt and pepper.
  2. Sear Steak: Heat olive oil in a pan over high heat until smoking. Add steak and sear 3-4 minutes until browned. Flip, reduce heat to medium, and cook 3-4 minutes more until juices appear. Transfer steak to a bowl to cool.
  3. Cook Onions: Add diced onion, a generous pinch of salt, and butter to the pan drippings. Cook 5-7 minutes, scraping up browned bits, until onions soften.
  4. Add Peppers: Dice red bell pepper, green bell pepper, pepperoncini, pickled red peppers, and jalapeños (about 1½-2 cups total). Add to onions and cook ~5 minutes until starting to soften.
  5. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 400°F.
  6. Chop Steak: Chop cooled steak into small pieces and return to the bowl with accumulated juices.
  7. Mix Dip: Add onion-pepper mixture and cream cheese. Grate in provolone, reserving a little for topping. Add Worcestershire and cayenne; mix thoroughly.
  8. Assemble: Transfer ½ of the dip to a small baking dish and smooth the top. Place dish on a sheet pan and sprinkle with reserved provolone. (Reserve remaining half to bake fresh later.)
  9. Bake: Bake 20-25 minutes, until bubbling and heated through.
  10. Broil: Broil ~1 minute until top is browned. Repeat with second batch as needed.
