Philly Cheese Steak Dip

Ingredients

  • 1 Lb beef top sirloin steaks
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • 1 tbs olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 1 tbs butter
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 1 green bell pepper
  • ½ C pepperoncini peppers
  • 7 pickled red peppers
  • 3 jalapeno peppers
  • 2 (8 ounce) packages cream cheese, softened
  • ½ lb provolone cheese
  • ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper

Directions

  1. Slice steak into thick pieces. Season generously on both sides with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan over high heat until smoking.
  3. Add steak slices and sear until bottoms are browned, 3 to 4 minutes.
  4. Flip over and reduce heat to medium. Cook until juices appear on the tops, 3 to 4 minutes more. Transfer to a bowl to cool.
  5. Add diced onion, a big pinch of salt, and butter to the meat juices in the pan.
  6. Cook and stir over medium heat, scraping up the browned bits, until onions start to soften, 5 to 7 minutes.
  7. Dice up the red bell pepper, green bell pepper, pepperoncini, pickled red peppers, and jalapenos until you have 1½ to 2 cups.
  8. Add to the onions amd cook and stir until starting to soften, about 5 minutes.
  9. Preheat the oven to 400°
  10. Chop steak into small pieces. Place back into the bowl with the accumulated meat juices
  11. Add the onion-pepper mixture and cream cheese.
  12. Grate in the provolone cheese, saving a little for the top.
  13. Drizzle in Worcestershire sauce and sprinkle in cayenne pepper and mix thoroughly.
  14. Transfer half of the dip into a small baking dish and smooth the top with a fork.
  15. Place baking dish on top of a sheet pan and sprinkle the remaining provolone cheese on top. Save the other ½ to bake fresh for your guests.
  16. Bake in the preheated oven until dip is bubbling and heated through, 20 to 25 minutes.
  17. Broil until top is browned, about 1 minute more. Repeat with the second batch of dip.
