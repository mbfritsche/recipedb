Pâté de Campagne

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pork liver
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp tender quick curing salt
  • 1½ lb ground pork
  • ¼ C white wine

Directions

  1. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 325°F.
  2. Blend Liver: Process liver, garlic, seasonings, and wine until the mixture is smooth and soupy.
  3. Mix Pork: Pour liver mixture over ground pork and mix thoroughly to combine.
  4. Assemble Terrine: Transfer mixture to a terrine mold and top with bay leaves and thyme.
  5. Bake: Cover and bake in a bain-marie filled to ¾ height at 325°F for 2½ hours.
  6. Chill: Cool completely, then refrigerate overnight.
  7. Serve: Slice and serve with crusty bread, black olives, cornichons, and French mustard.
