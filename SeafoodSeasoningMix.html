Seafood Seasoning Mix

Ingredients

  • ¼ C salt
  • 2 tbs red pepper
  • 1 tbs black pepper
  • 2 tbs paprika
  • 1½ tbs onion powder
  • 1 tbs garlic powder
  • 1 tsp sweet basil
  • ½ tsp dry hot mustard
  • ½ tsp ground bay leaves
  • ¼ tsp file powder
  • ¼ tsp thyme
  • ¼ tsp tarragon
  • pinch oregano
  • pinch rosemary

Directions

  1. Mix all ingredients together and store in a glass jar. Use as needed in seafood recipes as a replacement for salt and pepper.
