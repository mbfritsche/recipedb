Corn and Crab Bisque

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lump crab meat
  • 8 oz fresh cooked corn (with ½ C stock)
  • ¼ C peanut oil
  • ¼ C flour
  • 2 oz unsalted butter
  • ¼ large yellow onion, chopped
  • ¼ red bell pepper, chopped
  • ¼ green bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 rib celery, chopped
  • 1 tbs garlic, minced
  • 2 C shrimp stock
  • ½ C dry white wine
  • 1½ tsp fresh thyme (½ tsp dried)
  • 3½ C heavy whipping cream
  • 1 tbs salt
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 3 tbs parsley, chopped
  • 3 tbs green onions, chopped
  • 16 jumbo crab claws (optional)

Directions

  1. Make Roux: In a black iron pot, cook flour and peanut oil together to form a light brown roux. Remove from heat and set aside once the desired color is reached.
  2. Sauté Vegetables: In a 4-qt saucepan, melt butter and sauté onion, bell peppers, celery, and garlic 1 minute.
  3. Build Base: Add shrimp stock, white wine, and thyme; bring to a boil.
  4. Thicken: Whisk in the reserved roux until thickened. Add whipping cream, lower heat to a simmer, and continue whisking. Stir in salt, hot sauce, corn, and corn stock.
  5. Finish: Simmer 3-4 minutes.
  6. Serve: Divide into 4 large bowls and garnish with parsley, green onions, and crab claws.
