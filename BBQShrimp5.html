BBQ Shrimp #5

Ingredients

  • 8-10 pounds head-on shrimp
  • 1 lb butter
  • 1 tsp ground rosemary
  • 1 lb margerine
  • 4 lemons sliced
  • 6 oz. Worcestershire
  • 1 tsp Tabasco
  • 8 tbs ground black pepper
  • 4 tsp salt
  • 4 cloves garlic

Directions

  1. Make Sauce: Melt butter and margarine in a saucepan. Stir in Worcestershire, pepper, rosemary, lemon slices, Tabasco, salt, and garlic until well combined.
  2. Assemble: Divide shrimp between two large shallow baking pans and pour the hot sauce evenly over them, mixing gently to coat.
  3. Bake: Cook in a 400°F oven for 15-20 minutes, turning shrimp once during baking.
  4. Serve: Serve hot with the pan juices.
