Chili

Ingredients

  • 1½-2 lbs ground beef
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 large (14½ oz) diced seeded tomatoes
  • 1 small can tomato sauce
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • dash oregano
  • dash basil
  • 1 beef bouillon cube
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Brown & Sauté: Brown beef in a large, heavy pot. Add chopped onions and garlic and cook until translucent.
  2. Simmer: Add remaining ingredients, bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer 2-3 hours, adding water as needed (about 2 C total).
