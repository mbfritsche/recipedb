Thai Peanut Butter Sauce Sauce

Ingredients

  • 4 cans of coconut milk
  • 40 g red curry paste
  • 1-2 tbs chili soy bean paste
  • 2-4 tbs extra-chunky peanut butter
  • 1 C sugar
  • 1-2 tsp salt (to taste)

Directions

  1. Reduce Sauce: Combine all ingredients in a heavy-bottomed pan. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer and cook until reduced by half, skimming oil from the surface as it reduces.
  2. Serve: Serve with moo satay (Thai pork kebabs) or with toast points as an appetizer.
