Smothered Oxtails

Ingredients

Oxtails
  • 6 lbs oxtails
  • 2 C flour, all purpose
  • 1 tbs Cajun seasoning
  • 1 stick of butter, unsalted
  • Extra virgin olive oil
Sauce
  • 6 C beef broth low sodium
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 1 white onion, sliced
  • 3 tbs unsalted butter
  • 3 tbs extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 tbs flour, all purpose
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp onion powder
  • Fresh chopped parsley to garnish.

Directions

Oxtails
  1. Preheat: Heat oven to 350°F.
  2. Prep Oxtails: Rinse oxtails, pat dry, and place in a large bowl. Coat with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  3. Coat: Combine Cajun seasoning and flour in a bag. Add oxtails and shake to coat. Tap off excess and set aside.
  4. Brown: Heat butter and olive oil in a large skillet. Brown oxtails in batches until golden on all sides.
  5. Finish: Repeat until all oxtails are browned.
Sauce
  1. Sauté Base: In the same pan, add butter and olive oil. Add onions and cook until softened, about 5 minutes. Add garlic and cook briefly.
  2. Season & Thicken: Add spices and stir well. Add flour and cook for a few minutes.
  3. Build Sauce: Add broth, bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until slightly thickened.
  4. Bake: Pour sauce over oxtails, cover with foil, and bake until very tender, about 3 to 3½ hours.
  5. Serve: Serve hot with rice and fried corn.
