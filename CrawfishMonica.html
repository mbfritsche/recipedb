Crawfish Monica

Ingredients

  • 1 lb crawfish tails, boiled and peeled
  • 1 lb cooked rotini
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, peeled and finely chopped
  • 1 stalk celery, peeled and finely chopped
  • 1 jalapeño, peeled, seeded and finely chopped
  • 1 bunch green onions, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 1 stick butter
  • 1 pt heavy cream (16 oz)
  • 2½ tsp Cajun seasoning to taste
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp lemon pepper

Directions

  1. Cook Pasta: Cook pasta al denté according to package directions and set aside.
  2. Sauté Vegetables: Melt butter in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add vegetables and sauté 20 minutes until softened.
  3. Add Garlic & Seasoning: Stir in garlic and half of the seasonings; sauté ~5 minutes more until fragrant.
  4. Cook Seafood: Add seafood and sauté 5-6 minutes, just until cooked. Stir in heavy cream and remaining seasonings.
  5. Simmer Sauce: Reduce heat to low and simmer 5-10 minutes, until the sauce thickens.
  6. Combine: Add pasta, toss well, cover, and simmer 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  7. Serve: Serve hot with French bread.

Chef's Notes

  • If you don't have crawfish, you can substitute shrimp, crabmeat, or oysters.
  • Bind equal parts of the butter with flour (~2 tbs or so) if you need to thicken the sauce. This is a beurre manié
