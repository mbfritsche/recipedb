Onion-Crusted Pork Tenderloin

Ingredients

  • 3 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 3 tbs olive oil
  • 1½ tsp minced fresh rosemary or ¼ tsp crushed dried rosemary
  • 1¼ Tabasco sauce
  • 1 clove garlic
  • ½ tsp salt
  • pinch ground cloves
  • 2 pork tenderloins, about 1 lb each
  • 1½ tbs white wine
  • 2 tbs bread crumbs

Directions

  1. Prepare Marinade: In a 9" baking dish, mix onions, 1 tbs olive oil, rosemary, 1 tsp Tabasco, garlic, salt, cloves, and breadcrumbs until well combined.
  2. Marinate Pork: Coat pork tenderloins with the onion mixture. Cover and refrigerate at least 2 hours, preferably overnight.
  3. Separate: Remove pork from the onions and set aside.
  4. Cook Onions: Heat 1 tbs olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over high heat. Add onion mixture and sauté 1 minute. Reduce heat to medium‑low and cook 12-15 minutes, stirring often, until onions are golden and very tender. Splash with chicken stock if dry.
  5. Deglaze: Add wine and simmer until fully evaporated; remove from heat.
  6. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 400°F.
  7. Brown Pork: Heat remaining 1 tbs olive oil in a medium skillet. Sear pork on all sides, ~4 minutes total.
  8. Assemble: Rinse, dry, and lightly grease the marinating dish. Transfer pork to dish and press onion mixture firmly over top and sides.
  9. Roast: Roast 35-40 minutes, until internal temperature reaches 150°F.
  10. Serve: Serve as an entrée with saffron rice and steamed vegetables, or slice for sandwiches.
