Pork Sausage

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground pork (20% fat)
  • 2 cloves crushed garlic
  • 2 tsp curing salt
  • ¼ C pistachio
  • Black chanterelle mushrooms
    • Reconstitute in warm water, then boil until water has evaporated
    • Truffle will substitute here, too
  • 2 tbs white wine
  • 1 tsp ground pepper

Directions

  1. Mix Sausage: Combine all ingredients, mix thoroughly, and shape into a log. Wrap tightly in plastic wrap, then wrap again in aluminum foil.
  2. Cure: Refrigerate 2-3 days (up to 1 week) to cure.
  3. Prep Pan: When ready to cook, heat butter and olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
  4. Cook Sausage: Unwrap sausage and add to the pan with oil and butter. Add 1 large onion, quartered and separated, and ~12 small potatoes, peeled.
  5. Simmer: Cover and cook over medium heat 30-45 minutes, until sausage and potatoes are cooked through.
  6. Poaching Option: Alternatively, keep sausage wrapped in foil and poach in gently boiling water ~1½ hours.
