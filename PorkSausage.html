Pork Sausage

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground pork (20% fat)
  • 2 cloves crushed garlic
  • 2 tsp curing salt
  • ¼ C pistachio
  • Black chanterelle mushrooms
    • Reconstitute in warm water, then boil until water has evaporated
    • Truffle will substitute here, too
  • 2 tbs white wine
  • 1 tsp ground pepper

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients, mix well and wrap in a roll in plastic wrap and then aluminum foil
  2. Cure in the refrigerator 2-3 days (up to a week)
  3. After it has cured, add butter and olive oil to a pan over medium heat.
  4. Unwrap the sausage and add to the oil and butter
  5. Add one large onion, quartered and separated, a dozen small potatoes, peeled
  6. Cover and cook over medium heat 30-45 minutes
  7. You can also poach in water in the foil, low boil for 1½ hours.
