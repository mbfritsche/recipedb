Yellow Cake

Ingredients

  • 360 g all-purpose flour, sifted
  • 5 large eggs
  • 2 C granulated sugar
  • 8 oz unsalted butter, softened
  • 1¼ C whole milk
  • 1 tbs baking powder
  • ½ tsp table salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Cooking spray

Directions

  2. Preheat: Heat oven to 350°F. Grease two 9" round cake pans and dust with flour.
  3. Mix Batter: Beat butter and sugar until fluffy. Add eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition.
  4. Add Dry Ingredients: In a separate bowl, combine flour, baking powder, and salt. Add to the batter alternately with milk, mixing on low speed until just combined. Stir in vanilla.
  5. Bake: Divide batter evenly between pans and bake until a tester inserted in the center comes out clean, about 25-28 minutes.
  6. Cool & Serve: Cool in pans briefly, then remove and cool completely on a wire rack before serving.
