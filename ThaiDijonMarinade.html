Thai Dijon Marinade

Ingredients

  • 3 C Dijon mustard
  • 1 C Creamy peanut butter
  • ¾ C Honey
  • ½ C Soy sauce
  • ½ C Sesame oil
  • ½ C Vegetable oil
  • 2 tbs Lime juice

Directions

  1. Whisk all ingredients and refrigerate. Use as a baste or marinade for roasted chicken or pork.
