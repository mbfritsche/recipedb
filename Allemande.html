Allemande

Ingredients

  • 1 qt veal velouté
  • 2 egg yolks
  • ½ C heavy cream
  • 1½ tsp lemon juice
  • Salt and white pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Reduce Sauce: Bring the velouté to a gentle simmer and reduce slightly.
  2. Make Liaison: Whisk egg yolks and cream together in a stainless-steel bowl.
  3. Temper: Slowly whisk a small amount of hot velouté into the liaison. Repeat until about ½ of the sauce is incorporated.
  4. Combine: Gradually stir the tempered liaison back into the saucepan.
  5. Heat Gently: Reheat to a very low simmer - do not boil.
  6. Finish: Add lemon juice, salt, and pepper to taste. Strain through cheesecloth.
  7. Variation: Chicken velouté may be used if that's what you have.
