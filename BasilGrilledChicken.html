Basil Grilled Chicken

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken thighs
  • ¾ tsp ground pepper
  • ½ C butter, melted
  • ¼ C chopped fresh basil
  • ½ C butter, softened
  • 2 tbs minced fresh basil
  • 1 tbs Parmesan cheese
  • ¼ tsp garlic powder
  • dash salt, pepper

Directions

  1. Season Chicken: Press pepper into the meaty sides of the chicken thighs.
  2. Baste: Mix melted butter with chopped basil and brush lightly over the chicken.
  3. Make Basil Butter: Beat softened butter with basil, Parmesan, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until smooth. Set aside.
  4. Grill: Grill chicken over medium coals for 8-10 minutes per side, basting frequently with the melted butter mixture.
  5. Serve: Serve grilled chicken topped with the basil-butter mixture and garnish with fresh basil sprigs.
