Emeril Legasse's Seafood Gumbo

Ingredients

  • 2 tbs olive oil
  • ½ C chopped onions
  • ¼ C chopped celery
  • ¼ C chopped green bell peppers
  • ¼ C chopped red bell peppers
  • 1 tbs salt
  • 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • ½ C Italian plum tomatoes, peeled, seeded and chopped
  • 2 tbs minced garlic
  • 1 tbs minced shallots
  • 2 qt Fish Stock
  • ½ lb firm fish, such as grouper, tilefish, monkfish, or sea bass, diced
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • ½ tsp hot pepper sauce
  • 6 bay leaves
  • 1 tbs minced fresh basil
  • 1 tsp minced fresh oregano
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 C fresh okra, sliced (about 8 large okra)
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • ½ lb peeled medium fresh shrimp
  • 1 C shucked fresh oysters, with their liquor
  • ½ lb fresh lump crab meat, picked over for shells and cartilage
  • 1 tsp filé powder
  • ½ C chopped green onions
  • 4 C cooked long-grain white rice

Directions

  1. Sauté Base: Heat oil in a large pot over high heat. Add onions, celery, and green and red peppers; sauté 1 minute. Season with salt and pepper and sauté 1 minute more. Add tomatoes, garlic, and shallots; sauté ~4 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  2. Build Gumbo: Stir in stock. Add fish, Worcestershire, hot pepper sauce, bay leaves, basil, oregano, and thyme. Bring to a boil and cook over high heat ~8 minutes, stirring occasionally. Reduce heat to medium.
  3. Add Okra: Fold in okra and Cajun seasoning. Lower heat and simmer 15 minutes. Skim impurities from the surface, increase heat to high, and cook 5 minutes more.
  4. Finish Seafood: Fold in shrimp, oysters, and crabmeat. Reduce heat and simmer 5 minutes.
  5. Thicken: Slowly sprinkle in filé powder, stirring constantly to incorporate. Simmer 2 minutes, then remove from heat.
  6. Serve: Serve hot over rice, topped with green onions.
