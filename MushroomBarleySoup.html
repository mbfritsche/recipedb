Mushroom Barley Soup (v)

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Mushrooms, sliced
  • 32 oz vegetable broth
  • 2 tbs butter
  • 6 sprigs fresh thyme, leaves removed, or 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 large carrots, chopped
  • 2 celery stalks, chopped
  • ½ C barley
  • ¼ C freshly chopped parsley
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Sauté Base: In a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat, melt butter. Add thyme and mushrooms and cook 2-3 minutes, until edges turn golden.
  2. Add Vegetables: Stir in garlic, onion, carrots, and celery. Season with salt and pepper and cook ~10 minutes, until softened.
  3. Simmer Soup: Add barley and broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer ~25 minutes, until barley is tender and the soup begins to thicken.
  4. Serve: Ladle into bowls and garnish with parsley.

Chef's Notes

  • This is a vegetarian recipe. Substituting avocado oil for the butter will make it vegan
