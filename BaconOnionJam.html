Bacon Onion Jam

Ingredients

  • 4 uncooked thick applewood-smoked bacon slices, chopped
  • 1 tbs butter
  • 2 medium-size sweet onions, chopped
  • 4 large shallots, chopped
  • ½ C balsamic vinegar
  • 3 tbs light brown sugar
  • 2½ tsp kosher salt
  • 2 tbs chopped fresh chives
  • 2 tsp chopped fresh thyme

Directions

  1. Cook Bacon: In a medium skillet over medium-low heat, cook bacon until crisp (8-10 minutes). Remove and drain, reserving drippings.
  2. Sauté Onions: Add butter to the drippings and melt. Increase heat to medium, add onions, and sauté 10-12 minutes until tender.
  3. Build Flavor: Add shallots, vinegar, sugar, and kosher salt; cook 1-2 minutes until sugar dissolves.
  4. Caramelize: Reduce heat to low and cook, stirring occasionally, 20-25 minutes until onions are deeply tender and browned.
  5. Finish: Remove from heat and stir in chives, thyme, and bacon.
  6. Cool & Store: Cool about 20 minutes or refrigerate airtight up to 3 days.
