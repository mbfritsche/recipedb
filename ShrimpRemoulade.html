Shrimp Remoulade

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs med-large shrimp
  • 2 tbs paprika
  • ¾ C Creole mustard
  • ½ C celery hearts, chopped
  • ¼ C onion, chopped
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tbs parsley
  • 1 C chilled oil (cottonseed or corn)
  • 3 dashes Tabasco sauce

Directions

  1. Blanch Shrimp: Wash shrimp and add to a pot of plain boiling water. When the water returns to a boil, remove shrimp, drain, and transfer to chilled salted water (½ C salt per 1 gallon water). Chill thoroughly.
  2. Make Dressing: In a bowl, combine Creole mustard, paprika, parsley, sugar, Tabasco, celery, and onions. Slowly whisk in the chilled oil to form an emulsion.
  3. Marinate: Add chilled shrimp to the dressing and marinate 2-3 hours.
  4. Serve: Serve on a bed of chilled chopped lettuce with crackers as an appetizer.
