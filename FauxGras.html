Faux Gras (v)

Ingredients

  • 100g button mushrooms
  • 400g cooked green lentils
  • 140g toasted walnuts or pecans
  • 2 tbs olive oil
  • 2 tbs butter
  • 1 small onion, peeled and diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, peeled and minced
  • 2 tbs freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • 1 tbs soy sauce or tamari
  • 2 tsp minced fresh rosemary
  • 2 tsp fresh thyme, minced
  • 2 tbs fresh sage or flat leaf parsley
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • Dash cayenne pepper
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 2 tsp Cognac or brandy (optional)

Directions

  1. Sauté: Clean mushrooms, trim stem ends, and slice. Heat olive oil and butter in a skillet. Add onions and garlic and cook until translucent, about 5-6 minutes. Add mushrooms and cook until softened.
  2. Blend: In a food processor, combine lentils, nuts, lemon juice, soy sauce, herbs, Cognac if using, brown sugar, and cayenne. Add the mushroom mixture and process until smooth.
  3. Adjust: Taste and adjust with salt, pepper, additional Cognac, soy sauce, or lemon juice as needed.
  4. Chill: Transfer pât&eauteé to a serving bowl and refrigerate until firm.
  5. Serve: Serve with crackers, hearty bread, or toast. Accompany with shallot marmalade or pickles if desired.

Chef's Notes

  • Yield: Lentils roughly double in volume when cooked. About 1 cup (160 g) dried lentils will produce the amount needed for this recipe.
  • Cooking Tip: Cook lentils until soft, typically 20-30 minutes, following package directions as needed.
  • Gluten-Free Option: Use tamari in place of soy sauce.
  • Vegan Option: Replace the butter with an equal amount of avocado or olive oil.
  • Flavor Tip: Cognac or brandy is optional, but it adds an extra layer of richness and complexity to the finished pât&eauteé.
