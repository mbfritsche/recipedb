Vegan BBQ Sauce

Ingredients

  • 275 g organic ketchup
  • 80 g maple syrup
  • 3 tbs apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tbs soy sauce or tamari
  • 2 tbs molasses
  • 1 tbs vegan Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbs liquid smoke
BBQ Spice Blend
  • 1½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 1½ tsp chili powder
  • ¾ tsp black pepper
  • ¼ tsp onion powder
  • ¼ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper

Directions

  1. Mix Sauce: Add all ingredients to a mixing bowl and whisk until well combined. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt or pepper as needed. For more sweetness, add additional maple syrup; for more smokiness, add a few drops of liquid smoke or a pinch of smoked paprika.
  2. Simmer: Transfer the sauce to a small frying pan and simmer 3-5 minutes, stirring at least once per minute.
  3. Cool & Store: Remove from heat and let cool. Transfer to an airtight jar and refrigerate. The sauce will thicken as it chills.
  4. Storage: Keeps 2-3 months in the refrigerator.
