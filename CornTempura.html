Corn Tempura (v)

Ingredients

  • 2 ears of corn (1½ C or 9 oz)
  • 85 g flour
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • 150 ml club soda
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Sour cream or crème fraîche and smoked salmon (serving suggestion)

Directions

  1. Fry: Heat oil in a frying pan. Spoon ¼ cup tempura batter into the pan and fry like pancakes, 2–3 minutes per side, until golden.
  2. Drain & Serve: Cool on a wire rack, then serve with sour cream and caviar or smoked salmon.

Chef's Notes

  • Use half the club sode to mix the lumps out of the flour mixture - this is much easier. The final product should be slightly more liquid
