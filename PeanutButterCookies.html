Peanut Butter Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 C peanut butter
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla (optional)

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients.
  2. Shape dough into 1½" balls on a greased cookie sheet.
  3. Flatten with the bottom of a glass dipped in additional sugar.
  4. Bake at 350° for 10 minutes, and cool on a wire rack.
