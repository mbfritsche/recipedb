Jangjorim

Ingredients

  • 560g beef (eye round, brisket, or flank), cut into 3-inch chunks
  • 150g quail eggs or 6 boiled eggs, peeled
  • 85g jalapeño peppers
  • 1 Green onion
  • 7-10 garlic cloves
  • 2-3 slices fresh ginger
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp whole black peppercorns
  • 2 liters water
  • 120 ml soy sauce
  • 60 ml Mirin
  • 2-3 tbs brown sugar
  • 1 piece dried kelp (dashima or kombu) – optional

Directions

  1. Blanch Meat: Boil meat 5-10 minutes to remove impurities. Drain, discard water, and rinse meat under cold running water.
  2. Simmer: Add rinsed meat to a clean pot with 1 liter fresh water and remaining ingredients. Simmer 30-40 minutes. If using dried kelp (dashima), steep for 5 minutes, then remove and continue simmering.
  3. Strain: Pour contents through a strainer, reserving the broth. Set aside meat and quail eggs.
  4. Shred Meat: Shred meat into small pieces along the grain.
  5. Finish: Return shredded meat and quail eggs to the broth. Simmer an additional 5 minutes if seasoning is too mild.
  6. Cool & Store: Let cool to room temperature. Transfer meat, eggs, and optional peppers to a glass container, pour broth over to fully submerge, and refrigerate.
