Backbone Stew #2

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • ¾ C oil
  • 1½ lb pork loin/backbone (or pork chops with bone in)
  • 3-5 medium Irish potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 large bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 stalks celery, chopped
  • 1 qt water
  • Salt, black pepper and red pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Make Roux & Base: Cook oil and flour to make a roux. Add onion, bell pepper, and celery; sauté. Add garlic and cook 3 minutes more. Stir in water and mix thoroughly.
  2. Add Pork & Potatoes: Cut pork into bite-size pieces, season with salt, black pepper, and red pepper, and add to the pot along with potatoes.
  3. Simmer: Simmer 1 hour, until potatoes are tender.
  4. Serve: Serve over hot cooked rice with vinegar cabbage.

Chef's Notes

  • Chicken maybe substituted for pork
