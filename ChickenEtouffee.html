Chicken Étouffée

Ingredients

  • 8 chicken thighs, skinned
  • 2 medium onions, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • ¼ C thinly sliced celery
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 stick butter (4 oz)
  • 2 tbs avocado oil
  • 1½ tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 3 tbs flour
  • 2 tbs tomato paste
  • ¼ tsp Tabasco
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1½ C chicken stock, broth or water
  • 1 C chopped green onions
  • ½ C fresh parsley, minced
  • Cooked long grain white rice

Directions

  1. Brown Chicken: Heat a large, heavy skillet over medium-high. Add avocado oil and butter until hot. Season chicken thighs with Cajun seasoning and brown well; remove and set aside.
  2. Cook Trinity: Add onions, celery, and red bell pepper to the skillet and sauté about 5 minutes.
  3. Make Roux: Stir in flour and cook about 4 minutes, stirring constantly.
  4. Build Sauce: Mix in tomato paste, Tabasco, and Worcestershire. Add chicken stock and return chicken to the pan. Cover and cook 12 minutes.
  5. Finish: Uncover, add green onions and parsley, and cook 3 minutes more.
  6. Serve: Serve hot over steamed rice.
