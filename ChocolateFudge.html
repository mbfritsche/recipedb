Chocolate Fudge

Ingredients

  • 315 ml evaporated milk
  • 1 13-oz jar marshmallow cream
  • 1½ C margarine
  • 6 C sugar
  • 2 C chopped nuts
  • 2 12-oz packages semi-sweet chocolate chips
  • 2 tsp vanilla

Directions

  1. Cook Base: Bring evaporated milk and margarine to a boil, then remove from heat.
  2. Dissolve Sugar: Stir in sugar until dissolved. Return to medium heat, cover, and cook 2-3 minutes until steam washes sugar crystals from the pan sides (do not stir).
  3. Add Chocolate: Remove from heat and stir in chocolate chips until melted.
  4. Finish: Add marshmallow cream, nuts, and vanilla; beat until thick.
  5. Set: Pour into two buttered 9x13" dishes and let cool until firm.
