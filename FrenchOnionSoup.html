French Onion Soup

Ingredients

  • 4 tbs unsalted butter
  • 2 lbs large onion
  • ½ C white wine
  • 6 C low sodium beef broth
  • 1 tbs white wine vinegar
  • 1 tbs dried thyme
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp freshly ground pepper
  • 4 C cubed French bread
  • ¼ C extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 C shredded Swiss cheese

Directions

    Caramelize Onions: In a large heavy‑duty pot over medium‑high heat, melt butter. Add onions and cook 30–40 minutes, stirring and scraping the bottom occasionally, until deeply caramelized. Deglaze: Add white wine and cook ~5 minutes. Build Soup: Add beef broth, vinegar, and thyme. Bring to a boil, then lower heat and simmer 10 minutes. Season with salt and pepper. Prep Topping: Preheat oven to 450°F. In a large bowl, toss bread cubes with olive oil and cheese; set aside. Assemble: Spoon soup into oven‑safe bowls or ramekins. Top each with a handful of bread cubes. Bake: Transfer bowls to the oven and bake until croutons are golden and cheese is melted. Serve: Remove from oven, cool slightly, garnish with extra thyme, and serve hot.
