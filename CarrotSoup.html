Carrot Soup (v)

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs carrots, chopped (5 cups)
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 1 stalk celery, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 1 tbs butter
  • 1 tbs extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme or parsley, chopped
  • 2 C water
  • 4 C vegetable broth
  • ½ cup half-and-half (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Sauté Base: Heat butter and oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat. Add onion and celery; cook 4-6 minutes until softened.
  2. Add Aromatics: Stir in garlic and thyme (or parsley) and cook about 10 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Simmer: Add carrots, water, and broth. Bring to a lively simmer, then reduce heat and cook about 25 minutes until tender.
  4. Purée: Blend soup in batches until smooth, using caution with hot liquids.
  5. Finish: Stir in half-and-half (if using) and season with salt and pepper. Serve warm.

Chef's Notes

  • Vegetarian as written, vegan if the half-and-half and butter are replaced with plant-based alternatives
