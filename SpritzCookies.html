Spritz Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 C butter (softened)
  • 2½ C flour
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ¾ C sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp almond extract
  • dash salt

Directions

  1. Cream Base: Cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Beat in the egg.
  2. Add Dry & Flavor: Gradually mix in the sifted dry ingredients and almond extract until combined.
  3. Shape: Fill a cookie press and press cookies 1½" apart onto a lightly greased baking sheet.
  4. Bake: Bake at 350°F for 10 minutes, until set.
  5. Cool: Transfer to a wire rack and allow to cool completely.
