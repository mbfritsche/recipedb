Spritz Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 C butter (softened)
  • 2½ C flour
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ¾ C sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp almond extract
  • dash salt

Directions

  1. Cream butter and sugar, and add egg.
  2. Add sifted dry ingredients slowly and add almond extract.
  3. Fill cookie press and press cookies 1½" apart on a lightly greased cookie sheet.
  4. Bake at 350° for 10 minutes and cool on a wire rack.
