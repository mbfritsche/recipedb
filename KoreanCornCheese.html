Korean Corn Cheese

Ingredients

  • 2 ears fresh sweet corn kernels kernels removed and cobs discarded
  • ½ C mayonnaise
  • 4 oz shredded mozzarella
  • 1½ tsp sugar
  • 2 green onions, thinly sliced
  • salt and pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400° F
  2. Combine all ingredients into a mixing bowl and stir together.
  3. Pour mixture into a lightly greased baking dish and bake for 10 minutes or until mixture melts and bubbles.
  4. Transfer dish to broiler and broil for 2 minutes or until top is browned.
  5. Serve immediately

Chef's Notes

  • Although fresh corn is our preference, you can use canned corn or frozen corn. If using canned corn, thoroughly drain kernels from 1 (15 ounce) can and make sure to omit the 1½ tsp of sugar as canned corn sits in a naturally sweet brine. If using frozen corn (about 210 g), thaw completely and drain any excess water before continuing to make the recipe as instructed.
