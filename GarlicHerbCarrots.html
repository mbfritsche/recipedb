Garlic Herb Carrots (V)

Ingredients

  • 1 lb medium carrots, peeled and cut into 1/3-inch diagonal slices
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 shallot, minced
  • 2 tbs chopped fresh dill
  • 2 tbs chopped fresh parsley leaves
  • 1 tbs chopped fresh tarragon leaves
  • 3 tbs unsalted butter
  • 1 tbs red wine vinegar
  • 1½ tsp sugar
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Sauté: Melt butter in a large skillet over medium‑high heat.
  2. Cook Aromatics: Add garlic and shallot. Cook, stirring frequently, until fragrant, about 2 minutes.
  3. Cook Carrots: Add carrots and stir to coat. Cover and cook until just tender, 4-6 minutes, stirring once.
  4. Finish: Stir in red wine vinegar and sugar until combined, then remove from heat.
  5. Herbs & Season: Stir in dill, parsley, and tarragon. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
