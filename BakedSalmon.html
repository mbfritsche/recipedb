Baked Salmon

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs salmon filet
  • Avocado oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • Soy sauce
  • Seasoned soy sauce
  • Fish sauce
  • Oyster sauce
  • Black bean sauce
  • Palm sugar
  • Lime juice (optional)
  • Bird's eye chilies (optional)

Directions

  1. Prep Salmon: Coat both sides of the salmon with avocado oil on a baking tray. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  2. Add Sauce: Pour equal amounts of soy sauce, seasoned soy sauce, fish sauce, and oyster sauce around and over the fish. Add black bean sauce, lime juice, and bird's-eye chilies.
  3. Bake: Bake at 32°F for 15 minutes, or until the salmon flakes easily. Transfer fish to a serving dish to rest.
  4. Deglaze: Add ¼ C water to the pan to deglaze, then pour the sauce over the salmon.
  5. Optional: Reduce sauce briefly over direct heat before serving.
