Salmon in Tamarind Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 lb salmon filets, cut into 3 portions
  • 2 teaspoon neutral oil, for greasing the salmon
  • A pinch of salt
  • 6 cilantro sprigs
  • 2 small shallots, roughly chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1-2 Thai chilies, to taste, or use dried red chili flakes
  • 2 tbs neutral oil
  • ½ C chicken stock or water
  • 3 tbs palm sugar, finely chopped
  • 3 tbs tamarind paste, store bought or homemade
  • 1½ tbs fish sauce
  • ¼ C red peppers, julienned (Optional)
  • 3 tbs Fried shallots, (Optional)
  • Jasmine rice, for serving

Directions

  2. Prep Salmon: Lightly oil salmon and season with salt. Cook in an air fryer until just done, about 6 minutes. Let rest.
  3. Make Paste: Separate cilantro stems from leaves. Chop stems and combine with garlic, shallots, and chilies. Pound or process into a rough paste.
  4. Sauté: Heat oil in a pan and cook the paste until fragrant and lightly browned.
  5. Build Sauce: Add stock, palm sugar, tamarind, and fish sauce. Cook, stirring, until sugar dissolves and sauce reduces slightly.
  6. Finish Salmon: Add salmon to the sauce with any juices. Spoon sauce over and cook briefly to desired doneness.
  7. Serve: Plate salmon, spoon sauce over, and garnish with cilantro, chilies, and fried shallots. Serve with rice.
