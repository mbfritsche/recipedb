Miso Eggplant (V)

Ingredients

  • 3 Japanese eggplant, sliced in half and scored
  • Avocado oil
  • 2 tbs miso paste
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 tsp mirin
  • 1 tbs coconut aminos
  • 1 tbs maple syrup

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to broil.
  2. Halve eggplants longways, cut into 2" sections, and score the flesh in a diamond pattern
  3. Sear in Avocado oil over medium heat in a non-stick pan, 2-3 minutes per side. Remove to a baking tray
  4. Combine miso, mirin, sesame oil, coconut aminos and maple syrup and brush onto eggplant
  5. Broil 4-6 minutes or until caramelized
