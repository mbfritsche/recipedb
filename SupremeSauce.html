Supreme Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 qt chicken velouté
  • 1 C heavy cream
  • 2 tbs cold butter
  • Salt, white pepper and lemon juice to taste

Directions

  1. Reduce Velouté: Heat velouté in a medium saucepan and simmer until reduced by about 25%, stirring occasionally.
  2. Temper Cream: Pour cream into a metal bowl. Slowly whisk in a small amount of the hot velouté to temper.
  3. Combine: Gradually stir the tempered cream back into the sauce and return to a very low simmer.
  4. Finish: Swirl in raw butter until melted. Season to taste with salt, pepper, and lemon juice.
  5. Strain: Strain the sauce through cheesecloth until smooth.
