Fetuccine Aphrodite (V)

Ingredients

  • 12 oz fettucine
  • 16 oz canned tomatoes
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 5 celerey hearts, chopped
  • 2 carrots, julienned
  • 1 small white onion, chopped
  • 5 tbs olive oil
  • ½ C white wine

Directions

  1. Cook Pasta: Cook fettuccine per package directions. Drain well, drizzle with 1 tbs olive oil and 1 tbs Parmesan, and set aside.
  2. Start Sauce: Heat a separate pan over medium-high. Add tomatoes with their juice.
  3. Build Flavor: Add olive oil, garlic, celery hearts, carrots, onion, and white wine as the tomatoes cook.
  4. Simmer: Bring to temperature, reduce heat, cover, and simmer 15 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add more wine if the sauce becomes too concentrated.
  5. Serve: Spoon sauce over fettuccine. Serve with garlic bread and a hearty red wine.
