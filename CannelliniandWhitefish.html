Cannellini and Smoked Whitefish Spread

Ingredients

  • 2 slices white sandwich bread, crusts removed
  • 160 ml milk or buttermilk
  • 1 18-ounce can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 lb smoked whitefish, all skin and bones removed
  • 1 tsp minced fresh garlic
  • 1 tsp finely grated lemon zest
  • 2 tbs chopped fresh herbs such as chives, parsley, tarragon
  • Salt and freshly ground pepper
  • Louisiana hot sauce

Directions

  1. Soak Bread: Soak bread in milk for a few minutes.
  2. Process Base: Add beans, whitefish, garlic, and zest to a food processor. Pulse to coarsely chop.
  3. Blend: Add soaked bread with milk and process until smooth.
  4. Finish: Pulse in herbs and season with salt, pepper, and a few drops of hot sauce.
  5. Store: Cover and refrigerate up to 5 days.
