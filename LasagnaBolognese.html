Lasagna Bolognese

Ingredients

  • Ragu Bolognese
  • 1 (16 ounce) package lasagna noodles
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 pounds whole-milk ricotta cheese
  • 16 oz (2 C) fresh mozzarella cheese, diced
  • 8 oz (1 C) freshly shredded Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp pepper
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper, or to taste
  • ¼ C chopped fresh parsley

Directions

  1. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Mix Cheese Filling: Beat eggs in a large bowl. Stir in ricotta, ½ the mozzarella, and ½ the Parmigiano-Reggiano. Season with salt, pepper, cayenne, and parsley.
  3. Cook Noodles: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook lasagna noodles ~8 minutes, until tender but firm. Drain, rinse, and hold in cold water. Reserve some pasta water if the Bolognese is thick.
  4. Layer Base: Spread ¼ of the sauce in the bottom of a 10x15" baking pan.
  5. Layer 1: Add a third of the noodles, then ½ of the ricotta mixture.
  6. Layer 2: Spread ¼ of the sauce, add another third of the noodles, and cover with remaining ricotta mixture.
  7. Layer 3: Spread ¼ of the sauce over the cheese.
  8. Set Layers: Lightly tap the pan on a work surface to settle.
  9. Top Layer: Arrange remaining noodles, spread remaining sauce, and top with remaining mozzarella and Parmigiano-Reggiano.
  10. Bake Covered: Cover loosely with foil (do not touch the cheese). Place pan on a baking sheet and bake 30 minutes.
  11. Uncover & Finish: Remove foil and bake 30 minutes more, until bubbling and golden.
  12. Rest: Let rest 20 minutes before cutting and serving.
