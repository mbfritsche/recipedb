Sesame Ginger Dressing

Ingredients

  • 100 g carrots, diced
  • 50 g shallots, diced
  • 2 tbs ginger, grated
  • 1 tbs sugar
  • 120 ml C rice vinegar
  • 60 ml C soy sauce
  • ¼ tsp kosher salt
  • 240 ml canola oil
  • 30 ml sesame oil

Directions

  1. Blend Base: Add carrots, shallots, ginger, vinegar, soy sauce, and seasonings to a blender. Blend until smooth.
  2. Emulsify: With the blender running, slowly stream in canola oil until emulsified.
  3. Finish: Add sesame oil and blend briefly to combine.
  4. Store: Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate.

Chef's Notes

  • This is a copycat recipe of the house dressing available in many sushi restaurants
