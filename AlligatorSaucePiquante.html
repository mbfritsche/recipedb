Alligator Sauce Piquante

Ingredients

  • 2-3 lbs. alligator meat
  • 3 onions
  • 3 bell peppers
  • 5 ribs celery
  • 3-5 cloves garlic
  • 2 mild green chiles
  • 3 tablespoons oil
  • 3 tablespoons flour
  • 3 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 small can tomato paste
  • ½ C red wine
  • 1½ C stock or broth
  • 2 tbs Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp white pepper
  • 1½ tsp dried rosemary leaves
  • ½ tsp dried thyme leaves
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil for sauteing

Directions

  1. Make Stock: If using alligator or turtle bones, simmer with carrot, onion, celery tops, and peppercorns to make stock. Skim fat and reserve 2 cups. Otherwise, use Knorr chicken, beef, or fish bouillon.
  2. Brown Meat: Dice meat and sauté in a little oil until browned; set aside.
  3. Prep Vegetables: Sauté one onion and one bell pepper until tender, then purée and set aside. Sauté remaining onion, bell pepper, chiles, celery, and garlic.
  4. Make Roux: Cook oil and flour to a medium peanut-butter‑colored roux. Stir in sautéed vegetables, remove from heat, and mix well to prevent sticking.
  5. Build Sauce: Add tomatoes, tomato paste, wine, Worcestershire, and the onion-pepper purée. Season with Cajun seasoning and salt. Cook over medium 10 minutes, stirring.
  6. Simmer: Add meat, rosemary, and thyme. Simmer on low 30 minutes, stirring often. If using shrimp/seafood, cook base 20 minutes, then add shrimp for final 10 minutes.
  7. Serve: Serve over rice with French bread.

Chef's Notes

  • If alligator isn't available, you can substitute turtle, rabbit, frog legs, pork, shrimp, chicken, squirrel, or raccoon.
  • For a milder dish, reduce the cayenne. For a hotter version, replace 2 cans of stewed tomatoes with 2 cans of Rotel tomatoes.
