Egg Rolls

Ingredients

  • ½ head cabbage, shredded
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots, shredded
  • ¼ C celery, minced
  • 1 tbs oil
  • 1 tbs soy sauce
  • 3 tbs water
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 egg yolk
  • Spring roll wrappers
  • oil for deep frying

Directions

  1. Sauté Onion: Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan and sauté onion until translucent.
  2. Add Vegetables & Seasoning: Add cabbage, carrot, celery, soy sauce, water, sugar, salt, and sesame oil.
  3. Finish: Sweat vegetables briefly, then remove from heat
To construct egg rolls
  1. Prep Wrappers: Lay out spring roll wrappers on a clean work surface.
  2. Add Filling: Place ~1 tbsp of the cabbage mixture in the center of each wrapper.
  3. Roll: Fold in the sides and roll tightly to enclose the filling.
  4. Seal: Brush wrapper edges with egg yolk to seal securely.
To cook egg rolls
  1. Fry: Fry egg rolls in 325°F oil for 1-2 minutes, until golden brown.
  2. Drain & Serve: Drain on a wire rack and serve hot with sweet-and-sour dipping sauce.
