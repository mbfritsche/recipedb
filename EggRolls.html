Egg Rolls

Ingredients

  • ½ head cabbage, shredded
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots, shredded
  • ¼ C celery, minced
  • 1 tbs oil
  • 1 tbs soy sauce
  • 3 tbs water
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 egg yolk
  • Spring roll wrappers
  • oil for deep frying

Directions

  1. Heat 1 tbs of oil and sauté the onion. When translucent, add the cabbage, carrot, and celery and the soy sauce, water, sugar, salt, and sesame oil.
  2. Sweat the remainder of the vegetables briefly and remove from heat.
To construct egg rolls
  1. Spread out the spring roll wrappers.
  2. Place a small amount (about 1 tbs) of the cabbage mixture in the center of each sheet.
  3. Roll up, folding the edges in tightly.
  4. Brush the edges of the wrapper with the egg yolk to fasten securely.
To cook egg rolls
  1. Fry the egg rolls in 325° oil for about 1-2 minutes or until golden. Drain on a wire rack and serve with sweet and sour dipping sauce.
