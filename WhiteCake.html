White Cake

Ingredients

  • 2¾ C sifted cake flour
  • 3 tsp baking powder
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • 1 C vegetable shortening
  • 1½ tsp vanilla
  • 1½ C sugar
  • 1 C milk
  • 8 egg whites
  • ½ C sugar

Directions

  1. Prep Dry: Sift flour, baking powder, and salt together; set aside.
  2. Cream Base: Cream shortening, vanilla, and 1½ cups sugar until light and fluffy.
  3. Mix Batter: Add the flour mixture and milk alternately, beating only until smooth after each addition.
  4. Whip Whites: In a separate bowl, beat egg whites until frothy. Gradually add remaining ½ C sugar and continue beating until stiff, glossy peaks form.
  5. Fold: Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the cake batter.
  6. Bake: Grease and flour cake pans. Divide batter between pans and bake at 350°F until a knife inserted in the center comes out clean.
  7. Cool & Frost: Cool cakes in pans 10 minutes, then remove and cool completely. Frost with basic butter frosting.
