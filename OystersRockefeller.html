Oysters Rockefeller

Ingredients

  • 36 fresh oysters on the half shell
  • 6 tbs butter
  • 6 tbs finely minced raw spinach
  • 3 tbs minced onion
  • 3 tbs minced parsley
  • 5 tbs bread crumbs
  • ½ tsp Herbsaint (or Pernod)
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Tabasco sauce to taste

Directions

  1. Make Topping: In a saucepan, melt butter and add all ingredients except the oysters. Cook, stirring constantly, 15 minutes. Press the mixture through a sieve or food mill and allow to cool.
  2. Prepare Oysters: Line six pie tins with rock salt. Nestle 6 oysters per tin into the salt.
  3. Top Oysters: Divide the topping into 36 equal portions and place one portion on each oyster.
  4. Broil: Broil until the topping is browned and bubbling.
  5. Finish & Serve: Splash cooked oysters with a little anise liqueur and serve immediately.
  6. Yield: 6 portions (6 oysters each)
