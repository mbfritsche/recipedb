Chili Crisp and Honey Roasted Salmon

Ingredients

  • 1 lb salmon
  • 2 tbs chili crisp
  • 2 tbs soy sauce
  • 1 tbs honey
  • 2 bunches broccolini
  • 1 tbs olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic salt
  • 1 tsp lemon pepper
  • 2 C cooked rice quinoa, farro, or any other grain will work too
  • 1 tbs chopped chives for garnish optional

Directions

  1. Preheat: Heat oven to 450°F. Line a large sheet pan with parchment paper or coat with olive oil. Cut salmon into four equal strips.
  2. Mix Sauce: In a small bowl, combine chili crisp and soy sauce.
  3. Coat Salmon: Brush salmon with some of the chili crisp-soy mixture and set aside, using about one-third to one-half of the sauce.
  4. Roast Broccolini: Toss broccolini with olive oil and seasonings. Arrange on the sheet pan, leaving space for salmon. Roast for 10 minutes.
  5. Add Salmon: Remove pan from oven and add salmon. Drizzle salmon with honey. Return to oven and roast for 12 minutes.
  6. Serve: Remove from oven. Serve salmon and broccolini over rice. Drizzle with remaining sauce, extra honey, and chives if desired.

Chef's Notes

  • Quinoa, farro, or any other grain will work too
