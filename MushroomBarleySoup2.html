Mushroom Barley Soup #2 (V)

Ingredients

  • 16 oz baby bella mushrooms, sliced
  • 8 oz white mushrooms, cleaned and chopped
  • ½ C canned crushed tomatoes
  • 1 yellow onion, chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 2 celery stalks, chopped
  • 1 carrot, chopped
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • Kosher salt
  • Black pepper
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • 6 C vegetable broth
  • 1 C pearl barley, rinsed
  • ½ C packed chopped parsley

Directions

  1. Brown Mushrooms: Heat extra-virgin olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high until shimmering. Add baby bella mushrooms and cook ~5 minutes, until softened and lightly colored. Remove and set aside.
  2. Sauté Vegetables: Add a bit more olive oil to the pot. Add onions, garlic, celery, carrots, and chopped white mushrooms. Cook 4-5 minutes over medium-high. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Add Tomatoes & Spices: Stir in crushed tomatoes, coriander, smoked paprika, and cumin. Cook ~3 minutes, stirring frequently.
  4. Simmer Barley: Add broth and pearl barley. Bring to a rolling boil 5 minutes, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer ~45 minutes, until barley is tender.
  5. Finish: Return cooked baby bella mushrooms to the pot and cook ~5 minutes, until heated through.
  6. Serve: Finish with fresh parsley and serve hot.
