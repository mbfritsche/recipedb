Thunder Cake

Ingredients

  • 1 C shortening (or substitute with butter)
  • 1¾ C sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 3 eggs, separated
  • 1 C cold water
  • 80 g puréed tomatoes
  • 2½ C cake flour
  • ½ C cocoa powder
  • 1½ tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt

Directions

  1. Prep Oven & Pans: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two 8½" round cake pans.
  2. Cream Base: Cream shortening and sugar until light and fluffy. Beat in vanilla and egg yolks.
  3. Add Liquids: Mix in cold water and puréed tomatoes until combined.
  4. Whip Whites: Beat egg whites until stiff peaks form, then gently fold into the batter.
  5. Add Dry: Sift together cake flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt. Gradually fold into the wet mixture until smooth.
  6. Bake: Divide batter evenly between prepared pans and bake 35-40 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
  7. Cool & Frost: Cool cakes completely on wire racks, then frost with chocolate buttercream frosting.
