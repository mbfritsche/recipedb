Scallop Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 4 oz scallops
  • ½ C flour
  • ¼ tsp baking powder
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 C club soda

Directions

  1. Make Purée: Add all ingredients to a food processor and purée until completely smooth.
  2. Fry: Heat oil in a frying pan. Spoon ¼ C of the scallop mixture into the pan and fry like pancakes, 2-3 minutes per side, until golden.
  3. Cool & Serve: Transfer to a wire rack to cool briefly before serving.
