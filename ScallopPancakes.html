Scallop Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 4 oz scallops
  • ½ C flour
  • ¼ tsp baking powder
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 C club soda

Directions

  1. Put all ingredients in the food processor and puree until smooth.
  2. Heat oil in a frying pan and add ¼ C of the scallop mixture and fry them like pancakes, about 2-3 minutes on each side. Cool on a wire rack.
