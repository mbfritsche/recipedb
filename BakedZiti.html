Baked Ziti

Ingredients

  • 1 pkg. (16 oz.) ziti pasta, uncooked
  • 1 jar (24 oz.) prepared pasta sauce
  • 1 container (15 oz.) ricotta cheese
  • 1 pkg. (8 oz.) shredded mozzarella, divided
  • ¼ C grated Parmesan Cheese

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 350° F
  2. Cook pasta as directed on package, omitting salt. Drain pasta, reserving ½ C pasta cooking water
  3. Mix pasta sauce, ricotta and reserved pasta cooking water in large bowl
  4. Add pasta and ½ C mozzarella and mix lightly
  5. Spoon into 13x9" baking dish sprayed with cooking spray, sprinkle with remaining mozzarella and Parmesan
  6. Cover, bake 30 min, uncover and continue baking for another 10 minutes
