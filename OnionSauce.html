Onion Sauce

Ingredients

  • 56 g (~½ stick) unsalted butter
  • 2 large yellow onions, peeled, cut in half and thinly sliced
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • ½ C red wine
  • 2 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 1 large sprig fresh sage
  • 2 C strong beef broth
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp prepared yellow mustard
  • ¼ tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tbs dark balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tbs cornstarch dissolved in 1 tablespoon water
  • 2 tbs cold unsalted butter

Directions

  1. Caramelize Onions: Melt butter in a saucepan over medium-high heat. Add sliced onions and sugar and cook ~30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until deeply golden and caramelized.
  2. Deglaze: Add red wine and herbs. Bring to a rapid boil 2 minutes, then reduce heat and simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Build Sauce: Add stock, mustard, salt, pepper, and Worcestershire. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer, cover, and cook 20 minutes.
  4. Finish: Remove herb sprigs. Add vinegar, whisk in cornstarch, and simmer 1-2 minutes, whisking continuously, until thickened.
  5. Mount Butter: Whisk in cold butter until melted and smooth. Adjust seasoning with salt, pepper, and additional mustard if desired.
