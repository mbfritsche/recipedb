Avocado Dressing

Ingredients

  • 2 avocados, halved and pitted
  • 230 g mayonnaise
  • 2 serranos, seeded
  • 75 g onion, minced
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 30 ml honey
  • 30 ml Worcestershire sauce
  • 150 ml lemon juice
  • 1 tsp sea salt
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • pinch cayenne pepper

Directions

  1. Mash: In a large bowl, mash avocados with lemon juice.
  2. Blend: Transfer avocado mixture to a blender, add remaining ingredients, and blend until smooth.
  3. Chill: Pour into an airtight container and refrigerate overnight. Makes about 2 cups

Chef's Notes

  • 75 g of onion is about half a medium-sized onion
  • You can also substitute hot honey to add a little zing
