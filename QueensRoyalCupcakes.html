Queen's Royal Cupcakes

Ingredients

Cake
  • 15g vinegar
  • 300 ml milk
  • 50 ml vegetable oil
  • 60g butter (melted and cool)
  • 2 eggs
  • 5 ml vanilla essence
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 75g cocoa powder
  • 300g caster sugar
  • 10g bicarbonate of soda
  • 100g white chocolate chips
Buttercream Frosting
  • 90g high percentage dark chocolate
  • 100g butter
  • 125g icing sugar
  • Preheat oven to 150°C

Directions

Cake
  1. Mix Dry: Combine flour, sugar, cocoa powder, and bicarbonate of soda in a bowl.
  2. Mix Wet: In a separate bowl, whisk eggs with vanilla, melted butter, oil, milk, and vinegar.
  3. Combine: Gradually add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients, mixing until smooth and lump-free. Stir in chocolate chips.
  4. Bake: Spoon the batter evenly into cupcake liners using a metal spoon. Bake 15-18 minutes, until cupcakes are golden, springy to the touch, and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
  5. Cool: Remove from the oven and allow to cool completely before frosting or serving.
Frosting
  1. Make Frosting: Cream sugar and butter together until light and creamy.
  2. Add Chocolate: Beat in the warm, melted chocolate until fully incorporated.
  3. Finish: Pipe the icing using a decorating bag or spread smoothly with an offset spatula.
