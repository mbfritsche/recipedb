White Chocolate Cheesecake

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (16 oz) cream cheese, softened
  • ½ C sugar
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • 2 eggs
  • 4 squares white baking chocolate, chopped and divided; you may substitute ¾ white chocolate chips
  • 1 ready-to-use chocolate-flavor crumb crust

Directions

  1. Mix cream cheese, sugar, and vanilla with electric mixer until well blended.
  2. Add eggs and mix until well blended. Stir in ½ C of the white chocolate.
  3. Pour into crust. Sprinkle with remaining white chocolate.
  4. Bake at 350° for 35 minutes or until center is almost set.
  5. Allow to cool, then refrigerate 3 hours or overnight. Makes 8 servings.
