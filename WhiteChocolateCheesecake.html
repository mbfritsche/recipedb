White Chocolate Cheesecake

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (16 oz) cream cheese, softened
  • ½ C sugar
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • 2 eggs
  • 4 squares white baking chocolate, chopped and divided; you may substitute ¾ white chocolate chips
  • 1 ready-to-use chocolate-flavor crumb crust

Directions

  1. Make Filling: Beat cream cheese, sugar, and vanilla with an electric mixer until smooth.
  2. Add Eggs & Chocolate: Beat in eggs until well blended, then stir in ½ C white chocolate.
  3. Assemble: Pour filling into the prepared crust and sprinkle with remaining white chocolate.
  4. Bake: Bake at 350°F for 35 minutes, until the center is almost set.
  5. Chill: Cool completely, then refrigerate 3 hours or overnight before serving.
