Gumbo Greens

Ingredients

  • 1 lb smoked turkey (tails, wings, or legs)
  • 1 lb smoked hot Louisiana sausage or Andouille, sliced
  • 1 lb large shrimp peeled and deveined
  • 3 lb frozen collard greens
  • 1 C vegetable oil
  • 1 C all-purpose flour
  • 1 large onion chopped
  • 1 bell pepper chopped
  • 3 celery stalks chopped
  • 1 tbs minced garlic
  • 48 ounces chicken broth
  • 2 tbs seasoning salt
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1 tsp crushed red pepper
  • 2 tsp gumbo file powder

Directions

  1. Prepare Turkey Stock: Boil smoked turkey in about 8 cups of water until tender, about 45 minutes. Reserve 4-6 cups of the cooking liquid.
  2. Make Roux: In a large pot, heat vegetable oil over medium heat. Gradually whisk in flour, stirring constantly, until the roux turns a deep brown color, about 15–20 minutes.
  3. Add Aromatics: Stir in onion, bell pepper, and celery. Cook until softened, about 5 minutes. Add garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  4. Build Gumbo: Add turkey and pour in reserved turkey liquid and chicken broth. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  5. Season: Stir in seasoning salt, Cajun seasoning, and crushed red pepper. Reduce heat and simmer for about 1 hour.
  6. Add Sausage: Add sliced smoked sausage and continue simmering.
  7. Add Greens & Shrimp: Stir in frozen collard greens and simmer until tender, 45 minutes to 1 hour. Add shrimp during the last 10 minutes of cooking.
  8. Finish: Stir in gumbo filé powder until well combined and gumbo thickens.

Chef's Notes

  • Serve over rice with French bread or Jalapeño cornbread and plenty of Louisiana hot sauce.
