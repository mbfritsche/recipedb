Thai Pork Loin

Ingredients

  • 12 oz boneless pork loin
  • 2 tsp dark soy sauce
  • 1 tsp fish sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbs lime juice
  • 1 C peanut oil
  • ½ C fresh coriander chopped coarsely
  • 2 scallions, using all, chopped
  • Salt and freshly ground pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Make Marinade: Blend soy sauce, fish sauce, garlic, and lime juice. With the motor running, slowly stream in peanut oil until emulsified.
  2. Marinate Pork: Transfer marinade to a bowl, stir in coriander and scallions, add pork loin, cover, and refrigerate at least 2 hours.
  3. Prep Fire: Prepare a wood or charcoal grill and allow it to burn down to hot embers.
  4. Season Pork: Remove pork from marinade and season generously with salt and pepper.
  5. Grill: Grill pork ~5 minutes per side, basting occasionally with the marinade, until cooked to your preferred doneness.
  6. Serve: Slice and serve warm.
