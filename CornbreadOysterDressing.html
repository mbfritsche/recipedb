Cornbread Oyster Dressing

Ingredients

  • 8 to 10 cups coarsely crumbled cornbread
  • 2 slices white bread, toasted and torn into small pieces
  • 2 large hard-boiled eggs, chopped
  • 2 C chopped celery
  • 1 C chopped onion
  • 1 pint shucked oysters, drained and chopped, or 2 cans (8 ounces each) whole oysters, drained and chopped
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp poultry seasoning
  • 5 to 6 cups turkey or chicken broth

Directions

  1. Mix Base: In a large bowl, combine the first eight ingredients until evenly mixed.
  2. Moisten: Stir in enough broth to make the mixture very wet.
  3. Assemble: Pour into a greased 13x9" baking dish or a shallow 3-qt baking dish.
  4. Bake: Bake uncovered @400°F for 45 minutes, or until lightly browned on top.
