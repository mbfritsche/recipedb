Chocolate Pecan Sheet Cake

Ingredients

Cake
  • 2 C white sugar
  • 2 C all-purpose flour
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 C water
  • ½ C vegetable shortening
  • ½ C margarine
  • 3 tbs unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ C buttermilk
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
Icing
  • 16 oz confectioners' sugar
  • 3 tbs unsweetened cocoa powder
  • ½ C butter
  • 6 tbs milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 C chopped pecans

Directions

  2. Preheat: Heat oven to 350°F and grease a 9x13" pan.
  3. Mix Dry: In a bowl, combine sugar, flour, and salt.
  4. Make Cocoa Mixture: In a saucepan, combine water, shortening, margarine, and cocoa. Bring to a boil while stirring.
  5. Combine: Pour hot cocoa mixture into the dry ingredients and mix well.
  6. Add Wet Ingredients: Dissolve baking soda in buttermilk. Add eggs and vanilla, then mix into the batter until smooth.
  7. Bake: Pour batter into the prepared pan and bake until set, about 25-30 minutes.
  8. Make Icing: In the saucepan, combine sugar, cocoa, butter, milk, vanilla, and pecans. Heat until warm, stirring frequently.
  9. Finish: Pour icing over the hot cake immediately after baking.
  10. Cool & Serve: Let cool completely before slicing and serving.
