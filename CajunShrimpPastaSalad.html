Cajun Shrimp Pasta Salad

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb. shrimp, peeled
  • ¼ C frozen peas
  • 1 stalk celery, finely chopped
  • 1 12-oz package medium shell pasta
  • 5 tbs mayonnaise or salad dressing
  • 80 ml olive oil
  • ¼ C balsamic vinegar
  • 1 C Zatarain's Shrimp, Crab, and Crawfish boil
  • Louisiana hot sauce to taste
  • 2 green onions, chopped (divided use, optional)

Directions

  1. Cook Shrimp: Peel shrimp. Bring 3 qt water and crawfish boil seasoning to a rolling boil (use less seasoning if preferred). Add shrimp, cover, remove from heat, and steep 10 minutes.
  2. Add Peas: Uncover, add frozen peas, cover again, steep 10 minutes, then drain.
  3. Cook Pasta: Boil pasta according to package directions; drain and cool.
  4. Make Dressing: Emulsify olive oil and balsamic vinegar, then fold into the mayonnaise. Add Louisiana hot sauce to taste.
  5. Combine: Mix shrimp, peas, pasta, celery, onions (if using), and dressing until well coated.
  6. Chill & Serve: Refrigerate until thoroughly chilled. Garnish with chopped parsley or green onions.

Chef's Notes

  • For a lighter alternative, skip the pasta and serve the shrimp salad in an avocado split in half
