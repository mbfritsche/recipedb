Garlic Aioli

Ingredients

  • ½ C olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, peeled
  • 1 large egg yolk, at room temperature
  • ½ tsp freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • ¼ tsp kosher salt

Directions

  1. Infuse Oil: Warm olive oil and garlic over medium‑low until garlic is tender but not browned (12–14 minutes). Remove garlic and cool oil to room temperature.
  2. Start Aioli: Whisk egg yolk, lemon juice, and salt in a bowl set on a non-slip towel.
  3. Emulsify: While whisking constantly, add the garlic oil one drop at a time. Once thickened and pale, continue with a very slow drizzle until creamy.
  4. Boost Garlic (Optional): Mash one reserved garlic clove and stir into the aioli.
  5. Serve: Use immediately or refrigerate until needed.
