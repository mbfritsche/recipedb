Cream of Mushroom Soup #2 (v)

Ingredients

  • 2½ pounds button mushrooms, washed and chopped
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed and minced
  • 8 oz unsalted butter (2 sticks)
  • 1 C all-purpose flour
  • 3 qt beef broth or vegetable stock
  • 1 dry bay leaf
  • 1 sprig fresh thyme
  • ¼ C dry sherry (optional)
  • 2 C heavy cream
  • ½ tsp ground nutmeg
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Sauté Base: In a heavy-bottomed 6-8‑quart stock or soup pot over medium-low heat, melt butter. Add onion, garlic, bay leaf, and thyme; cook until onions are translucent.
  2. Cook Mushrooms: Add chopped mushrooms and cook until most of their liquid releases. Add sherry (if using) and reduce by half.
  3. Build Roux: Sprinkle in flour and stir well to prevent lumps.
  4. Add Stock: Slowly whisk in broth or stock. Bring to a simmer over medium-high heat, stirring constantly to avoid scorching.
  5. Simmer: Once boiling, reduce heat to low and simmer uncovered 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Finish: Stir in heavy cream and nutmeg; season with salt and pepper to taste.
  7. Blend: Remove bay leaf and thyme. Purée with a blender or immersion blender until smooth.

Chef's Notes

  • Food processor is best for chopping the mushrooms
  • Recipe may be halved. Freezes well for larger batches.
