Chicken and Dumplings

Ingredients

Chicken Soup
  • 4½ C chicken stock
  • 3 C water
  • 1 C roux
  • 5½ lb hen, cut into 12 pieces
  • 2 medium onions, chopped
  • 2 medium green bell pepper
  • 1 stalk celery
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1 bunch green onions
  • 1 bunch parsley
Dumplings
  • 2 C flour
  • 2 tbs shortening
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp Cajun seasoning

Directions

  1. Start Soup: Bring broth and water to a boil in a large pot. Whisk in roux until fully dissolved.
  2. Simmer: Add hen, onions, bell pepper, celery, bay leaves, and Cajun seasoning. Simmer partially covered 2½ hours.
  3. Finish Soup: Remove bay leaves; stir in green onions and parsley.
  4. Make Dumplings: Mix flour, shortening, baking powder, and Cajun seasoning.
  5. Cook Dumplings: Drop dumpling dough by tablespoonfuls into gently boiling soup. Cook 10 minutes uncovered, then 20 minutes covered.
  6. Serve: Serve hot.
