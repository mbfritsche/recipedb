Avocado Deviled Eggs

Ingredients

  • 12 eggs
  • 2 medium avocados, chopped
  • 1 medium tomato, chopped
  • 2 tbs red onion, finely chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tbs cilantro, finely chopped
  • 1 tbs fresh lime juice
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • Sprinkle of paprika
  • ½ jalapeño pepper, minced (optional)

Directions

  1. Boil Eggs: Place eggs in a pot, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Turn off heat, cover, and let sit until cooked.
  2. Cool & Peel: Transfer eggs to ice water to cool, then peel.
  3. Prep Filling: Slice eggs lengthwise and remove yolks to a bowl. Add avocados and mash until smooth.
  4. Mix: Stir in remaining ingredients until well combined.
  5. Fill: Spoon mixture into egg white halves.
  6. Serve: Sprinkle with paprika, top with jalapeño if desired, and serve.
