Biggie Shrimp

Ingredients

  • 1 lb peeled raw shrimp
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, chopped
  • 1 C milk
  • 1 C Half-and-half
  • 3 tbs butter
  • 3 tbs flour
  • 1 egg yolk, beaten
  • 3 tbs olive oil
  • 2 tbs soy sauce
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp white pepper
  • ¼ tsp Accent
  • dash sugar
  • 1 tsp rice wine
  • cooked pasta of choice

Directions

  1. Marinate Shrimp: Peel and devein shrimp. Marinate with soy sauce, white pepper, salt, sugar, and Accent for 30 minutes.
  2. Sauté Shrimp: Heat olive oil and 1 tbsp butter over medium-high until foaming. Sauté onion and garlic until limp, add shrimp, and cook until pink. Add rice wine halfway through. Remove shrimp and set aside.
  3. Make Roux: In the same pan, melt remaining 2 tbs butter over medium-low. Gradually stir in flour until smooth.
  4. Build Sauce: Slowly add milk and half-and-half, stirring constantly. Simmer 10 minutes until thickened.
  5. Finish: Return shrimp to the sauce and warm through. Stir in beaten egg yolk gently but do not boil.
  6. Season: Remove from heat and season with salt, black pepper, Louisiana hot sauce, basil, and parsley. Thin with chicken stock if needed.
  7. Serve: Serve over cooked pasta with plenty of garlic bread.
