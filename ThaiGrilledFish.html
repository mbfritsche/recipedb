Thai Grilled Fish

Ingredients

  • 4-5 lb. sea bass
  • 6 large cloves of garlic, lightly crushed
  • 3 long stalks of lemon grass, julienned
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp MSG

Directions

  1. Prep Fish: Clean, scale, and gut the sea bass, leaving the head and fins intact. Score both sides and place the fish on a sheet of aluminum foil.
  2. Season: Season both sides and the body cavity with salt, pepper, and MSG.
  3. Aromatics: Layer lemongrass and garlic over the fish and inside the cavity.
  4. Seal: Wrap the fish tightly in foil, adding a second layer if needed to seal completely.
  5. Grill: Place the wrapped fish on a charcoal grill over indirect heat (never directly over coals). Grill ~15 minutes, or until the fish is cooked through.
  6. Serve: Present the fish in the foil to retain juices and serve with Thai seafood sauce (nam chim).
