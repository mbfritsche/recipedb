Shrimp Creole

Ingredients

  • 4 lb shrimp
  • ¾ C bacon grease
  • 3 stalks celery, chopped
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 8 oz tomato sauce
  • 2 dashes tabasco sauce
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1½ tsp white pepper
  • ½ tsp thyme
  • ½ tsp basil
  • ½ C parsley
  • 3 large peeled tomatoes (or one 14.5 oz can of tomatoes)
  • 1 bunch green onions, chopped
  • 2 tbs sugar
  • 2½ C shrimp stock
  • Salt, black pepper and cayenne to taste

Directions

  1. Sauté Base: Cook onions, garlic, bell pepper, and celery in bacon grease 5 minutes, until softened.
  2. Simmer Sauce: Add tomato sauce and simmer 30 minutes.
  3. Add Stock: Stir in stock and simmer 10 minutes more.
  4. Build Flavor: Add tomatoes, green onions, bay leaf, sugar, Tabasco, lemon juice, and seasonings. Simmer 1 hour.
  5. Cook Shrimp: Add shrimp and simmer 5-7 minutes, just until cooked through.
  6. Serve: Serve hot over rice, garnished with additional parsley.

Chef's Notes

  • One 14½‑oz can peeled stewed tomatoes (with or without chiles) may replace fresh tomatoes.
