Butter Chicken

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs, cubed
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • ½ tsp ground turmeric
  • 6 tbs butter, divided into 2 tbsp chunks
  • 1½ C yellow onion, diced
  • 3 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tbs grated ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3" stick of cinnamon
  • 14 oz tomato sauce
  • 1 C of water
  • 1 C of heavy cream
  • Salt and pepper
  • Chopped cilantro for garnish

Directions

  1. Marinate Chicken: Toss chicken with chili powder, turmeric, salt, and pepper. Let marinate 15 minutes.
  2. Brown Chicken: Brown chicken in 2 tbs butter; remove and set aside.
  3. Build Sauce: Melt remaining 2 tbs butter over medium heat. Add onions, garlic, garam masala, ginger, chili powder, cumin, cayenne, cinnamon, salt, and pepper. Cook until fragrant.
  4. Simmer: Stir in tomato sauce and bring to a simmer. Add water and cream, return to a simmer, then add chicken. Cover and cook 10-15 minutes.
  5. Finish: Stir in final 2 tbs butter and adjust seasoning.
  6. Serve: Garnish with cilantro and serve over rice.
