Oyster Jambalaya

Ingredients

  • 1 oz tasso
  • 1 oz ham, diced
  • 2 oz andouille
  • 3 oz bacon fat
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 3 stalks celery, chopped
  • 2 bell pepper, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 1 bunch green onions, chopped
  • 1 bunch parsely, chopped
  • 4 dz raw oysters
  • 2 tsp thyme
  • 2 C parboiled rice
  • 1 C oyster liquid
  • 2 C beef stock
  • Salt, pepper, and hot sauce to taste
  • 2 fresh chili peppers, chopped (OPTIONAL)

Directions

  1. Render Meats: In a black iron pot, render tasso, ham, and sausage in bacon fat until lightly browned.
  2. Sauté Trinity: Add onions, celery, bell pepper, garlic, and chili peppers (if using). Sauté until vegetables are translucent.
  3. Add Rice & Liquid: Stir in rice, seasonings, oyster liquid, and stock. Bring to a boil.
  4. Simmer: Reduce heat to lowest setting, add green onions, parsley, and oysters. Cover and simmer 25-30 minutes, until rice is tender.
  5. Stir Rule: Stir only once, about halfway through cooking, to avoid gummy rice.
  6. Serve: Fluff gently and serve hot.
  7. Yield: About 6½ cups
