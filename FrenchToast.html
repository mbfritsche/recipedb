French Toast

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbs half-and-half
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 4 pieces white bread
  • 2 tbs butter

Directions

  1. Make Custard: Whisk eggs, half‑and‑half (or cream), and cinnamon until well combined.
  2. Soak Bread: Add bread pieces and coat thoroughly on both sides. Let soak longer if bread is stale.
  3. Cook: Heat butter in a sauté pan over medium‑high until bubbly. Cook bread about 3 minutes per side until browned.
  4. Serve: Remove, slice diagonally, and serve with desired toppings, such as cinnamon sugar, confectioners' sugar, maple syrup, whipped cream, marmalade, or fresh fruit.
