Cajun Lentil Stew

Ingredients

  • 1 16-oz bag of brown lentils, rinsed under cold water
  • ½ lb. hot smoked sausage
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1½ lb spareribs
  • 1 green pepper, diced
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 2 stalks celery, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 jalapeno pepper, seeded and diced
  • 2 green onions, minced
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 10 C water
  • 1 14-oz can Rotel tomatoes
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp thyme leaves
  • 1 tsp salt

Directions

  1. In a large kettle, heat the oil and sweat the vegetables and the smoked sausage, about 10-12 minutes.
  2. Add the lentils, ribs, seasonings, water, and tomatoes. Substitute regular tomatoes in this step if you want a less spicy dish. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to a simmer and cover.
  3. Cook for 1½ hours or until the lentils are soft. You may need to add more water.
  4. Taste for seasoning and serve hot over rice with hot French bread
