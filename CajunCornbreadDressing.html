Cajun Cornbread Dressing

Ingredients

  • 3 packages Jiffy Corn Muffin mix
  • 1 yellow onion, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 stalks celery, chopped
  • 2 jalapenos, seeded and minced
  • 2 lbs shrimp, cut in half
  • 1 lb crawfish
  • ½ lb crab meat
  • 1 lb bacon, cubed
  • 2 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ¼ C butter, melted
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 4 eggs
  • 1-2 qt oysters, shucked
  • 1½ qt chicken stock
  • 1 lb gizzards
  • 1 lb liver

Directions

  1. Prep Cornbread & Stock: Prepare cornbread per package directions. Simmer giblets in 1 quart water to make stock.
  2. Poach Oysters: Remove giblets. Poach oysters in the hot stock just until done; reserve oysters and stock.
  3. Cook Bacon & Veg: Brown bacon lardons; remove. Sauté onion, celery, bell pepper, garlic, and jalapeño in bacon drippings until translucent.
  4. Add Seafood: Gently warm crawfish and crabmeat in the vegetables - do not overcook.
  5. Combine: Crumble cornbread. Mix with bacon, vegetables, seasonings, oysters, crab, and crawfish.
  6. Moisten & Bind: Gradually add reserved stock until damp but not wet. Add eggs one at a time to bind (use only as many as needed).
  7. Bake: Transfer to a buttered casserole and bake at 400éF for 30 minutes, until golden brown.
