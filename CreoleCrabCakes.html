Creole Crab Cakes

Ingredients

  • 8 oz crab meat (lump white or claw meat)
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • ½ tsp dry mustard
  • 1 tbs mayonnaise
  • ½ tsp lemon juice
  • 1½ tsp prepared mustard
  • 1½ tsp melted butter
  • ½ tsp parsley
  • ¼ C bread crumbs
  • ½ tsp creole seasoning, or to taste
  • vegetable or olive oil for pan frying

Directions

  1. Mix Base: Gently combine crabmeat with seasonings, Worcestershire sauce, mayonnaise, mustard, and egg until just mixed, taking care not to break up large crab pieces.
  2. Bind: Add breadcrumbs a little at a time until the mixture just holds together; you may not need all of them.
  3. Form & Cook: Shape into crab cakes and pan-fry in vegetable or olive oil until golden brown on both sides.
  4. Serve: Serve immediately while hot.
