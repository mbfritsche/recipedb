Chicken Teriyaki Jambalaya

Ingredients

  • ½ lb chicken thighs, cut into medium chunks
  • ¼ lb ground pork or ground beef
  • 3 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp MSG
  • 3 tbs teriyaki sauce
  • 2 tbs hot sauce
  • 1 large yellow onion, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 stalk celery, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 bunch green onions, chopped
  • 2 tbs parsley
  • 1 tbs Better than Bouillon vegetable base
  • 1 head broccoli
  • 2 C Uncle Ben's converted rice
  • 3 C water

Directions

  1. Prep Broccoli: Steam broccoli florets 3 minutes in 3 C water with vegetable base; reserve broccoli and broth.
  2. Marinate Meats:
    • Toss chicken with salt, black pepper, MSG, teriyaki sauce, and hot sauce; marinate 30 minutes.
    • Season ground pork with remaining seasonings; marinate 30 minutes.
  3. Brown Meats: Heat 2 tbs oil in a very hot cast-iron skillet. Sear chicken until browned; remove. Brown ground pork; drain and combine with chicken.
  4. Cook Veggies: Add onion, celery, bell pepper, garlic, and green onions to the skillet; sauté until browned and deglazed.
  5. Combine: Return meats to the skillet. Stir in rice and parsley to coat.
  6. Simmer: Add chicken stock, bring to a boil, then cover and cook on lowest heat 15 minutes.
  7. Finish: Add steamed broccoli

Chef's Notes

  • The ground pork or beef is optional and can be omitted if you don’t have it on hand.
