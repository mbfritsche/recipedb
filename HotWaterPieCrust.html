Hot Water Pie Crust

Ingredients

  • 544 g high-protein all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 180 g lard or beef suet
  • 180 ml water

Directions

  1. In a large mixing bowl, whisk flour and salt to combine.
  2. In a small saucepan, combine lard or suet and water and bring to a simmer over medium-high heat
  3. When fat has melted completely, immediately pour it over dry ingredients. Using a flexible spatula, stir mixture until it forms a shaggy dough.
  4. When dough is cool enough to handle, use your hands to shape it into a mass. Flip dough onto a lightly-floured surface and knead until it becomes a smooth dough, about 1 minute
  5. Use dough immediately in any recipe calling for a hot water crust. To roll dough, place it between two 13-by-18-inch pieces of parchment and roll to desired thickness.

Chef's Notes

  • King Arthur or Heckers have a high-protein AP flour offering
  • If using table salt, use half the volume
  • This is the best crust for savory meat pies, like those found in Great Britain
