Lemon Cake

Ingredients

Cake
  • 1 C butter, softened
  • 2 C sugar
  • 4 large eggs, room temperature
  • 2 tsp lemon extract
  • 1¾ C all-purpose flour
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp grated lemon zest
Frosting
  • 4 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 2 tbs butter, softened
  • 2 C confectioners' sugar
  • 2 tsp lemon juice
  • 1½ tsp grated lemon zest

Directions

Cake
  1. Cream Base: In a large bowl, cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy (~5 minutes). Beat in eggs and lemon extract.
  2. Add Dry: Combine flour and salt, then gradually mix into the creamed mixture until smooth. Stir in lemon zest.
  3. Bake: Spread batter into a greased 13x9" baking pan. Bake at 350°F for 18-22 minutes, until the center is set and edges are lightly golden.
  4. Cool: Allow to cool completely before serving or glazing.
Frosting
  1. Make Frosting: In a large bowl, beat cream cheese and butter until light and fluffy.
  2. Sweeten & Flavor: Beat in confectioners' sugar, lemon juice, and lemon zest until smooth and spreadable.
  3. Frost & Store: Spread frosting over cooled bars and refrigerate until serving.
