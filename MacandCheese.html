Macaroni and Cheese

Ingredients

  • 2 C milk
  • 2 tbs butter (1 oz)
  • 2 tbs all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 12 - 16 oz shredded cheese (Cheddar, Monterrey Jack, Mozzarella, Gouda, Gruyere, Swiss, etc.)
  • ¼ tsp paprika (optional)
  • 16 oz elbow macaroni, cooked
  • 3 tbs panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbs shredded Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tbs olive oil
  • Garlic powder, to taste

Directions

  1. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Make Topping: Combine panko and Parmesan, moisten with olive oil, and mix until evenly coated; set aside.
  3. Warm Milk: Microwave milk on HIGH for 1½ minutes.
  4. Make Roux: Melt butter in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium-low. Whisk in flour until smooth and cook about 1 minute, whisking constantly.
  5. Build Sauce: Gradually whisk in warm milk and cook, whisking constantly, ~5 minutes until thickened.
  6. Add Cheese & Pasta: Whisk in salt, black pepper, paprika, and 1 C shredded cheese. Stir in pasta until well combined.
  7. Assemble: Spoon mixture into a lightly greased 2-qt baking dish. Sprinkle remaining cheese over the top and finish with the panko mixture.
  8. Bake: Bake 20 minutes, until golden and bubbly.

Chef's Notes

  • This recipe works well as a base for crabmeat, crawfish, shrimp, bacon, smoked sausage, hot dogs, or bratwurst.
  • Try one or more of the following to enhance depth and balance:
    • 2 tsp dry mustard
    • ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
    • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
    • 1 tbs sour cream
    • Evaporated milk
