Artichoke Ratatouille Chicken Casserole

Ingredients

  • 3 Japanese eggplants (about 1 pound)
  • 4 plum tomatoes
  • 1 medium sweet yellow pepper
  • 1 medium sweet red pepper
  • 1 medium onion
  • 1 can (14 ounces) water-packed artichoke hearts, drained and quartered
  • 2 tbs minced fresh thyme
  • 2 tbs capers, drained
  • 2 tbs olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon Creole seasoning, divided
  • 1½ pounds boneless skinless chicken breasts, cubed
  • 1 C white wine or chicken broth
  • ¼ C grated Asiago cheese
  • Hot cooked pasta, optional

Directions

  1. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Prep Veggies: Cut eggplant, tomatoes, peppers, and onion into ¾" pieces. Place in a large bowl.
  3. Season Veggies: Stir in artichoke hearts, thyme, capers, oil, garlic, and ½ tsp Creole seasoning.
  4. Prep Chicken: Season chicken with remaining Creole seasoning and place in a 13x9" baking dish coated with cooking spray.
  5. Assemble: Spoon vegetable mixture over the chicken and drizzle with wine.
  6. Bake Covered: Cover and bake 30 minutes.
  7. Finish Baking: Uncover and continue baking 30-45 minutes, until chicken is cooked through and vegetables are tender.
  8. Serve: Sprinkle with cheese and serve with rice, pasta, or garlic mashed potatoes.
