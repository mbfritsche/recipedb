Shredded Tofu Tacos

Ingredients

  • 1 16-oz package super firm tofu
  • 2 tbs avocado oil, divided
  • 4 tbs soy sauce, divided
  • 5 tbs tomato paste, divided
  • 3 tsp cumin, divided
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ onion diced
  • 4 minced garlic cloves
  • ½ C pineapple juice
  • 6 soft or hard taco shells, warmed
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • ½ C shredded lettuce
  • ¼ C sliced black olives
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • Additional toppings
    • Shredded cheese
    • Red onion
    • Cilantro
    • Salsa

Directions

  1. Prep Oven: Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Prep Tofu: Grate tofu into a large bowl using a hand grater.
  3. Season Tofu: Mix 1 tbs oil, 2 tbs soy sauce, 2 tbs tomato paste, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, garlic granules, and salt.
  4. Bake Tofu: Spread mixture on a baking sheet and bake 30 minutes, stirring every 10 minutes.
  5. Cook Aromatics: Heat remaining oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Add onion and garlic; cook 5 minutes.
  6. Build Sauce: Add remaining tomato paste, soy sauce, pineapple juice, cumin, and smoked paprika. Simmer on low 10 minutes, stirring often.
  7. Combine: Add baked tofu to the pan and stir until well coated.
  8. Serve: Fill taco shells with the tofu mixture and desired toppings (e.g., tomato, lettuce, olives, avocado).

Chef's Notes

  • As written, this is a vegetarian recipe - replacing the shredded cheese with a plant-based alternative will make it vegan
